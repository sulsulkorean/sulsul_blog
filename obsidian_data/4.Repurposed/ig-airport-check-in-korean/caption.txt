Airport check-in in Korea hits different when someone is waiting behind you.

Don’t freeze at the counter — practise these lines out loud before you fly:
• 체크인 하러 왔어요 — I’m here to check in
• 창가 자리 주세요 — Window seat, please

Save this for your next trip and say them out loud.
