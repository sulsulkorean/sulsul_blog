## Slide 1 — Title
Master Korean at the Convenience Store
Four lines for a smooth checkout.

## Slide 2 — Point and order
**이거 주세요** — *igeo juseyo*
"This one, please."
When: pointing at a snack or drink

## Slide 3 — Price
**얼마예요?** — *eolmayeyo?*
"How much is it?"
When: before you pay

## Slide 4 — Pay
**카드로 결제할게요** — *kadeuro gyeoljehalgeyo*
"I'll pay by card."
When: at checkout

## Slide 5 — Bag
A: **봉투 주세요** — *bongtu juseyo*
"A bag, please."

B: **봉투 필요 없어요** — *bongtu piryo eopseoyo*
"I don't need a bag."

When: they ask about a bag
