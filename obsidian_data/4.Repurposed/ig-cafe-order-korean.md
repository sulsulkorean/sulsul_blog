## Slide 1 — Title
Order Like a Local at Korean Cafes  
Master these simple phrases and impress!

## Slide 2 — Teaching
**커피 주세요** — *keopi juseyo*  
"Coffee, please"  
When: Ordering your drink

## Slide 3 — Teaching
**얼음 있어요?** — *eoreum isseoyo?*  
"Do you have ice?"  
When: Checking if ice is available

## Slide 4 — Teaching
**따뜻한 거 있어요?** — *ttatteuthan geo isseoyo?*  
"Do you have anything warm?"  
When: Asking for a warm drink option

## Slide 5 — Teaching
**테이크아웃 해요** — *teikeuaut haeyo*  
"I'll take it to go"  
When: Requesting a takeaway
