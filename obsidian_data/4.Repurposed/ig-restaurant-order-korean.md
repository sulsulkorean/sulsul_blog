## Slide 1 — Title
**Order with Confidence in Korean Restaurants**
Make your dining experience smoother with these key phrases.

## Slide 2 — 메뉴 추천해 주세요
**메뉴 추천해 주세요** — *menyu chucheonhae juseyo*
"Please recommend a dish."
When: You're unsure what to order.

## Slide 3 — 매운 거 있어요?
**매운 거 있어요?** — *maeun geo isseoyo?*
"Do you have something spicy?"
When: You're craving spicy food.

## Slide 4 — 물 더 주세요
**물 더 주세요** — *mul deo juseyo*
"Please give me more water."
When: Your glass is empty.

## Slide 5 — 계산서 주세요
**계산서 주세요** — *gyesanseo juseyo*
"Please give me the bill."
When: You're ready to pay.
