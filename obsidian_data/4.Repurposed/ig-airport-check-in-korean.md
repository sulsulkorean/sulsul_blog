## Slide 1 — Title
**Nail Your Korean at Airport Check-in**
Get through the counter without freezing.

## Slide 2 — Check-in
**체크인 하러 왔어요** — *chekeuin hareo wasseoyo*
"I'm here to check in."
When: walking up to the airline desk

## Slide 3 — Bags
**짐 하나 있어요** — *jim hana isseoyo*
"I have one bag."
When: answering about your bags

## Slide 4 — Seat
A: **창가 자리 주세요** — *changga jari juseyo*
"Window seat, please."

B: **복도 자리 주세요** — *bokdo jari juseyo*
"Aisle seat, please."

When: choosing your seat

## Slide 5 — Gate
**게이트 어디예요?** — *geiteu eodiyeyo?*
"Where is the gate?"
When: after you get your boarding pass
