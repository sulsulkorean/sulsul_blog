Korean restaurants get loud and fast — pointing at the menu only gets you so far.

Order with confidence using lines you’ll actually need:
• 메뉴 추천해 주세요 — Can you recommend something?
• 계산서 주세요 — The check, please

Save this for your next meal and practise out loud.
