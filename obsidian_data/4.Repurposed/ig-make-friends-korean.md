## Slide 1 — Title
**Make Friends Easily in Korean**
Start conversations with these friendly phrases!

## Slide 2 — 이름이 뭐예요?
**이름이 뭐예요?** — *ireumi mwoyeyo?*  
"What's your name?"  
When: Meeting someone new

## Slide 3 — 어디에서 왔어요?
**어디에서 왔어요?** — *eodieseo wasseoyo?*  
"Where are you from?"  
When: Curious about someone's background

## Slide 4 — 한국어 잘해요
**한국어 잘해요** — *hangugeo jalhaeyo*  
"You're good at Korean"  
When: Complimenting their language skills

## Slide 5 — 같이 놀래요?
**같이 놀래요?** — *gati nolraeyo?*  
"Do you want to hang out?"  
When: Inviting someone to join you
