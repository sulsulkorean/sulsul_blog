Soompi confirmed that Song Kang and Lee Jun-young lead tvN's "Four Hands, Two Sonatas," premiering August 29, 2026, and Korean drama fans filled threads immediately. **오늘 첫 방이에요** (*oneul cheot bangieoyo*), "Today is the first episode," is what fans post when the premiere date finally arrives, and **벌써 기대돼요** (*beolsseo gidaedwaeyo*), "I'm already excited," has been running in comment sections since the first trailer dropped. Neither phrase belongs to this drama alone — they are what Korean fans reach for every time a new drama starts.

> - Mark the premiere: **오늘 첫 방이에요** (*oneul cheot bangieoyo*), "Today is the first episode"
> - Show anticipation: **벌써 기대돼요** (*beolsseo gidaedwaeyo*), "I'm already excited"
> - React after watching: **드라마 시작했어요!** (*deurama sijakhaesseoyo!*), "The drama has started!"
> - Invite someone: **같이 봐요** (*gati bwayo*), "Let's watch together"
> - Ask for a reaction: **내용이 어때요?** (*naeyongi eottaeyo?*), "How is the story?"

## What do Korean fans say on premiere day?

Premiere week has its own language in Korean fan culture. Four phrases carry most of the energy from the announcement to the first episode credits:

**오늘 첫 방이에요.** — *oneul cheot bangieoyo.* — "Today is the first episode."

**드라마 시작했어요!** — *deurama sijakhaesseoyo!* — "The drama has started!"

**빨리 보고 싶어요.** — *ppalli bogo sipeoyo.* — "I can't wait to watch it."

**벌써 기대돼요.** — *beolsseo gidaedwaeyo.* — "I'm already excited."

The last two run in every fandom the week a new drama premieres. Swap in the show name or drop them into any fan thread as they are.

| Premiere moment | Korean phrase |
|---|---|
| Premiere day arrives | 오늘 첫 방이에요 (*oneul cheot bangieoyo*) |
| Building anticipation | 벌써 기대돼요 (*beolsseo gidaedwaeyo*) |
| After episode one | 내용이 너무 좋아요 (*naeyongi neomu joayo*) |
| Invite to watch together | 같이 봐요 (*gati bwayo*) |

## How do you react to the cast and story in Korean?

When the first episode airs, reactions come fast. If the lead actor makes an impression — and Song Kang has a consistent track record with drama fans — the first comment in any Korean thread is usually:

**주인공 너무 잘생겼어요.** — *juingong neomu jalsaengyeosseoyo.* — "The main character is so handsome."

For the story itself, one question works in every fan thread:

**내용이 어때요?** — *naeyongi eottaeyo?* — "How is the story?"

And if someone asks whether you have watched yet:

You: **오늘 첫 방 봤어요?** — *oneul cheot bang bwasseoyo?* — "Did you watch the first episode today?"  
Them: **아직요, 스포 말하지 마세요!** — *ajikyo, seupeo malhaji maseyo!* — "Not yet, please don't spoil it!"  
You: **알겠어요, 빨리 봐요.** — *algessoyo, ppalli bwayo.* — "OK, watch it fast."

## How do you invite someone to watch a K-drama in Korean?

The watch-party line is short:

**같이 봐요.** — *gati bwayo.* — "Let's watch together."

Add a time to make it a plan:

**오늘 밤에 같이 보실래요?** — *oneul bame gati bosilraeyo?* — "Would you like to watch together tonight?"

If you are coordinating across time zones, say:

**같이 실시간으로 봐요.** — *gati silsigan-euro bwayo.* — "Let's watch it in real time together."

Korean fans coordinate watch parties in these short lines — no extra grammar required.
