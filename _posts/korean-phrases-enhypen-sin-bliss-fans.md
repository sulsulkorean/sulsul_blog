## How do you open a fan reaction in Korean?

ENHYPEN's concept drops — the teaser images, the lore videos, the surprise tracklist reveal — each one lands inside Weverse in Korean first. ENGENEs who read Korean see the details before fan translators catch up. The phrase that opens most reaction threads:

**처음 들었을 때 어땠어?** (*cheoeum deureosseul ttae eottaesseo?*), "What did you think when you heard it for the first time?" — casual speech level, fine for fandom group chats.

If you want to compliment the title track specifically, **타이틀곡 진짜 좋다** (*taiteulgok jinjja jota*), "The title track is honestly great," is concise enough to quote directly in a live comment.

## How do fans talk about lore and theories?

The DARK BLOOD universe gives fans a reason to go deep — timelines, symbols in the jacket photos, lyrics that loop back to earlier EPs. Theory chats in Korean run on a handful of short questions:

You: **이 부분 무슨 뜻인 것 같아?** — *i bubun museun tteuttin geot gata?* — "What do you think this part means?"  
Them: **전에 나온 이야기랑 연결되는 것 같아.** — *jeone naon iyagirang yeongyeoldweneun geot gata.* — "I think it connects to the earlier story."  
You: **오, 나도 그렇게 생각했어!** — *o, nado geureoke saenggakhaesseo!* — "Oh, I thought so too!"

The payoff of knowing these phrases: you can drop a theory in a live comment thread and actual Korean ENGENEs will respond. **나도 그렇게 생각했어** (*nado geureoke saenggakhaesseo*) — "I thought the same thing" — is one of the most satisfying phrases in K-pop fan chat because it tells you your Korean landed.

## What do you say after the album grows on you?

The first listen is chaos. By the third loop, fans have a pick — a deep cut that wasn't the title track but somehow feels more personal. Share yours with:

**숨겨진 곡이 더 좋더라** (*sumgyeojin gogi deo jotdora*), "The hidden track [B-side] ended up being better for me."
