Korean phrases for finding your gate at Incheon Airport come down to one location question and one backup line. Say **탑승구 어디예요?** (*tapsseunggu eodiyeyo?*), "Where is the boarding gate?" when the departure board moves too fast, then name your gate number if the staff asks for it.

> - Ask for your gate: **탑승구 어디예요?** (*tapsseunggu eodiyeyo?*), "Where is the boarding gate?"
> - Name a specific gate: **115번 게이트요** (*ilbaeksip-o beon geiteuyo*), "Gate 115."
> - Find the exit area: **출구 어디예요?** (*chulgu eodiyeyo?*), "Where is the exit?"
> - If the reply is too fast: **다시 말해 주세요** (*dasi malhae juseyo*), "Please say that again."

## How do you ask for your boarding gate?

Incheon Airport signs are bilingual, but the departure board refreshes quickly and gate changes happen. The textbook location pattern puts **어디예요?** (*eodiyeyo?*) right after the place noun — toss the key word and raise your voice at the end.

You: **저기요, 탑승구 어디예요?** — *jeogiyo, tapsseunggu eodiyeyo?* — "Excuse me, where is the boarding gate?"  
Them: **몇 번 게이트세요?** — *myeot beon geiteseyo?* — "Which gate number?"  
You: **115번이요.** — *ilbaeksip-o beoniyo.* — "Gate 115."

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Ask for boarding gate | 탑승구 어디예요? (*tapsseunggu eodiyeyo?*) |
| Name your gate | 115번 게이트요 (*ilbaeksip-o beon geiteuyo*) |
| Ask for the exit | 출구 어디예요? (*chulgu eodiyeyo?*) |
| Find immigration area | 입국 심사 어디예요? (*ipguk simsa eodiyeyo?*) |
| Too fast to follow | 다시 말해 주세요 (*dasi malhae juseyo*) |

## What does staff say when you ask?

Airport staff usually answer with a direction plus a floor number. You only need to catch **왼쪽** (*oenjjok*, left), **오른쪽** (*oreunjjok*, right), or **2층** (*icheung*, second floor).

You: **115번 게이트 어디예요?** — *ilbaeksip-o beon geiteu eodiyeyo?* — "Where is Gate 115?"  
Them: **2층 왼쪽이에요.** — *icheung oenjjog-ieyo.* — "Second floor, to the left."  
You: **감사합니다.** — *gamsahamnida.* — "Thank you."

If you miss the direction, point at your boarding pass and say **여기요** (*yeogiyo*), "this one," while showing the gate number. Most staff switch to English for a single word, but repeating **115번이요?** (*ilbaeksip-o beoniyo?*) as a one-word check works just as well.

## What usually goes wrong before boarding?

Travelers often look for **공항** (*gonghang*, airport) on signs when they actually need **탑승구** (*tapsseunggu*, boarding gate) or **출국장** (*chulgukjang*, departures hall). The airport is the whole building — your gate is one counter inside it.

You: **출국장 어디예요?** — *chulgukjang eodiyeyo?* — "Where is the departures hall?"  
Them: **3층이에요. 엘리베이터 타세요.** — *samcheung-ieyo. ellibeiteo taseyo.* — "Third floor. Take the elevator."  
You: **네, 감사합니다.** — *ne, gamsahamnida.* — "Yes, thank you."

A second mistake is waiting until the final boarding call. Ask early at any information desk — **안내 데스크 어디예요?** (*annae deseukeu eodiyeyo?*) — so you have time if the gate moves.
