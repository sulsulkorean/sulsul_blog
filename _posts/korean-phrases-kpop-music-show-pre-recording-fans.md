A comeback stage is often filmed hours before the show airs, and 사전녹화 (*sajeonnokhwa*), the pre-recording, is the one fans can queue for on the day. **사전녹화 줄 여기 맞아요?** (*sajeonnokhwa jul yeogi majayo?*), "Is this the pre-recording line?" is what keeps you out of the wrong queue in a car park with four of them, and **번호표 받아야 돼요?** (*beonhopyo badaya dwaeyo?*), "Do I need to get a number ticket?" is what decides whether your morning counted.

> - Find the line: **사전녹화 줄 여기 맞아요?** (*sajeonnokhwa jul yeogi majayo?*), "Is this the pre-recording line?"
> - Ask about the ticket: **번호표 받아야 돼요?** (*beonhopyo badaya dwaeyo?*), "Do I need to get a number ticket?"
> - Ask the time: **몇 시에 들어가요?** (*myeot sie deureogayo?*), "What time do we go in?"
> - Check the rules: **응원봉 가지고 들어가도 돼요?** (*eungwonbong gajigo deureogado dwaeyo?*), "Can I bring my lightstick in?"
> - Hold your spot: **잠깐만 자리 좀 봐 주세요** (*jamkkanman jari jom bwa juseyo*), "Please watch my spot for a second."

## How does the queue actually work on the day?

Fan staff run the line, not the broadcaster, and almost everything is decided by a number ticket handed out at a time posted that morning.

1. **사전녹화 줄 여기 맞아요?** to the person at the end of the line, before you settle in behind them.
2. **번호표 받아야 돼요?** early, because standing in line without one can mean standing for nothing.
3. **몇 시에 들어가요?** so you know whether there is time to sit down and eat.
4. **응원봉 가지고 들어가도 돼요?** since the rules change per show and per stage.
5. **잠깐만 자리 좀 봐 주세요** to the fan behind you when you finally go find a bathroom.

## What do Korean speakers say back?

The fan running the list has answered this two hundred times and will answer it in one breath.

You: **사전녹화 줄 여기 맞아요?** — *sajeonnokhwa jul yeogi majayo?* — "Is this the pre-recording line?"  
Them: **네, 맞아요. 번호표는 저기서 나눠 줘요** — *ne, majayo. beonhopyoneun jeogiseo nanwo jwoyo* — "Yes, it is. They're handing out number tickets over there."  
You: **몇 시에 들어가요?** — *myeot sie deureogayo?* — "What time do we go in?"  
Them: **두 시쯤 입장이에요** — *du sijjeum ipjangieyo* — "Entry is around two."

**잠깐만 자리 좀 봐 주세요** is the one you will use most, and it is how people in a long line end up talking to each other. To have the chant ready before the doors open, see [Korean phrases when K-pop fans practice the fanchant together](/korean-phrases-kpop-fanchant-practice-fans).

## What usually goes wrong at a pre-recording?

The biggest one is joining a line without asking what it is. Broadcast stations run several recordings on the same day, and an hour in the wrong queue is an hour gone — **사전녹화 줄 여기 맞아요?** takes three seconds and settles it.

The second is missing the number ticket call while sitting somewhere with coffee. Ask **번호표 받아야 돼요?** and **몇 시에 들어가요?** as soon as you arrive, and leave your place properly with **잠깐만 자리 좀 봐 주세요** instead of just walking off.
