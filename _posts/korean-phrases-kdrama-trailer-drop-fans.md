August 2026 keeps dropping **K-drama trailers** at midnight KST — and the Korean fans type still works even if you watch the clip with English captions. **예고편 봤어요?** (*yegopyeon bwasseoyo?*), "Did you see the trailer?" opens the thread, and **기대돼요** (*gidaedwaeyo*), "I am looking forward to it," is what you send when the thumbnail alone breaks the group chat.

> - Ask if they watched: **예고편 봤어요?** (*yegopyeon bwasseoyo?*), "Did you see the trailer?"
> - Share the hype: **기대돼요** (*gidaedwaeyo*), "I am looking forward to it."
> - React to the preview: **대박이에요** (*daebakieyo*), "That is amazing."
> - Mention the release date: **언제 시작해요?** (*eonje sijakhaeyo?*), "When does it start?"
> - Invite a watch party: **같이 봐요** (*gati bwayo*), "Let us watch together."

## How do you talk about a trailer without spoiling the full plot?

Fan communities mix English show titles with Korean reactions — a few Korean lines signal you are in the room, not just lurking.

1. Drop the link and ask **예고편 봤어요?** before anyone else types.
2. When the cast looks perfect, send **대박이에요** — safe praise, no plot theories.
3. Add **기대돼요** so friends know you are counting down with them.
4. If the premiere date appears on screen, ask **언제 시작해요?** to flag the schedule.
5. Close with **같이 봐요** when you want a voice call on premiere night.

| Moment | Say this |
|---|---|
| Open the chat | 예고편 봤어요? (*yegopyeon bwasseoyo?*) |
| Hype the premiere | 기대돼요 (*gidaedwaeyo*) |
| Quick reaction | 대박이에요 (*daebakieyo*) |
| Ask the date | 언제 시작해요? (*eonje sijakhaeyo?*) |
| Watch together | 같이 봐요 (*gati bwayo*) |

## What do Korean speakers say back?

Replies in trailer threads stay loud and short.

You: **예고편 봤어요?** — *yegopyeon bwasseoyo?* — "Did you see the trailer?"  
Them: **응, 방금 봤어!** — *eung, banggeum bwasseo!* — "Yeah, I just watched it!"  
You: **기대돼요.** — *gidaedwaeyo.*

If someone shares a behind-the-scenes clip, **고마워요** (*gomawoyo*) works with peers; **감사합니다** (*gamsahamnida*) is safer when thanking a stranger in the comments.

## What usually goes wrong during trailer week?

Learners memorize dramatic lines from subtitles but never practice the short question fans send when a preview first lands.

A second mistake is typing long English paragraphs while friends switch to Korean for two seconds — one clear reaction keeps you in the conversation. For spin-off rumor chats, see [Korean phrases when a K-drama spin-off is announced](/korean-phrases-kdrama-spin-off-announcement-fans).
