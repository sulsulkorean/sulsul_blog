Billboard and Weverse lit up when all seven BTS members completed their military service in 2025 and the full group stepped back together. For every ARMY who waited through that time, knowing a handful of real Korean phrases changes what the reunion means — not just watching, but actually speaking to the moment.

> - Express how much you missed them: **보고 싶었어요** (*bogo sippeosseoyo*), "I missed you."
> - Promise your support: **항상 응원할게요** (*hangsang eungwonhalgeyo*), "I will always cheer for you."
> - Tell your bias you love them: **사랑해요** (*saranghaeyo*), "I love you."
> - Ask about the fan meeting: **팬미팅 언제예요?** (*paenmiting eonjeyeyo?*), "When is the fan meeting?"

## What do you say when your bias finally comes back?

The line most ARMY reach for is **보고 싶었어요** (*bogo sippeosseoyo*) — "I missed you." It is past-tense longing, and it lands with weight whether you say it at a fan sign, write it on Weverse, or shout it at a concert. The polite -었어요 ending keeps it warm without overdoing the formality.

You: **정말 보고 싶었어요!** — *jeongmal bogo sippeosseoyo!* — "I really missed you!"  
Them: **저도요!** — *jeodoyeo!* — "Me too!"  
You: **항상 응원할게요.** — *hangsang eungwonhalgeyo.* — "I will always cheer for you."

## How do Korean fans express support at a reunion event?

Korean fandoms have a specific phrase culture. At a concert or fan sign, **최고예요!** (*choegoeyeo!*), "You are the best!" rings out constantly. A more personal line is **항상 응원할게요** — the **-ㄹ게요** ending makes it a promise, not just a feeling.

If you want to ask for a photo: **같이 사진 찍어도 돼요?** (*gachi sajin jjigeodo dwaeyo?*), "Can we take a photo together?" The staff at official events will usually redirect you, but the phrase is the right one for informal fan gatherings before and after venues.

## What do you say when you meet another BTS fan in Korean?

When you spot another ARMY — at a listening cafe, outside a venue, or on the subway — the opener is simple. **방탄소년단 좋아해요?** (*Bangtan Sonyeondan joahaeyo?*), "Do you like BTS?" is a soft question that opens a full conversation. From there you can ask about their bias with **최애가 누구예요?** (*choe-ae-ga nugueyeo?*), "Who is your bias?"

The phrases above carry into any K-pop group reunion, not just BTS. They are evergreen: every group takes a break, every ARMY or fan club counts the days, and **보고 싶었어요** is always the first thing out.
