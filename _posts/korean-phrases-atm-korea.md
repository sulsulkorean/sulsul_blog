Korean ATMs are inside every subway station, GS25, CU, and bank branch in Seoul. The machine is easy to find. The buttons are not. **현금 인출기 어디 있어요?** (*hyeongeum inchulgi eodi isseoyo?*), "Where is the ATM?", gets you pointed in the right direction, and **외국 카드 되요?** (*oeguk kadeu dwaeyo?*), "Do foreign cards work here?", tells you whether to keep walking before you even insert your card.

> - Find the machine: **현금 인출기 어디 있어요?** (*hyeongeum inchulgi eodi isseoyo?*), "Where is the ATM?"
> - Check foreign card: **외국 카드 되요?** (*oeguk kadeu dwaeyo?*), "Do foreign cards work here?"
> - Select withdrawal: **현금 인출** (*hyeongeum inchul*), the on-screen button that starts a cash transaction
> - Check balance: **잔액 조회** (*janaek johoe*), balance inquiry
> - Report an error: **오류가 났어요** (*oryuga nasseoyo*), "An error came up"

## How do you find a cash machine in Korea?

Most convenience stores in Seoul have an ATM near the entrance. Subway stations keep one close to the ticket gate. If you cannot spot it, ask any cashier or station staff:

**현금 인출기 어디 있어요?** — *hyeongeum inchulgi eodi isseoyo?* — "Where is the ATM?"

You will usually get a point and the word **저기** (*jeogi*), "over there." Follow it.

If you need a full bank branch for a larger withdrawal, ask:

**은행 어디 있어요?** — *eunhaeng eodi isseoyo?* — "Where is a bank?"

Major Korean banks — KB 국민, 신한 (*Shinhan*), 우리 (*Woori*) — have branches on most commercial blocks in Seoul.

## What do the Korean buttons on an ATM mean?

Press the English button on the home screen first — most machines in Seoul offer one. If there is no English option, these four labels cover every transaction you will need:

| Screen label | What it does |
|---|---|
| 현금 인출 (*hyeongeum inchul*) | Cash withdrawal |
| 잔액 조회 (*janaek johoe*) | Balance inquiry |
| 계좌 이체 (*gyejwa iche*) | Transfer |
| 종료 (*jongnyo*) | Exit / Cancel |

The PIN prompt says **비밀번호를 눌러 주세요** (*bimilbeonhoreul nulleo juseyo*), "Please enter your PIN." Type your number and press 확인 (*hwagin*), "Confirm."

## What if a foreign card does not work at a Korean ATM?

Not every ATM accepts foreign cards. Check for a Visa, Mastercard, or Plus logo on the machine before you insert. If the screen shows an error, find the nearest staff member and say:

**오류가 났어요.** — *oryuga nasseoyo.* — "An error came up."

They will point you to a machine that accepts international cards or direct you to a bank branch.

To check before inserting:

You: **외국 카드 되요?** — *oeguk kadeu dwaeyo?* — "Do foreign cards work here?"  
Them: **저쪽 기계는 돼요.** — *jeojjok gigyeneun dwaeyo.* — "That machine over there works."  
You: **감사합니다.** — *gamsahamnida.* — "Thank you."

## Can you ask for help at a Korean bank?

Yes. Most large bank branches in Seoul have a staff member near the entrance, and many have basic English support. But two Korean lines move things faster when the branch is busy:

**환전하고 싶어요.** — *hwanjeonhago sipeoyo.* — "I would like to exchange currency."

**이거 어떻게 해요?** — *igeo eotteoke haeyo?* — "How do I do this?"

Point at the screen or your card when you say the second line. Staff respond every time.
