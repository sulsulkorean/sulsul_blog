Korean phrases at an ATM in Korea lean on Pattern 03 from the SULSUL workbook — noun plus **있어요?** (*isseoyo?*), "Is there…?" Ask **현금 인출기 있어요?** (*hyeongeum inchulgi isseoyo?*), "Is there a cash machine?" inside a bank branch or at a convenience store counter when the screen is in Korean only. Pattern 15 — **얼마** (*eolma*, how much) — covers limits: **얼마나 뽑을 수 있어요?** (*eolmana ppobeul su isseoyo?*), "How much can I withdraw?"

> - Find a machine: **현금 인출기 있어요?** (*hyeongeum inchulgi isseoyo?*), "Is there a cash machine?"
> - Check your limit: **얼마나 뽑을 수 있어요?** (*eolmana ppobeul su isseoyo?*), "How much can I withdraw?"
> - Ask for help: **도와주세요** (*dowajuseyo*), "Please help me."
> - Foreign card works: **외국 카드 돼요?** (*oeguk kadeu dwaeyo?*), "Do foreign cards work?"
> - Need a receipt: **영수증 나와요?** (*yeongsujeung nawayo?*), "Does a receipt come out?"

## How do you find an ATM that accepts your card?

Many **은행** (*eunhaeng*, bank) ATMs sit just inside the glass doors, while **편의점** (*pyeonuijeom*, convenience store) machines often run 24 hours. If the screen freezes, staff at the counter can reset it — start with a quick **있어요?** (*isseoyo?*) question before you wander the block.

You: **저기요, 현금 인출기 있어요?** — *jeogiyo, hyeongeum inchulgi isseoyo?* — "Excuse me, is there a cash machine?"  
Them: **안쪽에 있어요.** — *anjjoge isseoyo.* — "It is inside."  
You: **외국 카드 돼요?** — *oeguk kadeu dwaeyo?* — "Do foreign cards work?"

## What do you say when the machine stops mid-transaction?

Listen for **한도** (*hando*, limit) and **잔액** (*janag*, balance). If the amount is too high, the screen may flash an error without English.

You: **얼마나 뽑을 수 있어요?** — *eolmana ppobeul su isseoyo?* — "How much can I withdraw?"  
Them: **하루에 100만 원까지요.** — *harue baegman won kkajiyo.* — "Up to one million won per day."  
You: **알겠습니다. 감사합니다.** — *algesseumnida. gamsahamnida.* — "Understood. Thank you."

For exchanging bills before you deposit cash, the same **얼마예요?** (*eolmayeyo?*) line from [exchanging currency at a bank in Korea](/exchange-currency-bank-korea) works at the teller window next to the machines.

## What usually goes wrong at a Korean ATM?

Travelers often pick **통장** (*tongjang*, bankbook) services on the menu when they only need **현금** (*hyeongeum*, cash) — look for the cash icon or ask **현금만 뽑을게요** (*hyeongeumman ppobeulgeyo*), "I only want to withdraw cash."

A second mistake is walking away before the card ejects. Wait until the machine beeps and says **카드 받으세요** (*kadeu badeuseyo*), "Please take your card." If the card stays inside, **카드가 안 나와요** (*kadeuga an nawayo*), "The card is not coming out," gets staff moving fast.
