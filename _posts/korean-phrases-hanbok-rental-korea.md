Korean phrases for hanbok rental start with Pattern 19 — **빌리고 싶어요** (*billigo sipeoyo*), "I want to borrow / rent" — combined with the return-time question from Pattern 04: **몇 시까지 반납해야 해요?** (*myeot sikkaji bannapaehaya haeyo?*), "By what time must I return it?" Rental shops near **경복궁** (*Gyeongbokgung*) palace and **북촌** (*Bukchon*) village each run their own check-out system, but these two patterns cover the full visit from fitting room to drop-off.

> - Start the rental: **한복 빌리고 싶어요** (*hanbok billigo sipeoyo*), "I want to rent a hanbok."
> - Ask about a color: **이 색깔 있어요?** (*i saekkal isseoyo?*), "Do you have this color?"
> - Return deadline: **몇 시까지 반납해야 해요?** (*myeot sikkaji bannapaehaya haeyo?*), "By what time must I return it?"
> - Take photos: **여기서 사진 찍어도 돼요?** (*yeogiseo sajin jjigeodo dwaeyo?*), "Is it okay to take photos here?"
> - Add accessories: **헤어핀도 빌릴 수 있어요?** (*heeоpin-do billil su isseoyo?*), "Can I also borrow a hairpin?"

## How do you choose your hanbok at the shop?

Rental shops near Gyeongbokgung line racks by color and then by style — palace style **궁중** (*gungjung*) takes longer to fit than everyday **생활 한복** (*saenghwal hanbok*). Point to the color you want and use Pattern 19 straight away.

You: **한복 빌리고 싶어요. 이 색깔 있어요?** — *hanbok billigo sipeoyo. i saekkal isseoyo?* — "I want to rent a hanbok. Do you have this color?"  
Them: **네, 있어요. 사이즈가 어떻게 되세요?** — *ne, isseoyo. saijeu-ga eotteoke doeseyo?* — "Yes, we do. What is your size?"  
You: **보통 사이즈예요.** — *botong saijeu-yeyo.* — "I'm a standard size."

## What do you say when nothing fits perfectly?

The outer robe ties at the waist so fit is flexible, but if a size feels wrong say **조금 큰 거 있어요?** (*jogeum keun geo isseoyo?*), "Do you have a slightly larger one?" or **조금 작은 거 있어요?** (*jogeum jageun geo isseoyo?*), "Do you have a slightly smaller one?" Staff usually swap the skirt layer — the **치마** (*chima*) — and adjust the sash themselves without extra charge.

## What do you say on the way back to the shop?

Rental time is typically two to three hours from check-out. If your walk around the palace runs long, call ahead and say **조금 늦을 것 같아요** (*jogeum neujeul geot gatayo*), "I think I will be a little late." Most shops are flexible but need the notice before the next rental slot opens.

When you drop off the hanbok, close with **너무 예뻤어요** (*neomu yeppeosseoyo*), "It was so beautiful," followed by **감사합니다** (*gamsahamnida*), "Thank you." Staff hear this dozens of times a day and it still lands every time.
