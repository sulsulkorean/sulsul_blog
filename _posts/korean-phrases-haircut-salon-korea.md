Korean phrases for getting a haircut start before you sit down. Say **조금만 잘라 주세요** (*jogeumman jalla juseyo*), "Please cut just a little," and the stylist knows you want a trim, not a dramatic change. Show your phone and add **이렇게 해 주세요** (*ireoke hae juseyo*), "Please do it like this," and you skip ten minutes of guessing.

> - Ask for a light trim: **조금만 잘라 주세요** (*jogeumman jalla juseyo*), "Please cut just a little."
> - Point at a photo: **이렇게 해 주세요** (*ireoke hae juseyo*), "Please do it like this."
> - Keep the length: **길이는 그대로 주세요** (*giineun geudaero juseyo*), "Please keep the length the same."
> - Check the price first: **얼마예요?** (*eolmayeyo?*), "How much is it?"

## How do you tell the stylist what you want?

Most Korean hair salons display a price list near the front desk. Walk in, say **커트만 할게요** (*keoteuman halgeyo*), "I'll just get a cut," and the staff will seat you. If you need a wash first, they usually ask — listen for **샴푸도 할까요?** (*syampudo halkkayo?*), "Would you like a shampoo too?"

The safest move for travelers is a photo. Open your camera roll, point at the style, and say **이렇게 해 주세요**. If you only want the ends touched, say **끝만 다듬어 주세요** (*kkeutman dadeumeo juseyo*), "Please trim just the ends."

| What you want | Say this |
|---|---|
| Light trim | 조금만 잘라 주세요 (*jogeumman jalla juseyo*) |
| Same length | 길이는 그대로 주세요 (*giineun geudaero juseyo*) |
| Bangs only | 앞머리만 다듬어 주세요 (*apmeoriman dadeumeo juseyo*) |
| Show a photo | 이렇게 해 주세요 (*ireoke hae juseyo*) |

## What if the cut is going shorter than you planned?

Korean stylists move fast once the cape is on. If the scissors are heading somewhere you did not expect, speak up right away — politely, but clearly.

**너무 짧게 하지 마세요** (*neomu jjapge haji maseyo*) means "Please don't cut it too short." You can also say **조금만 더 길게요** (*jogeumman deo gilgeyo*), "A little longer, please," while they still have comb in hand.

You: **조금만 잘라 주세요. 길이는 그대로요.** — *jogeumman jalla juseyo. giineun geudaeroyo.* — "Please cut just a little. Keep the length the same."  
Them: **네, 알겠습니다. 샴푸부터 할게요.** — *ne, algetseumnida. syampubuteo halgeyo.* — "Yes, got it. I'll start with shampoo."  
You: **네, 감사합니다.** — *ne, gamsahamnida.* — "Yes, thank you."

## How do you pay and leave without awkward small talk?

When the mirror cape comes off, the stylist may ask **드라이도 할까요?** (*deuraido halkkayo?*), "Would you like a blow-dry?" For a basic cut, **커트만요** (*keoteumanyo*), "Just the cut," keeps the bill simple. Pay at the desk with **카드 되요?** (*kadeu dwaeyo?*), "Do you accept card?" — most salons do.
