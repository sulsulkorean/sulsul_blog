August 2026 renewal headlines kept dropping for summer hits — production companies confirming **시즌2** (*sijeun tu*, season 2) while cast schedules were still in negotiation. **시즌2 나온대요** (*sijeun tu naondaeyo*), "I heard season 2 is coming," is what Korean fans post with a screenshot link, and **벌써 기대돼요** (*beolsseo gidaedwaeyo*), "I'm already excited," fills comment threads. Add **공식 발표 났어요** (*gongsik balpyo nasseoyo*), "The official announcement is out," when the studio confirms the news.

> - Share the news: **시즌2 나온대요** (*sijeun tu naondaeyo*), "I heard season 2 is coming."
> - React with hype: **벌써 기대돼요** (*beolsseo gidaedwaeyo*), "I'm already excited."
> - Official news: **공식 발표 났어요** (*gongsik balpyo nasseoyo*), "The official announcement is out."
> - Cast return: **원래 배우 그대로예요** (*wollae baeu geudaeroyeyo*), "It's the original cast."
> - Ask when: **언제 시작해요?** (*eonje sijakhaeyo?*), "When does it start?"
> - Celebrate: **드디어 나왔네요** (*deudieo nawatneyo*), "It's finally here."
> - Share relief: **너무 기다렸어요** (*neomu gidaryeosseoyo*), "I waited so long."

## How do you join a season-2 renewal thread in Korean?

Renewal posts spread as screenshots — your comment only needs to react or ask one practical question.

1. Repost with **시즌2 나온대요** and the article link.
2. Reply to friends with **벌써 기대돼요** when you feel the same hype.
3. Ask **원래 배우 그대로예요?** (*wollae baeu geudaeroyeyo?*) if casting news is still unclear.
4. Follow up with **언제 시작해요?** once production dates leak.
5. Drop **드디어 나왔네요** (*deudieo nawatneyo*) when you have waited since the finale.

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Season 2 announced | 시즌2 나온대요 (*sijeun tu naondaeyo*) |
| Already excited | 벌써 기대돼요 (*beolsseo gidaedwaeyo*) |
| Same cast? | 원래 배우 그대로예요? (*wollae baeu geudaeroyeyo?*) |
| Official news | 공식 발표 났어요 (*gongsik balpyo nasseoyo*) |
| Start date | 언제 시작해요? (*eonje sijakhaeyo?*) |
| Finally here | 드디어 나왔네요 (*deudieo nawatneyo*) |

## What do Korean fans ask when renewal news is vague?

Comment sections mix rumor and official quotes — one clear question beats guessing in English.

You: **공식 발표 났어요?** — *gongsik balpyo nasseoyo?* — "Is the official news out?"  
Them: **네, 공식 발표 났어요.** — *ne, gongsik balpyo nasseoyo.* — "Yes, the official announcement is out."  
You: **원래 배우 그대로예요?** — *wollae baeu geudaeroyeyo?* — "Is it the original cast?"

If renewal talk overlaps with a bonus episode drop the same week, keep **특별편** (*teukbyeolpyeon*) and **시즌2** separate — see [Korean phrases when a K-drama bonus episode drops](/korean-phrases-kdrama-bonus-episode-fans) for the extra-episode chat.

## What usually goes wrong in renewal fan threads?

International fans quote English entertainment news while Korean threads already moved to cast rumors — **원래 배우 그대로예요?** (*wollae baeu geudaeroyeyo?*) catches you up in one line.

A second mistake is treating unconfirmed leaks as fact. Add **진짜예요?** (*jinjiyeyo?*), "Is it true?" when the source is a screenshot, not an official banner. When the wait has been long, type **드디어 나왔네요** (*deudieo nawatneyo*), "It's finally here," before you link the article.
