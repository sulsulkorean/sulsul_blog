Seoul cafes roll out iced menus the moment humidity hits — and the counter moves faster when you can say **아이스 아메리카노 한 잔 주세요** (*aiseu amerikano han jan juseyo*), "One iced Americano, please," instead of pointing at the photo board. If every table looks taken, **여기 앉아도 돼요?** (*yeogi anjado dwaeyo?*), "May I sit here?" opens a seat without the awkward English wave.

> - Order an iced coffee: **아이스 아메리카노 한 잔 주세요** (*aiseu amerikano han jan juseyo*), "One iced Americano, please."
> - Ask about seating: **여기 앉아도 돼요?** (*yeogi anjado dwaeyo?*), "May I sit here?"
> - React to the heat: **정말 더워요** (*jeongmal deowoyo*), "It is really hot."
> - Praise the drink: **시원해요** (*siwonhaeyo*), "It is refreshing."
> - Take it to go: **포장해 주세요** (*pojanghae juseyo*), "To go, please."

## How do you order at a busy summer cafe?

Korean cafe staff expect short, direct lines — not a full English sentence translated word for word.

1. Walk up and say **아이스 아메리카노 한 잔 주세요** while the menu loads on the screen.
2. If you want less sugar, add **덜 달게 해 주세요** (*deol dalge hae juseyo*), "Make it less sweet, please."
3. When you find an open chair, ask **여기 앉아도 돼요?** before you sit.
4. After the first sip, tell a friend **시원해요**.
5. If you are walking to a pop-up event, say **포장해 주세요** at the register.

| Moment | Say this |
|---|---|
| Order iced coffee | 아이스 아메리카노 한 잔 주세요 (*aiseu amerikano han jan juseyo*) |
| Claim a seat | 여기 앉아도 돼요? (*yeogi anjado dwaeyo?*) |
| Comment on weather | 정말 더워요 (*jeongmal deowoyo*) |
| Praise the drink | 시원해요 (*siwonhaeyo*) |
| Takeaway cup | 포장해 주세요 (*pojanghae juseyo*) |

## What do Korean speakers say back?

Staff and cafe regulars keep replies short — perfect for beginners.

You: **아이스 아메리카노 한 잔 주세요.** — *aiseu amerikano han jan juseyo.* — "One iced Americano, please."  
Them: **네, 잠시만요!** — *ne, jamsimanyo!* — "Yes, one moment!"  
You: **포장해 주세요.** — *pojanghae juseyo.*

If someone at your table offers the outlet, **감사합니다** (*gamsahamnida*) is enough. Match the person, not the grammar chart.

## What usually goes wrong at a Seoul cafe in summer?

Learners memorize **안녕하세요** (*annyeonghaseyo*) but never practice the iced-coffee order line every barista hears before noon.

A second mistake is staying silent while friends switch to Korean for two seconds to order — one clear phrase keeps you at the table. For a simpler coffee order, see [ordering coffee in Korean](/ordering-coffee-in-korean).
