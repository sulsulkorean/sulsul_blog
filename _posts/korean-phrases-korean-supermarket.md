Korean phrases for shopping at a Korean supermarket come down to three moments: finding an item in the aisles, getting a quick answer at the deli counter, and handling checkout without freezing. Say **이거 어디 있어요?** (*igeo eodi isseoyo?*), "Where is this?", when you cannot spot something on the shelf, then follow with **카드로 내도 돼요?** (*kadeuro naedo dwaeyo?*), "Can I pay by card?", before the total comes up on screen. Pattern 03 in the SULSUL workbook — noun plus **있어요?** (*isseoyo?*) — handles every location question in the store, and Pattern 23 — verb stem plus **주세요** (*juseyo*) — covers any counter request.

> - **이거 어디 있어요?** (*igeo eodi isseoyo?*) — "Where is this?"
> - **카드로 내도 돼요?** (*kadeuro naedo dwaeyo?*) — "Can I pay by card?"
> - **봉투 필요 없어요** (*bongtu piryo eopseoyo*) — "I don't need a bag."
> - **얼마예요?** (*eolmayeyo?*) — "How much is it?"

## How do you find a product in a large Korean store?

Chains like E-mart and Lotte Mart span several floors, with most aisle signs in Korean only. Look up the Korean word for what you need before you walk in, then show it on your phone if speaking on the spot feels like too much pressure. Staff near the customer-service desk move quickly, so get the one-question pattern solid first.

You: **이거 어디 있어요?** — *igeo eodi isseoyo?* — "Where is this?"  
Them: **3층에 있어요.** — *3 cheunge isseoyo.* — "It is on the third floor."  
You: **감사합니다.** — *gamsahamnida.* — "Thank you."

| Need | Say this |
|---|---|
| Find an item | 이거 어디 있어요? (*igeo eodi isseoyo?*) |
| Find the checkout | 계산대 어디예요? (*gyesandae eodiyeyo?*) |
| Confirm the price | 얼마예요? (*eolmayeyo?*) |
| Ask for a sample | 시식 해 봐도 돼요? (*sisik hae bwado dwaeyo?*) |

## What do staff ask at the checkout?

Two questions land every single time. The first is about a loyalty card and the second is about plastic bags. Most chains charge for bags now, so staff ask **비닐봉투 드릴까요?** (*binibbongtu deurilkkayo?*), "Would you like a plastic bag?", right after they scan the last item. Have your answer ready before the total appears on screen.

You: **카드로 내도 돼요?** — *kadeuro naedo dwaeyo?* — "Can I pay by card?"  
Them: **네, 카드 받아요. 봉투 드릴까요?** — *ne, kadeu badayo. bongtu deurilkkayo?* — "Yes, we take cards. Would you like a bag?"  
You: **봉투 필요 없어요.** — *bongtu piryo eopseoyo.* — "I don't need a bag."

If you do want a bag: **봉투 하나 주세요** (*bongtu hana juseyo*), "One bag, please." For a receipt: **영수증 주세요** (*yeongsujeung juseyo*), "Please give me a receipt."

## What happens at self-checkout?

Self-checkout lanes are marked **셀프 계산대** (*self gyesandae*) and work like most countries, except the screen language defaults to Korean. If the weight sensor pauses and shows a staff alert, press the help button and say **도와주세요** (*dowajuseyo*), "Please help me," when someone arrives. They fix it fast and you will not need more words.

The loyalty-card question comes before the first item is scanned. **멤버십 카드 없어요** (*membeosip kadeu eopseoyo*), "I don't have a membership card," is the complete reply — no full sentence needed.
