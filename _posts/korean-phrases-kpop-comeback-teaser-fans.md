August comeback season means teaser drops at midnight KST — and the Korean fans type still works even if you watch the clip with English captions. **티저 봤어요?** (*tijeo bwasseoyo?*), "Did you see the teaser?" opens the thread, and **컴백 기대돼요** (*keombaek gidaedwaeyo*), "I am looking forward to the comeback," is what you send when the thumbnail alone breaks the group chat.

> - Ask if they watched: **티저 봤어요?** (*tijeo bwasseoyo?*), "Did you see the teaser?"
> - Share the hype: **컴백 기대돼요** (*keombaek gidaedwaeyo*), "I am looking forward to the comeback."
> - React to the preview: **대박이에요** (*daebakieyo*), "That is amazing."
> - Mention the pre-release: **미리듣기 나왔어요** (*mirideutgi nawasseoyo*), "The pre-release dropped."
> - Invite a watch party: **같이 봐요** (*gati bwayo*), "Let us watch together."

## How do you talk about a teaser without spoiling the full MV?

Fan communities mix English artist names with Korean reactions — a few Korean lines signal you are in the room, not just lurking.

1. Drop the link and ask **티저 봤어요?** before anyone else types.
2. When the beat hits, send **대박이에요** — safe praise, no plot theories.
3. Add **컴백 기대돼요** so friends know you are counting down with them.
4. If a pre-release track appears, say **미리듣기 나왔어요** to flag the audio drop.
5. Close with **같이 봐요** when you want a voice call during the premiere.

| Moment | Say this |
|---|---|
| Open the chat | 티저 봤어요? (*tijeo bwasseoyo?*) |
| Hype the date | 컴백 기대돼요 (*keombaek gidaedwaeyo*) |
| Quick reaction | 대박이에요 (*daebakieyo*) |
| Pre-release alert | 미리듣기 나왔어요 (*mirideutgi nawasseoyo*) |
| Watch together | 같이 봐요 (*gati bwayo*) |

## What do Korean speakers say back?

Replies in comeback threads stay loud and short.

You: **티저 봤어요?** — *tijeo bwasseoyo?* — "Did you see the teaser?"  
Them: **응, 방금 봤어!** — *eung, banggeum bwasseo!* — "Yeah, I just watched it!"  
You: **컴백 기대돼요.** — *keombaek gidaedwaeyo.*

If someone shares a fancam clip, **고마워요** (*gomawoyo*) works with peers; **감사합니다** (*gamsahamnida*) is safer when thanking a stranger in the comments.

## What usually goes wrong during comeback week?

Learners memorize dramatic lines from subtitles but never practice the short countdown line fans send when a release date is confirmed.

A second mistake is typing long English paragraphs while friends switch to Korean for two seconds — one clear reaction keeps you in the conversation. For concert-day lines, see [Korean fan phrases at a K-pop concert](/korean-phrases-kpop-concert).
