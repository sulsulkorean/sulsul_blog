Source Music posted to LE SSERAFIM's Weverse on August 28, 2026 confirming the group's second single album **"Made My Night"** drops September 11 in South Korea — with pre-orders opening the same day. Fan chats around a same-day teaser and pre-order launch move fast, and the Korean lines fans actually send are shorter than most learners expect.

> - React to the drop: **나왔다** (*nawatda*), "It dropped."
> - Ask if they saw it: **티저 봤어요?** (*tijeo bwasseoyo?*), "Did you see the teaser?"
> - Express excitement: **기대돼요** (*gidaedwaeyo*), "I am so excited."
> - Announce pre-order: **미리 주문했어요** (*miri jumonhaesseoyo*), "I pre-ordered already."
> - Plan a listen: **같이 들어요** (*gati deureoyo*), "Let's listen together."

## How do you react in Korean when the teaser drops?

A teaser clip lands before anyone is ready — you have about thirty seconds to type something before the timeline floods. Short lines work best.

**나왔다** (*nawatda*) is a raw present-tense exclamation: "It dropped," "It's here." Send it the moment the video appears and the chat understands. Follow with **헐** (*heol*), the all-purpose Korean gasp — roughly "Oh my" — and you are already in the conversation.

You: **티저 봤어요?** — *tijeo bwasseoyo?* — "Did you see the teaser?"  
Them: **네, 완전 기대돼요!** — *ne, wanjeon gidaedwaeyo!* — "Yes, I am so excited!"  
You: **같이 들어요, 9월 11일에!** — *gati deureoyo, guwol sibirire!* — "Let's listen together on September 11!"

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Teaser just dropped | 나왔다 (*nawatda*) |
| Ask if they watched | 티저 봤어요? (*tijeo bwasseoyo?*) |
| Express excitement | 기대돼요 (*gidaedwaeyo*) |
| Pre-order confirmed | 미리 주문했어요 (*miri jumonhaesseoyo*) |

## What do you say when the full track releases on September 11?

When the song drops, group chats shift from countdown hype to replay talk. **몇 번 들었어요?** (*myeot beon deureosseoyo?*), "How many times did you listen?" is the standard check-in after a title track release. A high number earns respect — say **열 번 들었어요** (*yeol beon deureosseoyo*), "I listened ten times," and the reactions arrive fast.

If the melody catches you off guard, **이 노래 계속 들려요** (*i norae gyesok deullyeoyo*), "This song keeps playing in my head," is the most honest thing you can say.

## How do you use 미리 in K-pop fan Korean?

**미리 주문했어요** (*miri jumonhaesseoyo*) breaks down as: **미리** (in advance), **주문** (order), **했어요** (past tense "did"). The word **미리** carries weight in fandom culture — it signals loyalty and timing. Fans also apply it to streaming: **미리 듣기** (*miri deutgi*), "early listen," is what groups call a pre-release clip. Knowing the word connects you to conversations that happen before the general public hears anything.
