Someone always ends up unable to go, and the seat moves to another fan in a thread that lasts about four messages. **아직 남아 있어요?** (*ajik nama isseoyo?*), "Is it still available?" is the one that has to arrive early, and **양도 받고 싶어요** (*yangdo batgo sipeoyo*), "I'd like to take the transfer," is what turns a question into a claim on the seat.

> - Check it is open: **아직 남아 있어요?** (*ajik nama isseoyo?*), "Is it still available?"
> - Claim it: **양도 받고 싶어요** (*yangdo batgo sipeoyo*), "I'd like to take the transfer."
> - Ask about the seat: **자리 어디예요?** (*jari eodiyeyo?*), "Where is the seat?"
> - Arrange the handoff: **어디서 만날까요?** (*eodiseo mannalkkayo?*), "Where should we meet?"
> - Close the loop: **확인했어요** (*hwaginhaesseoyo*), "I've confirmed it."

## How does a ticket transfer thread actually go?

Transfer posts get answered by five people at once, so the reply that reads fastest usually wins the seat.

1. **아직 남아 있어요?** first — one line, no introduction, because the answer is either yes or the post is already dead.
2. Follow immediately with **양도 받고 싶어요** so there is no gap where someone else steps in.
3. **자리 어디예요?** gets you the block and row before you agree to anything.
4. **어디서 만날까요?** moves it to a real place and time, which is where most threads stall.
5. **확인했어요** at the end tells the other fan they can stop worrying about the seat.

## What do Korean speakers say back?

Replies are short because the other fan is answering several people in the same minute.

You: **아직 남아 있어요?** — *ajik nama isseoyo?* — "Is it still available?"  
Them: **네, 아직 있어요. 관심 있으세요?** — *ne, ajik isseoyo. gwansim isseuseyo?* — "Yes, it's still here. Are you interested?"  
You: **양도 받고 싶어요. 자리 어디예요?** — *yangdo batgo sipeoyo. jari eodiyeyo?*

If the answer comes as a screenshot of the seating map, **몇 층이에요?** (*myeot cheungieyo?*), "Which floor is it?" gets the one detail the image usually crops out. ## What usually goes wrong with a transfer?

Learners write a polite paragraph explaining who they are and why they want to go, and by the time it sends the seat is gone. **아직 남아 있어요?** on its own is not rude in a transfer thread — it is the expected opening.

The other slip is leaving the meeting arrangement vague. "Near the venue" turns into two people circling the same building on show day, so ask **어디서 만날까요?** and repeat the answer back in your own message.
