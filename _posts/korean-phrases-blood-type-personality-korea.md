Korean blood type personality phrases are small-talk gold. Asking **혈액형이 뭐예요?** (*hyeollaek-hyeong-i mwoyeyo?*), "What's your blood type?" opens a conversation loop that Koreans use the way many cultures use star signs — to guess personality, explain quirks, and check compatibility. Whether you believe it or not, knowing the vocab lets you join in rather than smile and nod.

> - Ask the question: **혈액형이 뭐예요?** (*hyeollaek-hyeong-i mwoyeyo?*) — "What's your blood type?"
> - Answer for type A: **저는 A형이에요** (*jeoneun A-hyeong-ieyo*) — "I'm type A."
> - That explains it: **역시 B형답네요** (*yeoksi B-hyeong-damneyo*) — "That's so type B of you."
> - Sceptical reply: **저는 별로 안 믿어요** (*jeoneun byeollo an mideoyo*) — "I don't really believe in it."
> - Curious follow-up: **근데 맞는 것 같아요** (*geunde manneun geot gatayo*) — "But it does seem accurate."

## How do you ask about blood type in Korean?

The question **혈액형이 뭐예요?** (*hyeollaek-hyeong-i mwoyeyo?*) comes up at meals, on group trips, and in first-meeting conversations. Koreans typically answer with just the letter and 형 (*hyeong*, type): **A형** (*A-hyeong*), **B형** (*B-hyeong*), **O형** (*O-hyeong*), or **AB형** (*AB-hyeong*). The whole exchange takes about four seconds once you know the loop.

You: **저는 O형이에요. 혈액형이 뭐예요?**  
*jeoneun O-hyeong-ieyo. hyeollaek-hyeong-i mwoyeyo?*  
"I'm type O. What's your blood type?"  

Them: **저는 A형이에요.**  
*jeoneun A-hyeong-ieyo.*  
"I'm type A."  

You: **오, 잘 맞겠다!**  
*o, jal matgetda!*  
"Oh, we must get along well!"

| Blood type | Common personality label in Korea |
|---|---|
| A형 (*A-hyeong*) | Careful, organised, perfectionistic |
| B형 (*B-hyeong*) | Free-spirited, creative, self-focused |
| O형 (*O-hyeong*) | Confident, sociable, competitive |
| AB형 (*AB-hyeong*) | Rational, artistic, unpredictable |

## What if someone says you are "so B형"?

**역시 B형답네요** (*yeoksi B-hyeong-damneyo*) is a teasing compliment. **역시** (*yeoksi*) means "as expected" or "just like I thought" — drop it in after someone confirms their type and you sound like a native. If the label fits, try **맞아요, 딱 맞아요** (*majayo, ttak majayo*), "Yes, exactly right." If it doesn't, **저는 좀 달라요** (*jeoneun jom dallayo*), "I'm a bit different," keeps the chat going.

## What if you don't believe in blood type personality?

Skip the long explanation and say **저는 별로 안 믿어요** (*jeoneun byeollo an mideoyo*), "I don't really believe in it." Koreans are used to sceptics and often reply **근데 맞는 것 같아요** (*geunde manneun geot gatayo*), "But it does seem accurate," which restarts the game. Saying **재미로 하는 거잖아요** (*jaemiro haneun geojanhayo*), "We're just doing it for fun," lands perfectly.
