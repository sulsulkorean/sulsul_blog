SM Entertainment confirmed via Weverse on August 25 that SHINee's Taemin drops solo album *PHASE 1: Soft Violence* on August 31, 2026 — and fan cafes are already filling with the Korean comeback vocabulary every Shawol needs. These are not news phrases. They are the evergreen speaking lines that work at any K-pop comeback: in fan cafes, at album release parties, or on WeVerse comment threads with Korean-speaking fans.

> - Comeback hype: **컴백 기다렸어요!** (*kambek gidarryeosseoyo!*), "I've been waiting for this comeback!"
> - It dropped: **나왔다!** (*nawatda!*), "It's out!"
> - Title track reaction: **역대급이에요** (*yeokdaegeum-ieyo*), "This is the best one yet."
> - MV check: **뮤비 봤어요?** (*myubi bwasseoyo?*), "Have you seen the MV?"
> - Bias declaration: **태민이 최애예요** (*Taemin-i choae-yeyo*), "Taemin is my bias."

## How do you talk about a K-pop comeback in Korean?

The comeback cycle runs the same way for every idol: teaser → MV drop → album release → music show wins. Fans narrate each step with a small set of phrases. **나왔다!** (*nawatda!*) fires the moment streaming goes live. Then **타이틀곡이 뭐예요?** (*taitulgok-i mwoyeyo?*) — "What is the title track?" — starts the group discussion.

You: **타이틀곡 들었어요?** — *taitulgok deureosseoyo?* — "Did you listen to the title track?"  
Them: **네! 역대급이에요. 소름 돋아요.** — *ne! yeokdaegeum-ieyo. soreum dodayo.* — "Yes! It's the best yet. I get goosebumps."  
You: **저도요! 컴백 기다렸어요.** — *jeodoyo! kambek gidarryeosseoyo.* — "Me too! I've been waiting for this comeback."

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 나왔다! (*nawatda!*) | It dropped! |
| 역대급이에요 (*yeokdaegeum-ieyo*) | Best one yet / all-time level |

## How do you react to the music video in Korean?

When the MV lands, fans react in real time on fan cafes and live streams. **대박!** (*daebak!*), "Amazing!" or "Jackpot!" is the all-purpose short reaction. **실화예요?** (*silhwa-yeyo?*), "Is this real?" expresses disbelief at how good a comeback is. For a more specific compliment, **음악이 진짜 좋아요** (*eumagi jinjja joayo*), "The music is really good," uses Pattern 7 from the SULSUL workbook — subject plus adjective plus **아/어요**.

You: **뮤비 봤어요? 대박이에요!** — *myubi bwasseoyo? daebak-ieyo!* — "Have you seen the MV? It's amazing!"  
Them: **네, 실화예요? 진짜 좋아요.** — *ne, silhwa-yeyo? jinjja joayo.* — "Yes, is this real? I really love it."  
You: **음악이 진짜 좋아요.** — *eumagi jinjja joayo.* — "The music is really good."

If you want to declare your bias to a new fan friend, **태민이 최애예요** (*Taemin-i choae-yeyo*) locks it in — **최애** (*choae*) is fan-speak for "number-one favorite."

## How do you say you've been a fan for a long time?

**오래된 팬이에요** (*oraedoen paneyo*), "I've been a fan for a long time," is Pattern 7 applied to fan identity. Pair it with an era reference: **샤이니 초창기부터 알았어요** (*Syaini chochanggi buteo arasseoyo*), "I've known SHINee since the early days," uses Pattern 19 — time anchor plus **부터** (*buteo*), "from."

Korean-speaking fans will immediately want to compare era favorites — **몇 집이 제일 좋아요?** (*myeot jip-i jeil joayo?*), "Which album do you like best?", is the question that opens a very long conversation.

You: **오래된 팬이에요?** — *oraedoen paneyo?* — "Have you been a fan for a long time?"  
Them: **네, 샤이니 초창기부터 알았어요.** — *ne, Syaini chochang-gibuteo arasseoyo.* — "Yes, I've known SHINee since the early days."  
You: **저도요. 몇 집이 제일 좋아요?** — *jeodoyo. myeot jip-i jeil joayo?* — "Me too. Which album do you like best?"
