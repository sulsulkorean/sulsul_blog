Korean phrases for parking pair Pattern 04 — place noun plus **어디예요?** (*eodiyeyo?*), "where is it?" — with Pattern 44 — verb plus **해도 돼요?** (*haedo dwaeyo?*), "may I?" At a mall or hotel, ask **주차장 어디예요?** (*juchajang eodiyeyo?*), "Where is the parking lot?" before you circle the block. At a narrow alley spot, **여기 주차해도 돼요?** (*yeogi juchahaedo dwaeyo?*), "Can I park here?" saves you from a ticket or an angry shop owner.

> - Find the lot: **주차장 어디예요?** (*juchajang eodiyeyo?*), "Where is the parking lot?"
> - Ask permission: **여기 주차해도 돼요?** (*yeogi juchahaedo dwaeyo?*), "Can I park here?"
> - Need a ticket: **주차권 주세요** (*juchagwon juseyo*), "Parking ticket, please."
> - Check the fee: **주차비 얼마예요?** (*juchabi eolmayeyo?*), "How much is parking?"
> - Pay on exit: **정산해 주세요** (*jeongsanhae juseyo*), "Please settle the bill."

## How do you find parking at a mall or hotel?

Large buildings in Seoul often put **주차장** (*juchajang*, parking lot) signs on basement levels — B1, B2 — but the entrance is easy to miss from the street. Pull up to the gate or the front desk and use Pattern 04 with the place name.

You: **주차장 어디예요?** — *juchajang eodiyeyo?* — "Where is the parking lot?"  
Them: **뒤쪽으로 가시면 돼요.** — *dwijjogeuro gasimyeon dwaeyo.* — "You can go to the back."  
You: **감사합니다.** — *gamsahamnida.* — "Thank you."

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Where is the parking lot? | 주차장 어디예요? (*juchajang eodiyeyo?*) |
| Can I park here? | 여기 주차해도 돼요? (*yeogi juchahaedo dwaeyo?*) |
| Parking ticket, please | 주차권 주세요 (*juchagwon juseyo*) |
| How much is parking? | 주차비 얼마예요? (*juchabi eolmayeyo?*) |
| Settle on exit | 정산해 주세요 (*jeongsanhae juseyo*) |

## What happens at the gate or exit booth?

Automated gates print a ticket when you enter — keep it on the dashboard. Some lots use license-plate recognition and charge when you leave. Listen for **주차권** (*juchagwon*, parking ticket) and **정산** (*jeongsan*, payment settlement).

You: **주차비 얼마예요?** — *juchabi eolmayeyo?* — "How much is parking?"  
Them: **3,000원이에요.** — *samcheon won-ieyo.* — "It is 3,000 won."  
You: **카드 돼요?** — *kadeu dwaeyo?* — "Do you accept card?"

For finding the building entrance after you park, **입구 어디예요?** (*ipgu eodiyeyo?*) from the SULSUL workbook Pattern 04 uses the same shape — place noun plus **어디예요?** — and staff answer the same way.

## What usually goes wrong when parking in Korea?

Travelers park in a white-lined spot outside a shop and return to a note on the windshield — **여기 주차해도 돼요?** before you turn off the engine prevents that conversation. A second mistake is losing the entry ticket; without it, the exit gate will not open and you will need **정산해 주세요** at the booth with your plate number.

If you are dropping someone at a hotel first, [checking into a hotel in Korean](/check-into-hotel-korean) covers the lobby lines — then ask where the **주차장** (*juchajang*) is at the same desk before you head back to the car.
