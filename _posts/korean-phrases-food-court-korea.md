The 식당가 (*sikdangga*) — the dining floor of Korean department stores — packs a dozen restaurant counters under one roof. Walk to a stall, point at the menu photo, pay at the counter, and take a numbered slip. When the speaker calls your number, collect your tray. Two phrases carry the whole system: **이거 하나 주세요** (*igeo hana juseyo*), "One of this, please," and **자리 있어요?** (*jari isseoyo?*), "Is there a seat?"

> - Order one dish: **이거 하나 주세요** (*igeo hana juseyo*), "One of this, please."
> - Find a seat: **자리 있어요?** (*jari isseoyo?*), "Is there a seat available?"
> - Check your number: **몇 번이에요?** (*myeot beonieyo?*), "What number is this?"
> - More side dishes: **반찬 더 주세요** (*banchan deo juseyo*), "More side dishes, please."
> - Ask about spice: **이거 매워요?** (*igeo maewoyo?*), "Is this spicy?"

## How do you order at a Korean food court counter?

Walk to the stall that interests you. Most counters display photos with Korean labels and numbers. Point at your choice and say:

**이거 하나 주세요.** — *igeo hana juseyo.* — "One of this, please."

For two portions: **두 개 주세요** (*du gae juseyo*), "Two, please." The staff will hand you a numbered slip — hold on to it until pickup.

You: **이거 하나 주세요.** — *igeo hana juseyo.* — "One of this, please."  
Them: **번호는 삼십오번이에요.** — *beonhoneun samsip o beonieyo.* — "Your number is 35."  
You: **감사합니다. 자리 어디 있어요?** — *gamsahamnida. jari eodi isseoyo?* — "Thank you. Where are the seats?"

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Order one portion | 이거 하나 주세요 (*igeo hana juseyo*) |
| Order two portions | 두 개 주세요 (*du gae juseyo*) |
| Check your number | 몇 번이에요? (*myeot beonieyo?*) |
| More side dishes | 반찬 더 주세요 (*banchan deo juseyo*) |

## What do you say if a dish looks too spicy?

Korean food courts stock noodles, bowls, and soups that range from mild to very spicy. Before paying, ask:

**이거 매워요?** — *igeo maewoyo?* — "Is this spicy?"

If you need a milder option: **맵지 않은 거 있어요?** (*maepji aneun geo isseoyo?*), "Is there something not spicy?" The staff will point you to a safer choice. For food allergy vocabulary, [Korean phrases for food allergies](/korean-phrases-food-allergies-korea) has what you need at any counter.

## What happens when the speaker calls your number?

The speaker calls your number followed by **번 손님** (*beon sonnim*), "Customer number ___." Walk to the pickup counter, show your slip, and collect your tray.

Side dishes — **반찬** (*banchan*) — are often self-service at a small station near the seating area. If the bowl looks low, ask the nearest staff: **반찬 더 주세요** (*banchan deo juseyo*). Seating is shared and first-come. If you spot an open chair near someone eating, a quick **여기 앉아도 돼요?** (*yeogi anjado dwaeyo?*), "May I sit here?" is polite when others are nearby.
