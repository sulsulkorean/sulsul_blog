August 2026 drama schedules kept dropping **특별편** (*teukbyeolpyeon*, bonus episodes) after main finales — extra scenes fans thought were lost, packaged for streaming the week after the last regular episode. Open a fan thread with **벌써 봤어요?** (*beolsseo bwasseoyo?*), "Already watched it?" or lead with **아직 안 봤어요** (*ajik an bwasseoyo*), "I haven't watched it yet," to keep friends abroad spoiler-free.

> - Ask if they watched: **특별편 봤어요?** (*teukbyeolpyeon bwasseoyo?*), "Did you watch the bonus episode?"
> - Say you have not yet: **아직 안 봤어요** (*ajik an bwasseoyo*), "I haven't watched it yet."
> - Request no spoilers: **스포일러 하지 마세요** (*seupoilleo haji maseyo*), "Please don't spoil it."
> - React after watching: **진짜 감동이었어요** (*jinjja gamdong-ieosseoyo*), "It was really touching."
> - Ask where to watch: **어디서 볼 수 있어요?** (*eodiseo bol su isseoyo?*), "Where can I watch it?"

## How do you join the bonus-episode chat without spoiling?

Fan threads move fast once a **특별편** lands on a streaming app. Lead with whether you have seen it — not your full review.

1. Open with **벌써 봤어요?** when the bonus lands on your streaming app.
2. If you have not watched, say **아직 안 봤어요** before anyone replies with plot details.
3. Add **스포일러 하지 마세요** when someone starts typing a recap.
4. After you finish, react with one short line like **진짜 좋았어요** (*jinjja joasseoyo*), "It was really good."
5. Close with **다음 시즌 기대돼요** (*daeum sijeun gidaedwaeyo*), "I'm looking forward to the next season," if a renewal rumor is trending.

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Did you watch the bonus? | 특별편 봤어요? (*teukbyeolpyeon bwasseoyo?*) |
| Not yet | 아직 안 봤어요 (*ajik an bwasseoyo*) |
| No spoilers | 스포일러 하지 마세요 (*seupoilleo haji maseyo*) |
| Where to stream | 어디서 볼 수 있어요? (*eodiseo bol su isseoyo?*) |
| Loved it | 진짜 감동이었어요 (*jinjja gamdong-ieosseoyo*) |

## What do Korean fans type when the bonus episode ends?

Comments mix reaction emojis with short **해요체** (*haeyoche*) lines — you only need one or two to sound natural.

You: **벌써 봤어요?** — *beolsseo bwasseoyo?* — "Already watched it?"  
Them: **네, 방금 봤어요.** — *ne, banggeum bwasseoyo.* — "Yes, I just watched it."  
You: **엔딩 어땠어요?** — *ending eottaesseoyo?* — "How was the ending?"

If someone has not caught up, **천천히 말해 주세요** (*cheoncheonhi malhae juseyo*) works when voice notes fly past your listening level — the same recovery line from [when someone speaks too fast](/korean-phrases-when-korean-is-too-fast).

## What usually goes wrong in bonus-episode fan chats?

Learners paste long English plot summaries instead of one Korean reaction line. Staff and fans both respond faster to **진짜 좋았어요** than a paragraph of translation.

A second mistake is mixing up **특별편** with the regular finale — **결말** (*gyeolmal*, ending) is the main last episode; **특별편** is the extra drop after. For finale-night chat lines, see [K-Drama season finale watch party phrases](/korean-phrases-kdrama-season-finale-watch-party).
