Korean phrases for taking food to-go in Korea center on one request line. Say **포장해 주세요** (*pojanghae juseyo*), "Please pack this to-go," while pointing at your tray or the item on the menu board. Pattern 23 in the SULSUL workbook — verb stem plus **아/어 주세요** (*a/eo juseyo*) — turns any action into a polite ask, and **포장하다** (*pojanghada*, to pack) is one of the highest-frequency verbs at quick-service counters.

> - **포장해 주세요** (*pojanghae juseyo*) — "Please pack this to-go."
> - **이거 포장해 주세요** (*igeo pojanghae juseyo*) — "Please pack this one to-go."
> - **같이 포장해 주세요** (*gati pojanghae juseyo*) — "Please pack them together."
> - **여기서 먹을 거예요** (*yeogiseo meogeul geoyeyo*) — "I will eat here."

## How do you ask at a restaurant or food stall?

Counter-service spots in Korea expect you to say **포장** (*pojang*, to-go) or **매장** (*maejang*, eat-in) before they start cooking. Say it when you order, not after the food is already on a tray.

You: **이거 포장해 주세요.** — *igeo pojanghae juseyo.* — "Please pack this one to-go."  
Them: **네, 포장이요?** — *ne, pojang-iyo?* — "Yes, to-go?"  
You: **네, 감사합니다.** — *ne, gamsahamnida.* — "Yes, thank you."

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Pack it to-go | 포장해 주세요 (*pojanghae juseyo*) |
| Pack this item | 이거 포장해 주세요 (*igeo pojanghae juseyo*) |
| Pack together | 같이 포장해 주세요 (*gati pojanghae juseyo*) |
| Eating here | 여기서 먹을 거예요 (*yeogiseo meogeul geoyeyo*) |
| Extra bag | 봉투 주세요 (*bongtu juseyo*) |

## What does staff ask after you say to-go?

They may confirm portions or sauce on the side. One-word answers work — **네** (*ne*, yes) or **아니요** (*aniyo*, no).

You: **양념 따로 주세요.** — *yangnyeom ttaro juseyo.* — "Please put the sauce on the side."  
Them: **알겠습니다.** — *algesseumnida.* — "Got it."  
You: **봉투도 주세요.** — *bongtudo juseyo.* — "A bag too, please."

Adding **좀** (*jom*, a little / please) softens the request: **이거 좀 포장해 주세요** (*igeo jom pojanghae juseyo*). For convenience-store reheating, see [Korean phrases for the convenience store in Korea](/korean-phrases-convenience-store).

## What usually goes wrong?

The most common mistake is waiting until the food is plated and then miming a box. By then the kitchen assumed **매장** (*maejang*, dine-in). Ask **포장** at the same moment you say **이거 주세요** (*igeo juseyo*).

You: **실례합니다, 포장으로 바꿀 수 있어요?** — *sillyehamnida, pojang-euro bakkul su isseoyo?* — "Excuse me, can I switch to to-go?"  
Them: **아직 안 했어요. 포장해 드릴게요.** — *ajik an haesseoyo. pojanghae deurilgeyo.* — "We have not started yet. We will pack it."  
You: **감사합니다.** — *gamsahamnida.* — "Thank you."

A second slip is forgetting **봉투** (*bongtu*, bag) at stalls that hand you a sealed container without a handle. **봉투 주세요** (*bongtu juseyo*) saves you from juggling hot soup on the subway.
