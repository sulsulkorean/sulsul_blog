August world tour season means presale windows open at noon KST and sell out in minutes — and the Korean fans type still works even if you bought your seat from abroad. **예매 성공했어요?** (*yemae seonggonghaesseoyo?*), "Did your booking go through?" opens the group chat, and **티켓팅 어려워요** (*tiketing eoryeowoyo*), "Ticketing is so hard," is what everyone types when the queue crashes before checkout.

> - Ask if they got a seat: **예매 성공했어요?** (*yemae seonggonghaesseoyo?*), "Did your booking go through?"
> - Share the struggle: **티켓팅 어려워요** (*tiketing eoryeowoyo*), "Ticketing is so hard."
> - Celebrate a win: **드디어 구했어요** (*deudieo guhaesseoyo*), "I finally got one."
> - Compare sections: **어느 구역이에요?** (*eoneu guyeog-ieyo?*), "Which section is it?"
> - Plan the trip: **같이 가요** (*gati gayo*), "Let us go together."

## How do you talk about presale day without spoiling the setlist?

Fan chats mix English artist names with Korean reactions — a few Korean lines signal you survived the queue, not just watched from the sidelines.

1. Drop into the thread and ask **예매 성공했어요?** before anyone else celebrates alone.
2. When the site freezes, send **티켓팅 어려워요** — everyone feels that pain.
3. If you finally checkout, say **드디어 구했어요** so friends know it is real.
4. Compare seats with **어느 구역이에요?** when screenshots start flying.
5. Close with **같이 가요** when you want to share a hotel or taxi to the venue.

| Moment | Say this |
|---|---|
| Open the chat | 예매 성공했어요? (*yemae seonggonghaesseoyo?*) |
| Queue crash | 티켓팅 어려워요 (*tiketing eoryeowoyo*) |
| Got a ticket | 드디어 구했어요 (*deudieo guhaesseoyo*) |
| Compare seats | 어느 구역이에요? (*eoneu guyeog-ieyo?*) |
| Travel together | 같이 가요 (*gati gayo*) |

## What do Korean speakers say back?

Replies on presale day stay loud and short.

You: **예매 성공했어요?** — *yemae seonggonghaesseoyo?* — "Did your booking go through?"  
Them: **응, 드디어 구했어!** — *eung, deudieo guhaesseo!* — "Yeah, I finally got one!"  
You: **같이 가요.** — *gati gayo.* — "Let us go together."

If someone shares a screenshot of their seat map, **축하해요** (*chukahaeyo*), "Congratulations," keeps the chat warm without asking for their login details.

## What usually goes wrong on world tour presale day?

Learners memorize dramatic lines from subtitles but never practice the short check-in line fans send when a sale opens.
