Noraebang — **노래방** (*noraebang*), private karaoke rooms — is where Korean friend groups celebrate birthdays, relieve stress, and sing until the timer beeps. The room moves faster when you can say **이 노래 틀어 주세요** (*i noraee teoreo juseyo*), "Please play this song," instead of hunting the touch screen in silence. When someone nails the chorus, **정말 잘 불러요** (*jeongmal jal bulleoyo*), "You sing really well," is the line everyone expects to hear.

> - Request a song: **이 노래 틀어 주세요** (*i noraee teoreo juseyo*), "Please play this song."
> - Cheer a friend: **정말 잘 불러요** (*jeongmal jal bulleoyo*), "You sing really well."
> - Offer to go first: **제가 먼저 할게요** (*jega meonjeo halgeyo*), "I'll go first."
> - Ask for another round: **한 곡 더 할까요?** (*han gok deo halkkayo?*), "Shall we do one more song?"
> - Order drinks: **음료수 주문할게요** (*eumnyosu jumunhalgeyo*), "I'll order drinks."

## How do you start a noraebang room?

Korean karaoke rooms run on short, direct lines — staff and friends reply in one breath.

1. When you walk in, confirm the booking with **노래방 예약했어요** (*noraebang yeyakhaesseoyo*), "I booked a noraebang room."
2. If someone is shy, say **제가 먼저 할게요** and grab the mic.
3. Search the catalog, tap your pick, and ask the staff to play it.
4. After a great chorus, tell them **정말 잘 불러요**.
5. Before the timer ends, ask **한 곡 더 할까요?**

| Moment | Say this |
|---|---|
| Confirm the booking | 노래방 예약했어요 (*noraebang yeyakhaesseoyo*) |
| Pick a song | 이 노래 틀어 주세요 (*i noraee teoreo juseyo*) |
| Cheer a friend | 정말 잘 불러요 (*jeongmal jal bulleoyo*) |
| Keep the night going | 한 곡 더 할까요? (*han gok deo halkkayo?*) |
| Order drinks | 음료수 주문할게요 (*eumnyosu jumunhalgeyo*) |

## What do Korean speakers say back?

Staff and friends keep replies short — perfect for beginners.

You: **이 노래 틀어 주세요.** — *i noraee teoreo juseyo.* — "Please play this song."  
Them: **네, 바로 틀어 드릴게요!** — *ne, baro teoreo deurilgeyo!* — "Yes, I'll play it right away!"  
You: **정말 잘 불러요.** — *jeongmal jal bulleoyo.*

If the room needs another microphone, **마이크 하나 더 주세요** (*maikeu hana deo juseyo*), "One more mic, please," is enough. Match the room energy, not the grammar chart.

## What usually goes wrong on noraebang night?

Learners memorize **안녕하세요** (*annyeonghaseyo*) but never practice **이 노래 틀어 주세요**, the line every noraebang remote hears all night.
