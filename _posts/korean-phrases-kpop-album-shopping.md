Comeback week in Seoul sends fans to **앨범** (*aereom*) album shops with a list of versions and a photocard wish list. At the counter, **앨범 있어요?** (*aereom isseoyo?*), "Do you have the album?" is the first line staff expect, and **포카 교환해요?** (*poka gyohwanhaeyo?*), "Want to trade photocards?" is what fans whisper outside the store when two people pull the same bias.

> - Ask about stock: **앨범 있어요?** (*aereom isseoyo?*), "Do you have the album?"
> - Pick a version: **이 버전으로 주세요** (*i beojeoneuro juseyo*), "This version, please."
> - Check a photocard: **포카 뭐 나왔어요?** (*poka mwo nawasseoyo?*), "Which photocard did you get?"
> - Offer a trade: **포카 교환해요?** (*poka gyohwanhaeyo?*), "Want to trade photocards?"
> - Ask the price: **얼마예요?** (*eolmayeyo?*), "How much is it?"

## How do you shop for an album without blocking the line?

Record shops on release day move fast — short Korean lines keep the queue flowing and show staff you know the routine.

1. Walk up and ask about stock before browsing every shelf.
2. Point at the cover you want and say **이 버전으로 주세요**.
3. After unboxing nearby, ask another fan **포카 뭐 나왔어요?**
4. If you both want a swap, offer **포카 교환해요?**
5. At checkout, confirm with **얼마예요?** if the sign is in Korean only.

| Moment | Say this |
|---|---|
| Ask about stock | 앨범 있어요? (*aereom isseoyo?*) |
| Choose a version | 이 버전으로 주세요 (*i beojeoneuro juseyo*) |
| Compare pulls | 포카 뭐 나왔어요? (*poka mwo nawasseoyo?*) |
| Offer a trade | 포카 교환해요? (*poka gyohwanhaeyo?*) |
| Confirm the price | 얼마예요? (*eolmayeyo?*) |

## What do Korean speakers say back?

Staff and fans keep replies short — perfect for a noisy shop floor.

You: **앨범 있어요?** — *aereom isseoyo?* — "Do you have the album?"  
Them: **네, 아직 있어요!** — *ne, ajik isseoyo!* — "Yes, we still have it!"  
You: **이 버전으로 주세요.** — *i beojeoneuro juseyo.*

If someone agrees to trade, **고마워요** (*gomawoyo*) closes the swap politely. For concert-day fan talk, see [Korean fan phrases at a K-pop concert](/korean-phrases-kpop-concert).

## What usually goes wrong on album release day?

Learners memorize artist names in English but never practice the short stock question every clerk hears on repeat.

A second mistake is staying silent while fans switch to Korean for ten seconds to compare photocards — one clear question keeps you in the trade circle. For comeback teaser chat lines, see [Korean phrases when a K-pop comeback teaser drops](/korean-phrases-kpop-comeback-teaser-fans).
