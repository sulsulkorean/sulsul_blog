When Yonhap News Agency reported BLACKPINK's return with *Deadline* on February 27, 2026 — the group's first full project in over three years — Korean fan chats moved faster than any translation could keep up with. The phrases that flooded those threads are evergreen: fans say the same lines every time a long-awaited album finally arrives. Master them now and you will be ready for every comeback, not just this one.

> - **앨범 들었어요?** (*aelbeom deureosseoyo?*) — "Did you listen to the album?"
> - **타이틀 곡이 뭐예요?** (*taitel gogi mwoyeyo?*) — "What is the title track?"
> - **뮤직비디오 봤어요?** (*myujikbideo bwasseoyo?*) — "Did you watch the music video?"
> - **오래 기다렸어요** (*orae gidaryeosseoyo*) — "I waited a long time."

## How do you react when the album finally drops?

The first thing fans say is a simple check — did you hear it yet? The Korean question lands the same way it does in English, just with one phrase instead of a full sentence.

You: **앨범 들었어요?** — *aelbeom deureosseoyo?* — "Did you listen to the album?"  
Them: **네, 진짜 좋아요!** — *ne, jinjja joayo!* — "Yes, it is really good!"  
You: **저도요. 오래 기다렸어요.** — *jeodoyo. orae gidaryeosseoyo.* — "Me too. I waited a long time."

| Fan moment | Say this |
|---|---|
| Ask if they listened | 앨범 들었어요? (*aelbeom deureosseoyo?*) |
| React with excitement | 진짜 좋아요! (*jinjja joayo!*) |
| Say you waited | 오래 기다렸어요 (*orae gidaryeosseoyo*) |
| Ask about the MV | 뮤직비디오 봤어요? (*myujikbideo bwasseoyo?*) |
| Ask the title track | 타이틀 곡이 뭐예요? (*taitel gogi mwoyeyo?*) |

## How do you talk about the concept or sound?

Korean fans are specific about which era they prefer. **이번 콘셉트 마음에 들어요** (*ibeon keonsep maeme deureoyo*), "I like this concept," opens any opinion thread. Add **왜냐하면** (*waenyahamyeon*, "because") and a one-word reason — **힘차요** (*himchayo*, strong/powerful) or **감성적이에요** (*gamseongjeogieyo*, emotional) — and the conversation runs itself.

You: **이번 콘셉트 어때요?** — *ibeon keonsep eottaeyo?* — "How is this concept?"  
Them: **좋아요. 이번이 제일 좋아요.** — *joayo. ibeoni jeil joayo.* — "It is good. This one is my favorite."  
You: **저도 동의해요.** — *jeodo donguihaeyo.* — "I agree."

For a b-side you love: **이 곡도 좋아요** (*i gokdo joayo*), "This track is good too." For the one that surprised you: **이거 예상 못 했어요** (*igeo yesang mot haesseoyo*), "I did not expect this."

## What if you have not listened yet and someone asks?

Say **아직 못 들었어요** (*ajik mot deureosseoyo*), "I have not listened yet," and they will immediately tell you which track to play first. That gives you one more phrase to practice: **어떤 곡부터 들어요?** (*eotteon gokbuteo deureoyo?*), "Which track should I listen to first?"

This is one of the best low-pressure ways to open a Korean conversation with a fan — you ask a genuine question, they answer enthusiastically, and the whole exchange feels natural from the first word.
