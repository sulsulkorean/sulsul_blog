Korean phrases at a **네일샵** (*neilsyap*, nail shop) lean on Pattern 23 from the SULSUL workbook — verb plus **아/어 주세요** (*a/eo juseyo*), "please do it for me." Point at a sample photo and say **이렇게 해 주세요** (*ireoke hae juseyo*), "Please do it like this." Pattern 01 — noun plus **주세요** (*juseyo*) — opens the visit: **젤네일 해 주세요** (*jelneil hae juseyo*), "Gel nails, please," when you sit down.

> - Order the service: **젤네일 해 주세요** (*jelneil hae juseyo*), "Gel nails, please."
> - Pick a color: **이 색으로 해 주세요** (*i saegeuro hae juseyo*), "Please do it in this color."
> - Show a reference photo: **이렇게 해 주세요** (*ireoke hae juseyo*), "Please do it like this."
> - Ask the price: **얼마예요?** (*eolmayeyo?*), "How much is it?"
> - Need more time: **조금만 기다려 주세요** (*jogeumman gidaryeo juseyo*), "Please wait a moment."

## How do you book and sit down?

Nail shops in Seoul cluster near **홍대** (*Hongdae*) and **강남** (*Gangnam*) — many take walk-ins, but weekend slots fill fast. Call or message **예약하고 싶어요** (*yeyakago sipeoyo*), "I want to make a reservation," using Pattern 02 from the workbook, then confirm the time at the door.

You: **안녕하세요, 젤네일 해 주세요.** — *annyeonghaseyo, jelneil hae juseyo.* — "Hello, gel nails, please."  
Them: **어떤 디자인 원하세요?** — *eotteon dijain wonhaseyo?* — "Which design do you want?"  
You: **이 사진처럼 해 주세요.** — *i sajincheoreom hae juseyo.* — "Please do it like this photo."

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Gel nails, please | 젤네일 해 주세요 (*jelneil hae juseyo*) |
| This color, please | 이 색으로 해 주세요 (*i saegeuro hae juseyo*) |
| Like this photo | 이렇게 해 주세요 (*ireoke hae juseyo*) |
| How much is it? | 얼마예요? (*eolmayeyo?*) |
| Wait a moment | 조금만 기다려 주세요 (*jogeumman gidaryeo juseyo*) |

## What if the tech asks follow-up questions?

Staff often confirm length and removal — **기존 젤 제거해 주세요** (*gijon jel jegeohae juseyo*), "Please remove the old gel," uses the same Pattern 23 shape as **포장해 주세요** (*pojanghae juseyo*) from [ordering at a bakery in Korea](/korean-phrases-bakery-korea).

You: **짧게 해 주세요.** — *jjalbge hae juseyo.* — "Please keep them short."  
Them: **네, 알겠어요.** — *ne, algesseoyo.* — "Yes, got it."  
You: **감사합니다.** — *gamsahamnida.* — "Thank you."

If the color looks wrong under the lamp, **조금 더 연하게 해 주세요** (*jogeum deo yeonhage hae juseyo*), "Please make it a bit lighter," is clearer than pointing silently. Pattern 32 — **처럼** (*cheoreom*, "like") — also works: **사진처럼 해 주세요** (*sajincheoreom hae juseyo*), "Like the picture, please."

## What usually goes wrong at a Korean nail salon?

Travelers show a Pinterest screenshot but never say **이렇게 해 주세요** — the tech picks a similar shade and the result feels off. Say the line while you hold the phone up. A second mistake is skipping removal: old gel plus new gel chips in a week; ask **제거해 주세요** (*jegeohae juseyo*), "Please remove it," before the new set starts.

You: **얼마예요?** — *eolmayeyo?* — "How much is it?"  
Them: **5만 원이에요.** — *oman won-ieyo.* — "It is 50,000 won."  
You: **카드 돼요?** — *kadeu dwaeyo?* — "Do you accept card?"
