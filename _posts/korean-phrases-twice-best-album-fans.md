ONCE are celebrating as TWICE drops their Japanese best-of compilation *#BEST 2015-2025* on August 26, 2026 — a full decade of Japanese releases collected in one place. The fan debate about favorite eras, hidden tracks, and which TWICE era defined you is already loud. These are the Korean phrases that let you join that conversation as a real participant, not a silent listener.

> - Favorite song question: **좋아하는 곡이 뭐예요?** (*joahaneun gok-i mwoyeyo?*), "What is your favorite song?"
> - Era peak: **어느 시대가 제일 좋아요?** (*eoneu sidaega jeil joayo?*), "Which era do you like most?"
> - Fan solidarity: **역시 TWICE야!** (*yeoksi TWICE ya!*), "That's TWICE for you!"
> - Long-time fan: **오래된 팬이에요** (*oraedoen paneyo*), "I've been a fan for a long time."
> - Goosebumps: **소름 돋아요** (*soreum dodayo*), "I get goosebumps."

## How do you talk about your favorite TWICE era in Korean?

The best-of format turns every fan into a reviewer. Pattern 7 from the SULSUL workbook — subject plus adjective plus **아/어요** — gives you the structure for any opinion. **제일 좋아요** (*jeil joayo*) means "I like [it] most." Plug in any era name or track title.

You: **좋아하는 곡이 뭐예요?** — *joahaneun gok-i mwoyeyo?* — "What is your favorite song?"  
Them: **저는 'Fancy'가 제일 좋아요.** — *jeoneun 'Fancy'-ga jeil joayo.* — "I like 'Fancy' the most."  
You: **어느 시대가 제일 좋아요?** — *eoneu sidaega jeil joayo?* — "Which era do you like most?"

## How do you express being a long-time ONCE in Korean?

Duration is Pattern 19 — time anchor plus **부터** (*buteo*), "from." **처음부터 팬이에요** (*cheoumbuteo paneyo*), "I've been a fan since the beginning," lands differently from **최근에 알게 됐어요** (*choegeune alge dwaesseoyo*), "I got to know them recently." Both are valid — fans at different stages of the journey bond over the catalog a best-of album surfaces.

You: **언제부터 팬이었어요?** — *eonjebuteo panie-osseoyo?* — "How long have you been a fan?"  
Them: **처음부터요! 다 외웠어요.** — *cheoumbuteoyo! da oewosseoyo.* — "From the very beginning! I memorized everything."  
You: **역시 ONCE야! 같이 들어요.** — *yeoksi ONCE ya! gachi deuleoyo.* — "ONCE for real! Let's listen together."

## How do you react when a song hits exactly right?

**소름 돋아요** (*soreum dodayo*), "I get goosebumps," is the go-to in-the-moment reaction. Stretch it with **이 곡 들을 때마다 소름 돋아요** (*i gok deureul ttaemada soreum dodayo*), "Every time I hear this song I get goosebumps" — Pattern 23, verb plus **ㄹ/을 때마다** (*l/eul ttaemada*), "every time I do."

You: **이 노래 들을 때마다 소름 돋아요.** — *i norae deureul ttaemada soreum dodayo.* — "Every time I hear this song I get goosebumps."  
Them: **저도요! 역시 TWICE야!** — *jeodoyo! yeoksi TWICE ya!* — "Me too! That's TWICE for you!"  
You: **같이 들어요.** — *gachi deuleoyo.* — "Let's listen together."
