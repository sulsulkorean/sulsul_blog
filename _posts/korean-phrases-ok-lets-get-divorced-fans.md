KBS2's romantic comedy **OK! Let's Get Divorced** (*오케이, 이혼해요!*) premiered on August 19, 2026, dropping viewers into a couple who decide to split — and then keep getting in each other's way. The drama's sharp, honest dialogue makes it a phrase mine for anyone learning Korean. These are the lines fans are already quoting, and the ones you'll want ready for the next watch-party debate.

> - The title phrase: **오케이, 이혼해요!** (*okei, ihonhaeyo!*) — "OK, let's get divorced!"
> - Ask for honesty: **솔직하게 말해 줘요** (*soljikage malhae jweoyo*) — "Tell me honestly."
> - Push back: **다시 생각해 봐요** (*dasi saenggakae bwayo*) — "Think about it again."
> - Hold on: **잠깐만요** (*jamkkanmanyo*) — "Wait a moment."
> - Not giving up: **포기 안 해요** (*pogi an haeyo*) — "I won't give up."

## What does the drama's key line mean in everyday Korean?

**솔직하게 말해 줘요** (*soljikage malhae jweoyo*), "Tell me honestly," is one of those drama lines that works in real life — at a café when a friend is not saying what they mean, or on a date when the mood shifts. **솔직하게** (*soljikage*) is the adverb for "honestly" or "frankly," and **말해 줘요** (*malhae jweoyo*) is a soft imperative: "please tell me." Stack them together and the phrase sounds firm but not aggressive.

You: **이 드라마 봤어요?**  
*i deurama bwasseoyo?*  
"Have you watched this drama?"  

Them: **아직이요. 재미있어요?**  
*ajikjiyo. jaemiisseoyo?*  
"Not yet. Is it good?"  

You: **완전 공감해요. 솔직하게 말해 줘요 이 대사 너무 좋아요.**  
*wanjeon gonggamhaeyo. soljikage malhae jweoyo i daesa neomu joayo.*  
"I totally relate to it. I love the line 'Tell me honestly.'"

## How do Korean fans debate relationship dramas?

After each episode, fans split into teams — the **이혼 팀** (*ihon tim*, team divorce) and the **화해 팀** (*hwahae tim*, team reconciliation). To join the debate in Korean, try **저는 화해 팀이에요** (*jeoneun hwahae tim-ieyo*), "I'm on the reconciliation team," or flip it to **저는 이혼 팀이에요** (*jeoneun ihon tim-ieyo*) if the writer convinced you. Ask for a friend's take: **어느 팀이에요?** (*eoneu tim-ieyo?*), "Which team are you on?"

## What other relationship phrases come from this drama?

The drama uses **다시 생각해 봐요** (*dasi saenggakae bwayo*), "Think about it again," every time one character stalls. **포기 안 해요** (*pogi an haeyo*), "I won't give up," is the counterpart. Both phrases travel well outside the screen — use them whenever a friend is making a decision you want them to reconsider, in exactly the same gentle but firm tone the drama models.
