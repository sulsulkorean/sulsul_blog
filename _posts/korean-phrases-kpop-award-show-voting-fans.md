Award season turns every fan chat into a countdown. **투표 어떻게 해요?** (*tupyo eotteoke haeyo?*), "How do you vote?" is the question new fans ask first, and **오늘 마감이에요** (*oneul magamieyo*), "It closes today," is the message that decides whether the fandom shows up in time.

> - Ask how it works: **투표 어떻게 해요?** (*tupyo eotteoke haeyo?*), "How do you vote?"
> - Check the daily limit: **하루에 몇 번 돼요?** (*harue myeot beon dwaeyo?*), "How many times a day can I do it?"
> - Flag the deadline: **오늘 마감이에요** (*oneul magamieyo*), "It closes today."
> - Rally the chat: **같이 투표해요** (*gachi tupyohaeyo*), "Let's vote together."
> - Report back: **투표했어요** (*tupyohaesseoyo*), "I voted."

## How do you join a voting round that is already running?

Voting threads are built out of short updates. One clear question gets an answer in seconds; a long explanation of why you are confused usually gets scrolled past.

1. Ask **투표 어떻게 해요?** in the chat instead of guessing at the buttons.
2. Follow with **하루에 몇 번 돼요?** so you know what a full day of voting looks like.
3. When the round is closing, **오늘 마감이에요** is the message that moves people.
4. **같이 투표해요** turns a reminder into an invitation, which reads much better than a demand.
5. Once you are done, **투표했어요** — that is how the chat keeps count of itself.

|---|---|
| Ask how to vote | 투표 어떻게 해요? (*tupyo eotteoke haeyo?*) |
| Check the daily limit | 하루에 몇 번 돼요? (*harue myeot beon dwaeyo?*) |

| Rally the chat | 같이 투표해요 (*gachi tupyohaeyo*) |
| Report back | 투표했어요 (*tupyohaesseoyo*) |

## What do Korean speakers say back?

Replies in a voting chat are quick and practical — everyone is on their phone doing the same thing.

You: **투표 어떻게 해요?** — *tupyo eotteoke haeyo?* — "How do you vote?"  
Them: **앱에서 하면 돼요. 하루에 한 번이에요** — *aebeseo hamyeon dwaeyo. harue han beonieyo* — "You can do it in the app. Once a day."  
You: **오늘 마감이에요?** — *oneul magamieyo?*

If someone answers with a screenshot instead of words, repeating **마감 언제예요?** (*magam eonjeyeyo?*), "When is the deadline?" gets you the one detail the image usually leaves out. ## What usually goes wrong during a voting push?

Learners wait until they fully understand the rules before saying anything, and the round closes while they are still reading. **투표 어떻게 해요?** on day one costs nothing and saves the whole week.

The other slip is silence after voting. Fandoms track momentum by how many people say **투표했어요**, so skipping it makes the chat look emptier than it is.
