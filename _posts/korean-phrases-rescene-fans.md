When Korea JoongAng Daily reported that RESCENE had topped the Korea Celebrity Brand Reputation Index on August 29, 2026, the group's fan community lit up — and conversations about them jumped from Korean-only spaces to international ones. Whether you are a long-time follower or a brand-new fan who just heard the name, these are the Korean phrases that let you hold a real conversation about them.

> - Introduce the group: **레센 알아요?** (*resen arayo?*) — "Do you know RESCENE?"
> - Announce the chart win: **1위 했어요!** (*il-wi haesseoyo!*) — "They topped the chart!"
> - Declare fandom: **팬이 됐어요** (*paeni dwaesseoyo*) — "I became a fan."
> - Ask for a recommendation: **노래 추천해 주세요** (*norae chucheonhae juseyo*) — "Please recommend a song."
> - Listen together: **같이 들어요** (*gachi deuroyo*) — "Let's listen together."

## How do you talk about RESCENE with Korean fans?

Korean fan conversations about a chart-topping group move fast — within hours the phrase **1위 했어요!** (*il-wi haesseoyo!*) is all over the timeline. If you catch someone wearing a RESCENE pin or see the name in a café playlist, these openers get you in.

You: **레센 알아요? 브랜드 평판 1위 했어요!** — *resen arayo? beulaendeu pyeongpan il-wi haesseoyo!* — "Do you know RESCENE? They topped the brand reputation chart!"  
Them: **저도 팬이에요. 노래 들어봤어요?** — *jeodo pan-ieyo. norae deureobwasseoyo?* — "I'm a fan too. Have you heard their songs?"  
You: **아직 초보인데 노래 추천해 주세요.** — *ajik choboin-de norae chucheonhae juseyo.* — "I'm still new to them — please recommend a song."

## What do you say when a Korean fan asks how long you have followed RESCENE?

New fans often get asked 언제부터 팬이에요? (*eonjebuteo pan-ieyo?*), "Since when have you been a fan?" Being new is fine — **최근에 알게 됐어요** (*choegeun-e alge dwaesseoyo*), "I just found out about them," is a natural answer.

You: **최근에 알게 됐어요. 아직 노래를 많이 몰라요.** — *choegeun-e alge dwaesseoyo. ajik noraereul mani mollayo.* — "I just found out about them recently. I still do not know many songs."  
Them: **괜찮아요. 같이 들어요!** — *gwaenchanayo. gachi deuroyo!* — "That's fine. Let's listen together!"  
You: **감사해요. 제일 좋은 노래가 뭐예요?** — *gamsahaeyo. jeil joeun noraega mwoyeyo?* — "Thank you. What's your favorite song?"

## What if you cannot follow the conversation yet?

Chart conversations in Korean move quickly — vocabulary, abbreviations, and group nicknames. When the pace gets too high, slow things down:

**천천히 말해 주세요** (*cheoncheonhi malhae juseyo*), "Please speak slowly," buys you time without killing the energy. Follow it with **다시 한번요?** (*dasi hanbeon-iyo?*), "Once more?" and most Korean fans will happily repeat.

The goal is not to understand every word — it is to stay in the conversation long enough that your Korean actually improves.
