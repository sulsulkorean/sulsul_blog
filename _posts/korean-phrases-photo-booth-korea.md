Korea's **인생네컷** (*insaeng nekkeot*, "four cuts of your life") booths line the shopping arcades of Hongdae, Myeongdong, and Sinchon — every menu is in Korean. The machine queues four automatic shots, waits for you to pose, and prints a glossy strip in under two minutes. A handful of phrases keeps you moving before the countdown fires.

> - Invite someone in: **같이 찍어요** (*gati jjigeoyo*), "Let's take a photo together."
> - Ask shot count: **몇 장 찍어요?** (*myeot jang jjigeoyo?*), "How many shots does it take?"
> - Pick a frame: **배경 골라요** (*baekyeong goldayo*), "Choose a background."
> - Ready check: **준비됐어요?** (*junbidwaesseoyo?*), "Are you ready?"
> - Call a redo: **다시 찍어요** (*dasi jjigeoyo*), "Let's take it again."

## How do you get started at an 인생네컷 booth?

Touch the screen to wake the machine, then tap the card or **카카오페이** (*Kakaopei*, KakaoPay) icon to pay. Once the timer appears, lock in your **필터** (*pilteo*, filter) by scrolling the preview gallery before the first shot fires. After four shots, the machine shows a preview where you pick sticker overlays and confirm the print layout.

You: **같이 찍어요!** — *gati jjigeoyo!* — "Let's take a photo together!"  
Them: **좋아요, 필터 골라요.** — *joayo, pilteo goldayo.* — "Sure, choose a filter."  
You: **이거 어때요?** — *igeo eottaeyo?* — "How about this one?"

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Invite a friend | 같이 찍어요 (*gati jjigeoyo*) |
| Ask shot count | 몇 장 찍어요? (*myeot jang jjigeoyo?*) |
| Choose filter | 필터 골라요 (*pilteo goldayo*) |
| Ready check | 준비됐어요? (*junbidwaesseoyo?*) |

## What do you say when the shot does not come out right?

Photo strips print instantly — a blink or blurry frame is gone. During the session, signal a retry with **다시 찍어요** (*dasi jjigeoyo*), "Let's take it again," and hold up one finger for one more second. If the finished strip shows someone cut off at the edge, tell the booth attendant **얼굴이 잘렸어요** (*eolguri jallyeosseoyo*), "The face got cut off," and they can often reprint.

A fast tip: booths count down with a loud beep series — one long beep means the shot just fired. If you blink on shot two, there are still two left to recover in the session.

## How do you split the strip with your friend?

When the strip slides out, say **나왔어요!** (*nawasseoyo!*), "It came out!" — the line everyone calls at the pickup slot. Most booths print two identical copies: share with **나눠요** (*nanwoyo*), "Let's split it," and each keep one half.

To grab a digital copy before leaving, ask **QR 코드 있어요?** (*QR kodeu isseoyo?*), "Is there a QR code?" Most modern booths let you scan from your phone and download the file on the spot.
