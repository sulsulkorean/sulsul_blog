Photo booths — especially **인생네컷** (*insaengne-keot*, "Life Four Cuts") — are packed into every alley in Hongdae, Sinchon, and Insadong. The machine runs entirely in Korean, and an automated voice counts you down whether you feel ready or not. Three phrases get you through every step from the first screen tap to the printed strip you carry home.

> - Choose your group size: **인원수를 선택하세요** (*inwonsurul seontak haseyo*), "Please select the number of people."
> - Choose a frame style: **프레임을 선택하세요** (*peuremireul seontak haseyo*), "Please select a frame."
> - Print your photos: tap **인화** (*inhwa*) — the button that means "Print."

## How do you get through the touchscreen when everything is in Korean?

Tap anywhere on the idle screen to wake it. The first question is always group size. The labels read:

**1인** (*il-in*) — one person  
**2인** (*i-in*) — two people  
**3인** (*sam-in*) — three people

Tap your number. The machine jumps straight to frame selection. Scroll left or right, pick the border you like, then tap **선택** (*seontak*), "Select," to lock it in.

From there the machine counts down automatically and shoots four frames in a row. If you freeze at any screen the voice repeats the prompt. If you cannot figure it out, step outside and say to the attendant:

**도와주세요.** — *dowajuseyo.* — "Please help me."

They will walk in and tap through the steps for you in about thirty seconds.

## What do you say to the friend you drag into the booth?

The frame selection screen is where every argument happens. These short lines help you reach a quick agreement before the timer runs out:

**이거 어때?** — *igeo eottae?* — "What do you think of this one?"  
**이걸로 하자.** — *igeolro haja.* — "Let's go with this one."  
**준비됐어?** — *junbidwaesseo?* — "Ready?"

When the machine announces the shoot it says **찍겠습니다** (*jjikgetseumnida*), "I will now take the photo." The final word before the shutter is **하나** (*hana*), "one." Straighten up before it arrives.

A typical exchange at the frame selection screen:

You: **이거 어때?** — *igeo eottae?* — "What do you think of this one?"  
Them: **이걸로 하자!** — *igeolro haja!* — "Let's go with this one!"  
You: **좋아, 준비됐어?** — *joa, junbidwaesseo?* — "Great, ready?"

## What do you say when the prints slide out?

After tapping **인화**, move to the side slot and wait about thirty seconds. When the strip drops in:

**나왔어!** — *nawasseo!* — "It came out!"  
**잘 나왔다!** — *jal nawatda!* — "These turned out great!"  
**다시 찍을까?** — *dasi jjigeulkka?* — "Should we take them again?"

Most booths print two identical strips — one for each person in a pair. Take both and split them at the perforation. Some machines show a second screen for brightness edits or sticker overlays before printing; look for **완료** (*wanryo*), "Done," to skip past it.

| Step | Korean cue |
|---|---|
| Choose group size | 인원수를 선택하세요 |
| Lock in the frame | 선택 |
| Skip the edit screen | 완료 |
| Collect your strips | 인화 |

Payment is usually before the first shot — most booths accept card. If the kiosk only takes coins, the attendant outside can make change.
