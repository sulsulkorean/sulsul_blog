Olive Young is where K-beauty trends land first — sheet masks, sunscreens, and lip tints stacked floor to ceiling. Counter staff expect a quick skin concern, not a full English paragraph. Start with **피부가 건조해요** (*pibuga geonjohaeyo*), "My skin is dry," then ask **이 제품 추천해 주세요** (*i jepum chucheonhae juseyo*), "Please recommend this product," while pointing at the shelf.

> - Describe dry skin: **피부가 건조해요** (*pibuga geonjohaeyo*), "My skin is dry."
> - Ask for a pick: **이 제품 추천해 주세요** (*i jepum chucheonhae juseyo*), "Please recommend this product."
> - Find sunscreen: **선크림 있어요?** (*seonkeurim isseoyo?*), "Do you have sunscreen?"
> - Ask about samples: **샘플 있나요?** (*saempeul innayo?*), "Do you have samples?"
> - Check the price: **이거 얼마예요?** (*igeo eolmayeyo?*), "How much is this?"

## How do you talk at the Olive Young counter?

Staff answer in short, helpful bursts — perfect for survival Korean.

1. Open with your skin type: **피부가 민감해요** (*pibuga mingamhaeyo*), "My skin is sensitive," or describe dryness in plain English first if you freeze.
2. Point at a display and say **이 제품 추천해 주세요**.
3. For summer, add **선크림 있어요?**
4. Before paying, ask **샘플 있나요?**
5. At checkout, confirm **이거 얼마예요?**

## What do staff say back?

You do not need perfect grammar — one clear concern gets a useful answer.

You: **피부가 건조해요.** — *pibuga geonjohaeyo.* — "My skin is dry."  
Them: **이 수분 크림 어때요?** — *i subun keurim eottaeyo?* — "How about this moisture cream?"  
You: **샘플 있나요?** — *saempeul innayo?*

If you want to test texture first, **테스트 해봐도 돼요?** (*teseuteu haebwado dwaeyo?*), "Can I try it?" works at many counters. Say it once, clearly.

## What usually goes wrong at Olive Young?

Learners memorize product names in English but never practice **피부가 건조해요**, the line staff hear fifty times before lunch.

A second mistake is skipping Korean entirely while friends switch for two seconds at the counter — one phrase keeps you in the shopping flow. For mall-wide phrases beyond beauty, see [Korean phrases at a shopping mall](/korean-phrases-shopping-mall-korea).
