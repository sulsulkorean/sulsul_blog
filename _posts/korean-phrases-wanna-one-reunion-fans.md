When Wanna One — the group that sold out arenas on their 2018 world tour — reunited on stage at KCON LA 2026 in August, Korean fans in the crowd and in fan chats back home had one phrase ready: **진짜요?!** (*jinjjayo?!*), "Are you serious?!" A reunion of a disbanded K-pop group turns ordinary fan conversation into an emotional sprint, and these are the Korean phrases that keep up with it.

> - Disbelief: **진짜요?!** (*jinjjayo?!*), "Are you serious?!"
> - Moving: **완전 감동이에요** (*wanjeon gamdongieyo*), "I am so moved / touched."
> - Missed them: **보고 싶었어요** (*bogo sipeosseoyo*), "I missed them so much."
> - Old fan: **옛날 팬이에요** (*yennal panieyo*), "I am an old fan."
> - Cried: **울었어요** (*ureosseoyo*), "I cried."

## How do you react to a K-pop reunion in Korean?

The first reaction to any reunion news is short and loud. Pattern 04 from the SULSUL workbook — **진짜** (*jinjja*), used as an intensifier — can front-load almost every reaction: **진짜 대박** (*jinjja daebak*), "That is genuinely incredible," **진짜 울었어요** (*jinjja ureosseoyo*), "I genuinely cried."

You: **완도원 다시 만났대요!** — *Wanna One dasi mannandaeyo!* — "Wanna One reunited!"  
Them: **진짜요?! 어디서요?** — *jinjjayo?! eodiseoyo?* — "Are you serious?! Where?"  
You: **KCON LA 2026에서요. 완전 감동이에요.** — *KCON LA 2026-eseyo. wanjeon gamdongieyo.* — "At KCON LA 2026. I am so moved."

| Fan feeling | Korean phrase |
|---|---|
| Are you serious?! | 진짜요?! (*jinjjayo?!*) |

| I cried | 울었어요 (*ureosseoyo*) |

## How do you talk about memories with Korean fans?

Reunion conversations quickly turn into shared memories. Pattern 33 from the SULSUL workbook — **기억나요?** (*gieoknayo?*), "Do you remember?" — opens that door. **기억나요? 그때 진짜 좋았는데** (*gieoknayo? geuttae jinjja joannneunde*), "Do you remember? That time was so good."

You: **기억나요? 2018년 투어요.** — *gieoknayo? 2018nyeon tueyo.* — "Do you remember the 2018 tour?"  
Them: **당연하죠! 옛날 팬이에요?** — *dangyeonhajyo! yennal panieyo?* — "Of course! Are you an old fan?"  
You: **네, 처음부터 좋아했어요.** — *ne, cheoeum-buteo joahaeosseoyo.* — "Yes, I have liked them from the start."

**레전드예요** (*lejeondeueyeyo*), "They are legends," is the phrase Korean fans use for groups whose impact outlasts their active years. It works across any idol group reunion.

## How do you ask if a group might come back for good?

Reunion fans always want to know if this is a one-off moment or a real return. Pattern 36 from the workbook — **으면 좋겠어요** (*eumyeon jokesseoyo*), "I would love it if" — lets you say it without demanding an answer.

You: **다시 활동하면 좋겠어요.** — *dasi hwaldonghamyeon jokesseoyo.* — "I would love it if they came back for real."  
Them: **저도요. 아직 모르겠어요.** — *jeodoyo. ajik moreugesseoyo.* — "Me too. I still don't know."  
You: **언젠가는 꼭 다시 해줬으면 해요.** — *eonjenganneun kkok dasi haejwosseumyeon haeyo.* — "I really hope they do it again someday."

If your Korean friend already knows **완전 감동이에요**, they will feel the reunion through your words — not just your emoji.
