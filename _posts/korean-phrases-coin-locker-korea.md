Korean phrases for a **짐 보관함** (*jim bogwanham*, luggage locker) combine a location question and a price check. Say **짐 보관함 어디 있어요?** (*jim bogwanham eodi isseoyo?*), "Where is the luggage locker?" at a subway hub or bus terminal, then **얼마예요?** (*eolmayeyo?*), "How much is it?" before the door locks. Pattern 04 — **어디 있어요?** (*eodi isseoyo?*) — is the line you use at every major **역** (*yeok*, station).

> - Find the lockers: **짐 보관함 어디 있어요?** (*jim bogwanham eodi isseoyo?*)
> - Ask the price: **얼마예요?** (*eolmayeyo?*), "How much is it?"
> - Need a bigger size: **큰 사이즈 있어요?** (*keun saijeu isseoyo?*), "Is there a large size?"
> - How long can I store: **몇 시간까지요?** (*myeot sigan kkajiyo?*), "Until what time?"
> - Open again: **다시 열어 주세요** (*dasi yeoreo juseyo*), "Please open it again."

## How do you find and pay for a locker at the station?

Lockers sit near exits or customer service corners — signs say **보관함** (*bogwanham*) or show a suitcase icon.

1. Ask **짐 보관함 어디 있어요?** at the information desk if signs are unclear.
2. Tap the screen or read the label for size — point and ask **큰 사이즈 있어요?**
3. Confirm **얼마예요?** before payment — some take coins, others take card only.
4. Store your bag, keep the ticket or SMS code the machine prints.
5. Before leaving, check **몇 시간까지요?** so you are not surprised by overtime fees.

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Where are lockers? | 짐 보관함 어디 있어요? (*jim bogwanham eodi isseoyo?*) |
| Price | 얼마예요? (*eolmayeyo?*) |
| Large size | 큰 사이즈 있어요? (*keun saijeu isseoyo?*) |
| Time limit | 몇 시간까지요? (*myeot sigan kkajiyo?*) |
| Stuck locker | 다시 열어 주세요 (*dasi yeoreo juseyo*) |

## What does staff say when you ask?

Station staff answer with a floor number or a direction — catch **1층** (*ilcheung*, first floor) or **출구 쪽** (*chulgu jjok*, exit side).

You: **짐 보관함 어디 있어요?** — *jim bogwanham eodi isseoyo?* — "Where is the luggage locker?"  
Them: **1층 3번 출구 옆이에요.** — *ilcheung sam beon chulgu yeop-ieyo.* — "Next to Exit 3 on the first floor."  
You: **감사합니다.** — *gamsahamnida.* — "Thank you."

If your locker will not reopen, take the receipt to the desk and say **다시 열어 주세요** — Pattern 23 polite request. For KTX station navigation, see [Korean phrases for buying a KTX train ticket](/korean-phrases-ktx-train-ticket).

## What usually goes wrong with station lockers?

Travelers pick the smallest compartment and force a carry-on shut — **큰 사이즈 있어요?** before paying avoids the jammed-door panic.

A second mistake is missing the time limit. **몇 시간까지요?** at the start is cheaper than paying the overtime fine. For finding your platform after you collect your bag, [asking for directions on the subway](/ask-directions-korean-subway) covers the next step.
