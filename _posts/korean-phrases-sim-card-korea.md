Korean phrases for buying a **유심** (*yusim*, SIM card) lean on Pattern 03 — noun plus **있어요?** (*isseoyo?*), "do you have?" — and Pattern 01 — **주세요** (*juseyo*), "please give me." At Incheon Airport or a **휴대폰** (*hyudaeppon*, mobile phone) shop, ask **유심 있어요?** (*yusim isseoyo?*), "Do you have a SIM?" then point at the plan card and say **이거 주세요** (*igeo juseyo*), "This one, please."

> - Ask for a SIM: **유심 있어요?** (*yusim isseoyo?*), "Do you have a SIM?"
> - Pick a plan: **이거 주세요** (*igeo juseyo*), "This one, please."
> - Need data only: **데이터만 있는 거 있어요?** (*deiteoman inneun geo isseoyo?*), "Do you have a data-only one?"
> - Set up now: **지금 개통해 주세요** (*jigeum gaetonghae juseyo*), "Please activate it now."
> - Check price: **얼마예요?** (*eolmayeyo?*), "How much is it?"

## How do you buy a SIM at the airport or a phone shop?

Counters at Incheon and major carriers — SKT, KT, LG U+ — display plan cards with data amounts and day counts. Staff often start in English, but the counter queue moves faster when you lead with Korean.

You: **안녕하세요. 유심 있어요?** — *annyeonghaseyo. yusim isseoyo?* — "Hello. Do you have a SIM?"  
Them: **며칠 쓰실 거예요?** — *myeochil sseusil geoyeyo?* — "How many days will you use it?"  
You: **일주일이요. 데이터 많은 거 주세요.** — *ilju-ir-iyo. deiteo maneun geo juseyo.* — "One week. The one with a lot of data, please."

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Do you have a SIM? | 유심 있어요? (*yusim isseoyo?*) |
| This plan, please | 이거 주세요 (*igeo juseyo*) |
| Data-only option | 데이터만 있는 거 있어요? (*deiteoman inneun geo isseoyo?*) |
| Activate now | 지금 개통해 주세요 (*jigeum gaetonghae juseyo*) |
| How much? | 얼마예요? (*eolmayeyo?*) |

## What if you need help setting it up?

Pattern 23 — verb plus **아/어 주세요** (*a/eo juseyo*), "please do it for me" — covers activation and APN settings when the staff asks you to wait.

You: **지금 개통해 주세요.** — *jigeum gaetonghae juseyo.* — "Please activate it now."  
Them: **5분 정도 걸려요.** — *o bun jeongdo geollyeoyo.* — "It takes about five minutes."  
You: **와이파이 있어요?** — *waipai isseoyo?* — "Is there Wi-Fi?" (while you wait)

For staying online after you leave the shop, [Korean phrases for asking about Wi-Fi](/korean-phrases-wifi-korea) uses the same Pattern 03 line — **와이파이 있어요?** — at cafes and guesthouses.

## What usually goes wrong when buying a SIM in Korea?

Travelers point at a tourist SIM poster but ask for **esim** in English while the clerk is already speaking Korean — leading with Pattern 03 gets you to the right tray faster. A second mistake is walking away before activation finishes; **지금 개통해 주세요** at the counter means you test the connection before you leave.

If you land late and the telecom counter is closed, convenience stores sometimes sell prepaid cards — the same **있어요?** (*isseoyo?*) question works at [Korean convenience store](/korean-phrases-convenience-store) counters too, though the selection is smaller.
