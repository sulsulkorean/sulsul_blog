Korean phrases at a dentist narrow Special Unit 1 from the SULSUL workbook to **치과** (*chigwa*, dental clinic). Walk in with a sore tooth and open with **이가 아파요** (*iga apayo*), "My tooth hurts." When the hygienist rattles off instructions, Pattern 22 saves you — **천천히 말해 주세요** (*cheoncheonhi malhae juseyo*), "Please speak slowly," or **다시 말해 주세요** (*dasi malhae juseyo*), "Please say it again."

> - Name the pain: **이가 아파요** (*iga apayo*), "My tooth hurts."
> - Ask them to slow down: **천천히 말해 주세요** (*cheoncheonhi malhae juseyo*), "Please speak slowly."
> - Ask them to repeat: **다시 말해 주세요** (*dasi malhae juseyo*), "Please say it again."
> - Point to the spot: **여기가 아파요** (*yeogiga apayo*), "It hurts here."
> - Ask about treatment: **치료 받을 수 있어요?** (*chiryo badeul su isseoyo?*), "Can I get treatment today?"

## How do you check in at a dental clinic?

**치과** signs appear on the same streets as **약국** (*yakguk*, pharmacy) — often one floor above a cafe. Reception asks why you came. One symptom line is enough; they route you to the right chair.

You: **이가 아파요.** — *iga apayo.* — "My tooth hurts."  
Them: **언제부터 아팠어요?** — *eonjebuteo apasseoyo?* — "Since when has it hurt?"  
You: **어제부터요.** — *eojebuteoyo.* — "Since yesterday."

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Tooth hurts | 이가 아파요 (*iga apayo*) |
| Hurts here (point) | 여기가 아파요 (*yeogiga apayo*) |
| Speak slowly | 천천히 말해 주세요 (*cheoncheonhi malhae juseyo*) |
| Say it again | 다시 말해 주세요 (*dasi malhae juseyo*) |
| Treatment today? | 치료 받을 수 있어요? (*chiryo badeul su isseoyo?*) |

## What happens once you sit in the chair?

Dentists in Korea work fast. Listen for **열어 보세요** (*yeoreo boseyo*), "Please open (your mouth)," and **조금만 참으세요** (*jogeumman chameuseyo*), "Please hold on a moment." If a word flies by, Pattern 22 is safer than nodding and guessing.

You: **천천히 말해 주세요.** — *cheoncheonhi malhae juseyo.* — "Please speak slowly."  
Them: **네, X-ray 찍을게요.** — *ne, X-ray jjigeulgeyo.* — "Okay, we will take an X-ray."  
You: **아, 알겠어요.** — *a, algesseoyo.* — "Oh, got it."

For a general clinic visit — fever, stomach pain, not teeth — the post on [how to see a doctor in Korea](/see-doctor-korea-korean-phrases) covers **배가 아파요** (*baega apayo*) and triage at a hospital desk.

## How do you ask about cost and next steps?

Before drilling starts, ask **얼마예요?** (*eolmayeyo?*), "How much is it?" and whether insurance applies — **보험 돼요?** (*boheom dwaeyo?*), "Does insurance cover it?" Travel insurance varies; staff can tell you the out-of-pocket number in won.

You: **치료 받을 수 있어요?** — *chiryo badeul su isseoyo?* — "Can I get treatment today?"  
Them: **네, 바로 가능해요.** — *ne, baro ganeunghaeyo.* — "Yes, we can do it right away."  
You: **다시 말해 주세요.** — *dasi malhae juseyo.* — "Please say it again."

A common mistake is staying silent when you only caught half the explanation — **다시 말해 주세요** is normal in Korean clinics, not rude. A second mistake is waiting until the pain is unbearable; **치과** slots fill on weekday evenings, so calling ahead with **예약 있어요?** (*yeyak isseoyo?*), "Do you have an appointment slot?" saves a long wait.
