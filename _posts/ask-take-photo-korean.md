To ask someone to take your photo in Korean, hand over your phone and say **사진 찍어 주세요** (*sajin jjigeo juseyo*), "Please take a photo." It is polite **해요체** (*haeyoche*) and works with any stranger at a palace, a street market, or a rooftop cafe. Pattern 23 in the SULSUL workbook — verb stem plus the polite request ending **주세요** (*juseyo*) — covers exactly this kind of ask.

> - **사진 찍어 주세요** (*sajin jjigeo juseyo*) — "Please take a photo."
> - **같이 찍어 주세요** (*gachi jjigeo juseyo*) — "Please take one with us in it."
> - **한 번 더 찍어 주세요** (*han beon deo jjigeo juseyo*) — "Please take one more."
> - **여기서 찍어 주세요** (*yeogiseo jjigeo juseyo*) — "Please take it here."

## How do you start the request politely?

Pick someone who is not rushing. Open with **실례합니다** (*sillyehamnida*), "Excuse me," then extend your phone.

You: **실례합니다. 사진 찍어 주시겠어요?** — *sillyehamnida. sajin jjigeo jusigesseoyo?* — "Excuse me. Could you take a photo, please?"  
Them: **네, 알겠어요.** — *ne, algesseoyo.* — "Sure, got it."  
You: **감사합니다!** — *gamsahamnida!* — "Thank you!"

**주시겠어요?** (*jusigesseoyo?*) is slightly softer than **주세요** (*juseyo*). Both are fine with strangers.

## What do you say after they hand the phone back?

Check the photo before you walk away. If the angle is wrong or someone blinked, one more sentence fixes it.

| Need | Say this |
|---|---|
| Take a photo | 사진 찍어 주세요 (*sajin jjigeo juseyo*) |
| Include us both | 같이 찍어 주세요 (*gachi jjigeo juseyo*) |
| One more try | 한 번 더 찍어 주세요 (*han beon deo jjigeo juseyo*) |
| From this spot | 여기서 찍어 주세요 (*yeogiseo jjigeo juseyo*) |
| Vertical shot | 세로로 찍어 주세요 (*seroro jjigeo juseyo*) |

## What usually goes wrong?

The most common mistake is pointing at the camera and saying **photo** in English. People help anyway, but you miss a chance to practice. Another slip is forgetting to unlock the phone first — tap your passcode, open the camera app, then hand it over so they are not standing there waiting.

If the first shot cuts off your head, say **한 번 더 찍어 주세요** (*han beon deo jjigeo juseyo*) instead of trying to explain in English. Koreans retake tourist photos without thinking twice.

## A quick script at a busy spot

1. Choose someone who looks available — not someone sprinting for a train.
2. Say **실례합니다** and show your phone with the camera open.
3. Ask **사진 찍어 주세요** while pointing at the background you want.
4. Step into frame if you asked **같이 찍어 주세요** (*gachi jjigeo juseyo*).
5. Thank them with **감사합니다** (*gamsahamnida*) before you leave.
