Summer 2026 **팬사인회** (*paensainhoe*, fan sign events) stacked across Seoul venue calendars — album purchase proof, numbered wristbands, and a thirty-second window at the table where every fan rehearses the same two sentences. Hand over the album with **사인 부탁드려요** (*sain butakdeuryeoyo*), "Please sign it," and close with **감사합니다** (*gamsahamnida*) before staff moves you along.

> - Ask for a signature: **싸인해 주세요** (*ssainhae juseyo*), "Please sign it."
> - Ask permission for a photo: **사진 찍어도 돼요?** (*sajin jjigeodo dwaeyo?*), "May I take a photo?"
> - Say your name clearly: **제 이름은 …예요** (*je ireumeun …yeyo*), "My name is …"
> - Thank the artist: **감사합니다** (*gamsahamnida*), "Thank you."
> - Cheer them on: **화이팅** (*hwaiting*), "You got this!"

## How do you talk at the fan sign table in thirty seconds?

Staff control the line — your Korean only needs to cover hand-off, request, and thanks.

1. Hand over the album and say **사인 부탁드려요** while pointing at the cover.
2. If names are allowed, add **제 이름은 …예요** so they spell it correctly.
3. Ask **사진 찍어도 돼요?** only when staff announce photo rules — not every event allows it.
4. Say **화이팅** or **고마워요** (*gomawoyo*, "Thanks") as you step away.
5. Repeat **감사합니다** to the handler guiding the queue.

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Sign my album | 싸인해 주세요 (*ssainhae juseyo*) |
| Photo allowed? | 사진 찍어도 돼요? (*sajin jjigeodo dwaeyo?*) |
| My name is… | 제 이름은 …예요 (*je ireumeun …yeyo*) |
| Thank you | 감사합니다 (*gamsahamnida*) |
| Cheer | 화이팅 (*hwaiting*) |

## What does staff say when you reach the front?

Handlers keep the line moving — listen for **다음** (*daeum*, next) and **여기요** (*yeogiyo*, over here).

You: **사인 부탁드려요.** — *sain butakdeuryeoyo.* — "Please sign it."  
Them: **이름 말해 주세요.** — *ireum malhae juseyo.* — "Please say your name."  
You: **민수예요.** — *Minsuyeyo.* — "It's Minsu."

For asking a stranger to snap a photo outside the venue, **사진 찍어 주세요** (*sajin jjigeo juseyo*) from [asking someone to take your photo](/ask-take-photo-korean) covers the same Pattern 23 request ending.

## What usually goes wrong at a fan sign?

Fans rehearse English fan letters but freeze on **사인 부탁드려요** — the staff phrase they hear a hundred times per hour.

A second mistake is blocking the table after your turn. One **감사합니다**, step left, done. For album shopping lines earlier in the day, see [Korean phrases when shopping for K-pop albums](/korean-phrases-kpop-album-shopping).
