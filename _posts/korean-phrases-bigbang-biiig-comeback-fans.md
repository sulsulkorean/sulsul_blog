Allkpop reported that BIGBANG opened the XX:COSMOS world tour at Goyang Stadium on August 21, 2026 — the day after the group dropped the single **'BiiiG'** — marking their first full-group Korean concert run in years. If you are talking with Korean fans right now, the conversation is already in motion. These are the phrases that let you join it in Korean rather than watching from the outside.

> - Drop the news: **빅뱅 컴백했어요!** (*Bigbaeng keombaekhaesseoyo!*) — "BIGBANG came back!"
> - Ask if they heard it: **신곡 들었어요?** (*singok deureosseoyo?*) — "Did you listen to the new song?"
> - Share your verdict: **역시 빅뱅이에요** (*yeoksi Bigbaeng-ieyo*) — "It's BIGBANG, as expected."
> - Talk concert: **콘서트 가고 싶어요** (*konseoteu gago sipeoyo*) — "I want to go to the concert."
> - Ask about tickets: **티켓 구했어요?** (*tiket guhaesseoyo?*) — "Did you get tickets?"

## How do fans talk about a BIGBANG comeback in Korean?

When a group drops new music, Korean fans open group chats with a short announcement and then vote on the song. The word **컴백** (*keombaek*, comeback) covers any return — single, EP, or full album — so you can use it no matter what format **'BiiiG'** arrives in for each fan's timeline.

You: **빅뱅 컴백했어요! 신곡 들었어요?**  
*Bigbaeng keombaekhaesseoyo! singok deureosseoyo?*  
"BIGBANG came back! Did you listen to the new song?"  

Them: **네! 너무 좋아요. 역시 빅뱅이에요.**  
*ne! neomu joayo. yeoksi Bigbaeng-ieyo.*  
"Yes! I love it. It's BIGBANG, as expected."  

You: **콘서트 같이 봐요!**  
*konseoteu gachi bwayo!*  
"Let's go to the concert together!"

| Moment | Fan phrase |
|---|---|
| Comeback drops | 빅뱅 컴백했어요! (*Bigbaeng keombaekhaesseoyo!*) |
| Praise the song | 명곡이에요 (*myeonggok-ieyo*) — "It's a masterpiece." |
| React to the stage | 무대 대박이에요 (*mudae daebak-ieyo*) — "The stage is insane." |
| Ask about the concert | 콘서트 어땠어요? (*konseoteu eottaesseoyo?*) |

## What do you say when you finally get concert tickets?

Ticket drops for K-pop tours sell out in minutes. Once you have yours, the phrase **티켓 구했어요!** (*tiket guhaesseoyo!*, "I got tickets!") is the announcement — say it with an exclamation and fans will understand the relief. If you missed out, **다음엔 꼭 가고 싶어요** (*daeumenyeon kkok gago sipeoyo*), "I really want to go next time," keeps the conversation open.

## How do you talk about watching the concert live stream?

Many fans watch Korean concerts via live stream on Weverse or YouTube Live. If the show airs late your time, **라이브 챙겨 봤어요?** (*raibu chaenggyeo bwasseoyo?*), "Did you catch the live stream?" opens a recap conversation. Share your highlight with **이 무대 너무 좋았어요** (*i mudae neomu joasseoyo*), "I loved this stage," or ask for theirs: **어떤 무대가 제일 좋았어요?** (*eotteon mudaega jeil joasseoyo?*), "Which stage did you like the most?"
