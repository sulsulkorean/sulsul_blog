August finale week means the last episode of a hit K-drama drops Saturday night KST — and the Korean fans type still works even if you watch with English subtitles on delay. **결말 봤어요?** (*gyeolmal bwasseoyo?*), "Did you see the finale?" opens the thread, and **엔딩 어땠어요?** (*ending eottaesseoyo?*), "How was the ending?" is what you send when the credits roll and nobody can agree yet.

> - Ask if they finished: **결말 봤어요?** (*gyeolmal bwasseoyo?*), "Did you see the finale?"
> - Ask about the ending: **엔딩 어땠어요?** (*ending eottaesseoyo?*), "How was the ending?"
> - Warn against spoilers: **스포 하지 마세요** (*spo haji maseyo*), "No spoilers, please."
> - Share your reaction: **울었어요** (*ureosseoyo*), "I cried."
> - Invite a rewatch: **같이 다시 봐요** (*gati dasi bwayo*), "Let us watch again together."

## How do you react when the finale ends without ruining it for others?

Watch-party chats mix English show titles with short Korean reactions — a few Korean lines signal you finished the episode, not just read the recap.

1. Drop into the thread and ask **결말 봤어요?** once you hit the last scene.
2. When opinions split, send **엔딩 어땠어요?** to invite debate without a wall of text.
3. Before friends in other time zones catch up, say **스포 하지 마세요** in the chat header.
4. If the last scene hit hard, add **울었어요** — honest and short.
5. Close with **같이 다시 봐요** when you want a voice call for a second watch.

| Moment | Say this |
|---|---|
| Open the chat | 결말 봤어요? (*gyeolmal bwasseoyo?*) |
| Ask the ending | 엔딩 어땠어요? (*ending eottaesseoyo?*) |
| No spoilers | 스포 하지 마세요 (*spo haji maseyo*) |
| Emotional hit | 울었어요 (*ureosseoyo*) |
| Rewatch invite | 같이 다시 봐요 (*gati dasi bwayo*) |

## What do Korean speakers say back?

Replies after a finale stay loud and emotional.

You: **결말 봤어요?** — *gyeolmal bwasseoyo?* — "Did you see the finale?"  
Them: **응, 방금 봤어. 울었어.** — *eung, banggeum bwasseo. ureosseo.* — "Yeah, I just watched. I cried."  
You: **엔딩 어땠어요?** — *ending eottaesseoyo?* — "How was the ending?"

If someone asks whether to start the series now, **지금 봐도 돼요** (*jigeum bwado dwaeyo*), "You can watch it now," works better than a three-paragraph spoiler essay.

## What usually goes wrong during finale weekend?

Learners memorize dramatic lines from subtitles but never practice the short check-in line fans send when credits roll.
