September 2026 comeback week means the **K-pop dance practice video** drops a few days after the first stage — the clean studio take fans replay to catch every move, and the Korean fans type still works even if you watch it with English captions. **안무 영상 봤어요?** (*anmu yeongsang bwasseoyo?*), "Did you watch the dance practice?" opens the thread when someone shares the studio clip, and **칼군무 미쳤어요** (*kalgunmu michyeosseoyo*), "The synced choreo is insane," is what you send when the group hits the formation change together.

> - Ask about the clip: **안무 영상 봤어요?** (*anmu yeongsang bwasseoyo?*), "Did you watch the dance practice?"
> - Comment on the moves: **안무 진짜 어려워 보여요** (*anmu jinjja eoryeowo boyeoyo*), "The choreo looks really hard."
> - Praise the sync: **칼군무 미쳤어요** (*kalgunmu michyeosseoyo*), "The synced choreo is insane."
> - Point to a part: **이 부분 다시 봐요** (*i bubun dasi bwayo*), "Let's watch this part again."
> - Say you'll try it: **저 이거 따라 할래요** (*jeo igeo ttara hallaeyo*), "I'm going to try this dance."

## How do you join a dance practice thread without just dropping the link?

Dance practice threads mix English move names with short Korean reactions — a few clear lines signal you are studying the choreo, not just replaying for the bias cam.

1. Share the studio clip and ask **안무 영상 봤어요?** before anyone else types.
2. When the hardest section hits, send **안무 진짜 어려워 보여요** to pull the room into a breakdown chat.
3. On the formation change, add **칼군무 미쳤어요** — loud praise the whole thread can echo.
4. If one part needs a rewatch, type **이 부분 다시 봐요** to slow the room down together.
5. Before the thread moves on, say **저 이거 따라 할래요** when you want to learn it yourself.

| Open the chat | 안무 영상 봤어요? (*anmu yeongsang bwasseoyo?*) |

| Rewatch a part | 이 부분 다시 봐요 (*i bubun dasi bwayo*) |

## What do Korean speakers say back?

Replies in dance practice threads stay short, loud, and choreo-focused.

You: **안무 영상 봤어요?** — *anmu yeongsang bwasseoyo?* — "Did you watch the dance practice?"  
Them: **응, 칼군무 미쳤어!** — *eung, kalgunmu michyeosseo!* — "Yeah, the synced choreo is insane!"  
You: **저 이거 따라 할래요.** — *jeo igeo ttara hallaeyo.*
