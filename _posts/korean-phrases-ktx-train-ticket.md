Korean phrases for buying a KTX train ticket start with one wish line and two follow-ups. Say **표 사고 싶어요** (*pyo sago sipeoyo*), "I'd like to buy a ticket," name your destination city, then ask **몇 시에 출발해요?** (*myeot sie chulbalhaeyo?*), "What time does it depart?" Catching the clerk's follow-up questions matters as much as the opening line.

> - Open at the counter: **표 사고 싶어요** (*pyo sago sipeoyo*), "I'd like to buy a ticket."
> - Name where you are going: **부산 가요** (*Busan gayo*), "I'm going to Busan."
> - Ask departure time: **몇 시에 출발해요?** (*myeot sie chulbalhaeyo?*), "What time does it depart?"
> - Ask about seats: **창가 자리 있어요?** (*changga jari isseoyo?*), "Is there a window seat?"

## How do you start at the ticket counter?

KTX counters sit inside major stations like Seoul Station and Busan Station. Look for **KTX / SRT** signs above the window. The reusable pattern is verb stem + **고 싶어요** (*go sipeoyo*), "I'd like to…"

You: **안녕하세요, 표 사고 싶어요.** — *annyeonghaseyo, pyo sago sipeoyo.* — "Hello, I'd like to buy a ticket."  
Them: **어디까지 가세요?** — *eodikkaji gaseyo?* — "Where are you going?"  
You: **부산이요.** — *Busaniyo.* — "To Busan."

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Start the purchase | 표 사고 싶어요 (*pyo sago sipeoyo*) |
| Name destination | 부산이요 (*Busaniyo*) |
| Ask departure time | 몇 시에 출발해요? (*myeot sie chulbalhaeyo?*) |
| Ask for window seat | 창가 자리 있어요? (*changga jari isseoyo?*) |
| Need a receipt | 영수증 주세요 (*yeongsujeung juseyo*) |

## What does the clerk ask after your destination?

Once you name the city, the clerk asks about date and seat class. These replies stay short — one phrase each.

You: **오늘 오후에 표 있어요?** — *oneul ohu-e pyo isseoyo?* — "Do you have tickets for this afternoon?"  
Them: **3시 KTX 있어요.** — *se si KTX isseoyo.* — "There is a 3 o'clock KTX."  
You: **창가 자리 있어요?** — *changga jari isseoyo?* — "Is there a window seat?"

If the train you wanted is sold out, the clerk may offer the next one. Confirm with **그 표 주세요** (*geu pyo juseyo*), "That ticket, please." For station navigation, see [asking for directions on the Korean subway](/ask-directions-korean-subway).

## What usually goes wrong at the station?

The most common freeze point is the time question. Travelers nail the opening line but stumble when the clerk asks **몇 시 train 원하세요?** — answer with a time window: **2시에서 4시 사이요** (*du si-eseo ne si sa-iyo*), "Between 2 and 4 o'clock."

You: **몇 시 기차가 제일 빨라요?** — *myeot si gichaga jeil ppallayo?* — "Which train is the fastest?"  
Them: **12시 30분 기차가 제일 빨라요.** — *yeol du si samsip bun gichaga jeil ppallayo.* — "The 12:30 train is the fastest."  
You: **그 표 주세요.** — *geu pyo juseyo.* — "That ticket, please."

A second mistake is confusing **역** (*yeok*, train station) with **정류장** (*jeongnyujang*, bus stop). At a rail counter, say **KTX 표 파는 곳 어디예요?** (*KTX pyo paneun got eodiyeyo?*), "Where do they sell KTX tickets?"
