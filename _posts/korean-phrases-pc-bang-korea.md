Korean phrases for a **피시방** (*pisibang*, PC bang) begin before you sit down. The counter staff assign seats by number and charge by the hour, so saying **자리 있어요?** (*jari isseoyo?*), "Is there a seat available?" is the first phrase you need. Everything else — adding time, ordering food, or flagging broken gear — follows the same counter rhythm.

> - Check for a seat: **자리 있어요?** (*jari isseoyo?*) — "Is there a seat available?"
> - Pick your spot: **저 자리로 할게요** (*jeo jariro halgeyo*) — "I'll take that seat."
> - Add time: **한 시간 충전해 주세요** (*han sigan chungjeonhae juseyo*) — "Please add one hour."
> - Order food: **라면 주문할게요** (*ramyeon jumunhalgeyo*) — "I'll order ramyeon."
> - Pay and leave: **계산해 주세요** (*gyesanhae juseyo*) — "Please bill me."

## How do you check in at a Korean PC bang?

Staff sit behind a raised counter and log your seat in their system. Pay upfront or tap a card, then find your number on the row of monitors. Self-service kiosks at newer locations skip the spoken exchange — but when a staff member is at the desk, a clear phrase gets you seated in ten seconds.

You: **안녕하세요. 자리 있어요?**  
*annyeonghaseyo. jari isseoyo?*  
"Hello. Is there a seat available?"  

Them: **네, 몇 번 자리로 드릴까요?**  
*ne, myeot beon jariro deurilkkayo?*  
"Yes. Which seat number would you like?"  

You: **아무 자리나 괜찮아요.**  
*amu jarina gwaenchanayo.*  
"Any seat is fine."

| Situation | Phrase |
|---|---|
| No preference for seat | 아무 자리나 괜찮아요 (*amu jarina gwaenchanayo*) |
| Add thirty minutes | 삼십 분 충전해 주세요 (*samsip bun chungjeonhae juseyo*) |
| Mouse not working | 마우스 안 돼요 (*mauseu an dwaeyo*) |
| Ready to pay and go | 계산해 주세요 (*gyesanhae juseyo*) |

## What if your mouse or keyboard stops working?

PC bang staff swap peripherals fast — pointing at the item and saying **마우스 안 돼요** (*mauseu an dwaeyo*), "the mouse isn't working," or **키보드 이상해요** (*kibodeu isanghaeyo*), "the keyboard is acting up," brings a replacement in under a minute. Point at the item if you are not sure of the word.

## How do you order food at your PC bang station?

Most PC bangs have a menu on a tablet or a laminated card at the desk. Fried chicken, ramyeon, and tteokbokki are the staples. Press the call button and say the item name plus **주세요** (*juseyo*), "please." If the menu is entirely in Korean, **이거 주세요** (*igeo juseyo*), "this one, please," while pointing at the picture works every time. For drinks, **음료수도 같이 주세요** (*eumnyosudo gachi juseyo*) adds a beverage to your order.
