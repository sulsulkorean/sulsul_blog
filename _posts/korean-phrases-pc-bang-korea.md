Korean phrases at a **PC방** (*pisi-bang*, PC bang) start with Pattern 12 — verb plus **ㄹ/을 수 있어요?** (*l/eul su isseoyo?*), "Can I…?" Pattern 13 — **ㄹ/을 줄 알아요 / 몰라요** (*l/eul jul arayo / mollayo*), "know how to / don't know how to" — covers gaming: **게임할 줄 알아요** (*geimhal jul arayo*), "I know how to play games."

> - Ask to use a PC: **컴퓨터 사용할 수 있어요?** (*keompyuteo sayonghal su isseoyo?*), "Can I use a computer?"
> - Say you know a game: **게임할 줄 알아요** (*geimhal jul arayo*), "I know how to play games."
> - Extend your time: **시간 연장할 수 있어요?** (*sigan yeonjanghal su isseoyo?*), "Can I extend my time?"
> - Ask for headphones: **헤드셋 있어요?** (*hedeuset isseoyo?*), "Do you have headsets?"
> - Order a drink: **콜라 하나 주세요** (*kolla hana juseyo*), "One cola, please."

## How do you check in at a Korean PC bang?

**PC방** counters sit on almost every block in Seoul — neon signs, 24-hour hours, and gaming chairs behind the glass. Staff want a quick ID check, not a long English explanation.

You: **안녕하세요. 한 시간 쓸게요.** — *annyeonghaseyo. han sigan sseulgeyo.* — "Hello. I'll use one hour."  
Them: **신분증 있으세요?** — *sinbunjeung isseuseyo?* — "Do you have ID?"  
You: **네, 여기 있어요.** — *ne, yeogi isseoyo.* — "Yes, here it is."

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Can I use a computer? | 컴퓨터 사용할 수 있어요? (*keompyuteo sayonghal su isseoyo?*) |
| Know how to play | 게임할 줄 알아요 (*geimhal jul arayo*) |
| Extend time | 시간 연장할 수 있어요? (*sigan yeonjanghal su isseoyo?*) |
| Headsets available? | 헤드셋 있어요? (*hedeuset isseoyo?*) |
| One cola, please | 콜라 하나 주세요 (*kolla hana juseyo*) |

## What do you say when your session is running out?

Most PC bangs charge by the hour and beep when time is low. Walk to the counter and say **시간 연장할 수 있어요?** Pattern 44 — **해도 돼요?** (*haedo dwaeyo?*), "May I?" — fits when you want to switch seats.

You: **시간 연장할 수 있어요?** — *sigan yeonjanghal su isseoyo?* — "Can I extend my time?"  
Them: **네, 3,000원 더 내시면 돼요.** — *ne, samcheon won deo naesimyeon dwaeyo.* — "Yes, pay 3,000 won more."  
You: **네, 여기요. 감사합니다.** — *ne, yeogiyo. gamsahamnida.* — "Yes, here you go. Thank you."

For snacks without leaving your chair, Pattern 01 — **주세요** (*juseyo*) — works like at a [Korean convenience store](/korean-phrases-convenience-store): **라면 하나 주세요** (*ramyeon hana juseyo*).

## What usually goes wrong at a Korean PC bang?

Travelers often sit down before paying and hit a lock screen — check in at the desk first. A second mistake is whispering game names in English when staff ask what you play; a short **롤 할 줄 알아요** (*rol hal jul arayo*), "I know how to play LoL," or **게임할 줄 몰라요, 인터넷만 쓸게요** (*geimhal jul mollayo, inteonetman sseulgeyo*), "I don't know games, I'll just use the internet," clears the seat assignment fast.

You: **이 자리 비었어요?** — *i jari bieosseoyo?* — "Is this seat free?"  
Them: **네, 앉으세요.** — *ne, anjeuseyo.* — "Yes, go ahead and sit."  
You: **헤드셋 있어요?** — *hedeuset isseoyo?* — "Do you have headsets?"
