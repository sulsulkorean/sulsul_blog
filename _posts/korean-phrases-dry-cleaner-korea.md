Korean phrases at a dry cleaner come down to Pattern 03 from the SULSUL workbook — purpose plus **하고 싶어요** (*hago sipeoyo*), "I would like to…" Walk in with your coat and say **세탁하고 싶어요** (*setakhago sipeoyo*), "I'd like to dry-clean this," then ask **언제 되나요?** (*eonje doenayo?*), "When will it be ready?" before you leave for the day.

> - Request dry cleaning: **세탁하고 싶어요** (*setakhago sipeoyo*), "I'd like to dry-clean this."
> - Ask pickup time: **언제 되나요?** (*eonje doenayo?*), "When will it be ready?"
> - Need it today: **오늘 받을 수 있어요?** (*oneul badeul su isseoyo?*), "Can I pick it up today?"
> - Check the price: **얼마예요?** (*eolmayeyo?*), "How much is it?"
> - Get a receipt: **영수증 주세요** (*yeongsujeung juseyo*), "Receipt, please."

## How do you hand over clothes at the counter?

**세탁소** (*setakso*, dry cleaner) signs appear on side streets near hotels and apartment blocks. Staff tag each item and ask when you need it back — the opening line sets the whole visit.

You: **세탁하고 싶어요.** — *setakhago sipeoyo.* — "I'd like to dry-clean this."  
Them: **언제 필요하세요?** — *eonje piryohaseyo?* — "When do you need it?"  
You: **오늘 받을 수 있어요?** — *oneul badeul su isseoyo?* — "Can I pick it up today?"

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Dry-clean this | 세탁하고 싶어요 (*setakhago sipeoyo*) |
| When ready? | 언제 되나요? (*eonje doenayo?*) |
| Pick up today | 오늘 받을 수 있어요? (*oneul badeul su isseoyo?*) |
| How much? | 얼마예요? (*eolmayeyo?*) |
| Receipt | 영수증 주세요 (*yeongsujeung juseyo*) |

## What happens when you pick up?

Shops keep a paper ticket — **접수증** (*jeopsu jeung*, receipt slip) — with your phone number or a pickup code. Listen for **완료** (*wanryo*, finished) when your item is ready.

You: **얼마예요?** — *eolmayeyo?* — "How much is it?"  
Them: **12,000원이에요.** — *man icheon won-ieyo.* — "It is 12,000 won."  
You: **영수증 주세요.** — *yeongsujeung juseyo.* — "Receipt, please."

For self-service washing machines on a longer stay, [Korean phrases for a laundromat in Korea](/korean-phrases-laundromat-korea) covers the coin-machine version — **세탁소** (*setakso*) is for professional dry cleaning when you need a suit or coat pressed fast.

## What usually goes wrong at a dry cleaner?

Travelers often lose the pickup slip and cannot explain which item is theirs — **영수증 주세요** at drop-off gives you a number to show on return. A second mistake is assuming same-day service: **오늘 받을 수 있어요?** upfront prevents showing up at 6 p.m. when the shop closes at 7 with your jacket still on the rack.

You: **언제 되나요?** — *eonje doenayo?* — "When will it be ready?"  
Them: **내일 오후에요.** — *naeil ohu-eyo.* — "Tomorrow afternoon."  
You: **네, 알겠어요.** — *ne, algesseoyo.* — "Okay, got it."
