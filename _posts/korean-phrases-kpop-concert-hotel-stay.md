A K-pop concert weekend in Seoul often starts at the hotel desk after a late flight — **체크인** (*chekeuin*), check-in, is where you confirm your room and ask whether the show venue is walkable. **늦게 체크인해도 돼요?** (*neutge chekeuinhaedo dwaeyo?*), "Can I check in late?" saves a panicked call at midnight, and **공연장 어디예요?** (*gongyeonjang eodieyo?*), "Where is the concert venue?" is what you ask the front desk when maps still show the old stadium name.

> - Ask about late arrival: **늦게 체크인해도 돼요?** (*neutge chekeinhaedo dwaeyo?*), "Can I check in late?"
> - Confirm your booking: **예약 확인해 주세요** (*yeyak hwaginhae juseyo*), "Please confirm my reservation."
> - Find the venue: **공연장 어디예요?** (*gongyeonjang eodieyo?*), "Where is the concert venue?"
> - Meet friends in the lobby: **로비에서 만나요** (*robieseo mannayo*), "Let us meet in the lobby."
> - Ask about breakfast hours: **조식 몇 시예요?** (*josik myeot siyeyo?*), "What time is breakfast?"

## How do you check in when the concert runs late?

Hotels near major venues hear the same fan questions every comeback season — a few Korean lines get you to your room faster.

1. At the desk, hand over your passport and say **예약 확인해 주세요**.
2. If your flight lands after midnight, ask **늦게 체크인해도 돼요?** before you land.
3. Pin the venue on your phone, then ask **공연장 어디예요?** for walking directions.
4. Text your group **로비에서 만나요** so nobody waits at the wrong exit.
5. Before an early sound check, ask **조식 몇 시예요?** so you do not miss the buffet window.

| Moment | Say this |
|---|---|
| Confirm booking | 예약 확인해 주세요 (*yeyak hwaginhae juseyo*) |
| Late arrival | 늦게 체크인해도 돼요? (*neutge chekeinhaedo dwaeyo?*) |
| Find the venue | 공연장 어디예요? (*gongyeonjang eodieyo?*) |
| Lobby meetup | 로비에서 만나요 (*robieseo mannayo*) |
| Breakfast time | 조식 몇 시예요? (*josik myeot siyeyo?*) |

## What do Korean speakers say back?

Front-desk staff answer in clear, rehearsed lines — easy to catch even when you are tired from travel.

You: **예약 확인해 주세요.** — *yeyak hwaginhae juseyo.* — "Please confirm my reservation."  
Them: **네, 여기 키 카드예요.** — *ne, yeogi ki kadeuyeyo.* — "Yes, here is your key card."  
You: **공연장 어디예요?** — *gongyeonjang eodieyo?*

If a friend arrives first, **벌써 왔어요?** (*beolsseo wasseoyo?*), "You are already here?" opens the lobby reunion naturally. For ticket presale stress lines, see [Korean phrases for a K-pop world tour ticket presale](/korean-phrases-kpop-world-tour-ticket-presale).

## What usually goes wrong during a concert hotel stay?

Learners study **안녕하세요** (*annyeonghaseyo*) but never practice **예약 확인해 주세요**, the line every front desk expects first.

A second mistake is typing long English messages while your Korean friend asks the clerk a quick question — one venue question from you keeps the group moving. For taxi lines after the show, see [How to ask for a taxi in Korean](/ask-for-taxi-in-korean).
