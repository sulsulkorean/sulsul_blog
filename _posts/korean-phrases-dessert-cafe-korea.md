Korean phrases for ordering at a Korean dessert cafe come down to one line and one backup. Say **빙수 하나 주세요** (*bingsu hana juseyo*), "One bingsu, please," pick a topping style when the staff asks, then add **덜 달게 해주세요** (*deol dalge haejuseyo*), "Less sweet, please," if the menu looks heavy on syrup.

> - Order bingsu: **빙수 하나 주세요** (*bingsu hana juseyo*), "One bingsu, please."
> - Ask what's popular: **제일 인기 있는 거 뭐예요?** (*jeil ingi inneun geo mwoyeyo?*), "What is the most popular?"
> - Less sweet: **덜 달게 해주세요** (*deol dalge haejuseyo*), "Less sweet, please."
> - Eat here: **여기서 먹을게요** (*yeogiseo meogeulgeyo*), "I will eat here."

## How do you order at a Korean dessert cafe?

Korean dessert counters — bingsu bars, boba stands, patbingsu shops — all run on the same phrase frame. Say the item name, add **하나** (*hana*, one) or **두 개** (*du gae*, two), then close with **주세요** (*juseyo*, please). The pattern is [item] + number + 주세요.

You: **빙수 하나 주세요.** — *bingsu hana juseyo.* — "One bingsu, please."  
Them: **어떤 걸로 드릴까요? 딸기 아니면 팥?** — *eotteon geollo deurilkkayo? ttalgi animyeon pat?* — "Which kind? Strawberry or red bean?"  
You: **팥빙수 주세요.** — *patbingsu juseyo.* — "Red-bean bingsu, please."

| What to say | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Order bingsu | 빙수 하나 주세요 (*bingsu hana juseyo*) |
| Ask what's popular | 제일 인기 있는 거 뭐예요? (*jeil ingi inneun geo mwoyeyo?*) |
| Eat here | 여기서 먹을게요 (*yeogiseo meogeulgeyo*) |
| Ask for less sweet | 덜 달게 해주세요 (*deol dalge haejuseyo*) |
| Pay by card | 카드 돼요? (*kadeu dwaeyo?*) |

## What do you say if it is too sweet or you need a change?

Say **덜 달게 해주세요** before the staff starts pouring syrup. If you want toppings on the side, **따로 주세요** (*ttaro juseyo*) — "Please put it separately" — works for any item. For allergy questions, keep it short: **이거 우유 들어가요?** (*igeo uyu deureogayo?*), "Does this have milk?"

## How do you order to go or eat in at a Korean dessert cafe?

Say **여기서 먹을게요** if you are staying, or **테이크아웃이요** (*teikeuautiyo*), "To go," if you need it wrapped. At a boba counter, name the cup size before you close: **큰 거** (*keun geo*, large) or **작은 거** (*jageun geo*, small). If you are not sure about card payment, ask **카드 돼요?** (*kadeu dwaeyo?*), "Do you accept card?"

Most dessert cafes in Seoul, Busan, and Jeju take card without any fuss — but the phrase is handy at smaller street stalls. For ordering at a full Korean restaurant, the phrases in [ordering food at a Korean restaurant](/korean-phrases-ordering-restaurant-korea) pick up from here.
