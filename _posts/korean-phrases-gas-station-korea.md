Korean phrases at a gas station in Korea lean on Pattern 01 from the SULSUL workbook — noun plus **주세요** (*juseyo*), "please give me." Pull up to the pump and say **휘발유 주세요** (*hwibaryu juseyo*), "Regular gas, please," or **경유 주세요** (*gyeongyu juseyo*), "Diesel, please." Pattern 03 — **있어요?** (*isseoyo?*), "Is there…?" — covers self-service bays: **셀프 주유기 있어요?** (*selpeu juyugi isseoyo?*), "Is there a self-service pump?"

> - Regular gas: **휘발유 주세요** (*hwibaryu juseyo*), "Regular gas, please."
> - Diesel: **경유 주세요** (*gyeongyu juseyo*), "Diesel, please."
> - Self-service pump: **셀프 주유기 있어요?** (*selpeu juyugi isseoyo?*), "Is there a self-service pump?"
> - Fill amount: **만 원만 넣어 주세요** (*man wonman neoeo juseyo*), "Please put in only ten thousand won."
> - Card works: **카드 돼요?** (*kadeu dwaeyo?*), "Can I use a card?"

## How do you ask for the right fuel at a Korean gas station?

Most **주유소** (*juyuso*, gas station) chains in Korea — SK, GS Caltex, S-OIL — label pumps in Korean and English. If you rent a car, check whether it takes **휘발유** (*hwibaryu*, gasoline) or **경유** (*gyeongyu*, diesel) before you pull in.

You: **안녕하세요. 휘발유 주세요.** — *annyeonghaseyo. hwibaryu juseyo.* — "Hello. Regular gas, please."  
Them: **얼마나 넣을까요?** — *eolmana neoeulkkayo?* — "How much should I put in?"  
You: **만 원만 넣어 주세요.** — *man wonman neoeo juseyo.* — "Please put in only ten thousand won."

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Regular gas | 휘발유 주세요 (*hwibaryu juseyo*) |
| Diesel | 경유 주세요 (*gyeongyu juseyo*) |
| Self-service pump | 셀프 주유기 있어요? (*selpeu juyugi isseoyo?*) |
| Set an amount | 만 원만 넣어 주세요 (*man wonman neoeo juseyo*) |
| Card payment | 카드 돼요? (*kadeu dwaeyo?*) |

## What do you say at a self-service pump?

Many stations are **셀프** (*selpeu*, self-service). You pay inside first or tap your card at the machine, then fill. Pattern 44 from the workbook — **(V)아/어도 돼요?** (*a/eodo dwaeyo?*), "May I…?" — helps when you are unsure about the nozzle.

You: **셀프 주유기 있어요?** — *selpeu juyugi isseoyo?* — "Is there a self-service pump?"  
Them: **네, 저쪽이요.** — *ne, jeojjog-iyo.* — "Yes, over there."  
You: **카드 돼요?** — *kadeu dwaeyo?* — "Can I use a card?"

If the screen freezes before fuel flows, walk to the counter with **도와주세요** (*dowajuseyo*), "Please help me." The same **카드 돼요?** line from [withdrawing cash at an ATM in Korea](/korean-phrases-atm-korea) works when the pump rejects a foreign card.

## What usually goes wrong at a Korean gas station?

Travelers often choose **경유** (*gyeongyu*, diesel) by mistake because the green nozzle looks similar to gasoline in other countries — double-check the rental contract and say **휘발유 맞아요?** (*hwibaryu majayo?*), "This is regular gas, right?" before the attendant starts pumping.

A second mistake is assuming every pump takes a foreign card at the machine. If payment fails twice, go inside and ask **여기서 결제해도 돼요?** (*yeogiseo gyeoljehaedo dwaeyo?*), "Can I pay here?" so staff can run the card at the counter.
