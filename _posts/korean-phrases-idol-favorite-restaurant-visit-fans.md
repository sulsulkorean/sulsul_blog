A clip of an idol eating somewhere fills that restaurant for months, and the fans who show up all want the same two things: the seat and the dish. **여기가 그 자리예요?** (*yeogiga geu jariyeyo?*), "Is this the spot?" is how you find out without wandering, and **같은 걸로 주세요** (*gateun geollo juseyo*), "I'll have the same, please," is the order that makes the trip worth it.

> - Find the spot: **여기가 그 자리예요?** (*yeogiga geu jariyeyo?*), "Is this the spot?"
> - Ask to sit: **여기 앉아도 돼요?** (*yeogi anjado dwaeyo?*), "May I sit here?"
> - Find the dish: **그때 나온 메뉴가 뭐예요?** (*geuttae naon menyuga mwoyeyo?*), "Which dish was the one from the show?"
> - Order it: **같은 걸로 주세요** (*gateun geollo juseyo*), "I'll have the same, please."
> - Ask before the camera: **사진 찍어도 돼요?** (*sajin jjigeodo dwaeyo?*), "May I take a photo?"

## How do you visit without standing in the doorway?

Staff at these places have answered the same questions all week, so a direct question gets a warm answer much faster than hovering with a phone out.

1. **여기가 그 자리예요?** while you are still near the entrance — they will point, and that is the whole problem solved.
2. **여기 앉아도 돼요?** because the seat is sometimes reserved or simply taken.
3. **그때 나온 메뉴가 뭐예요?** gets you the real answer rather than the one guessed from a blurry screenshot.
4. **같은 걸로 주세요** is the shortest way to order it once you know.
5. **사진 찍어도 돼요?** before you lift the camera, every time.

## What do Korean speakers say back?

Owners of these restaurants are used to fans and usually answer before you finish the question.

You: **여기가 그 자리예요?** — *yeogiga geu jariyeyo?* — "Is this the spot?"  
Them: **네, 저기 창가 자리예요. 지금 비어 있어요** — *ne, jeogi changga jariyeyo. jigeum bieo isseoyo* — "Yes, the seat by the window. It's free right now."  
You: **여기 앉아도 돼요?** — *yeogi anjado dwaeyo?*

If the table is full, **기다릴게요** (*gidarilgeyo*), "I'll wait," is enough to get your turn held. For the outdoor version of the same trip, see [Korean phrases for a K-drama filming location tour in Seoul](/korean-phrases-kdrama-filming-location-tour-seoul).

## What usually goes wrong on a fan food trip?

The most common mistake is photographing first and asking after. Most owners say yes, but a few have signs up because of it, and **사진 찍어도 돼요?** takes two seconds.

The other slip is ordering from the picture instead of asking. Menus change, and the dish in the clip is often listed under a name nobody would guess, so **그때 나온 메뉴가 뭐예요?** saves you from going home with the wrong meal.
