A Korean snack goes viral roughly once a month — one short video, and by the weekend every convenience store shelf where it should sit is empty. The hunt has its own small vocabulary. **이거 신상이에요?** (*igeo sinsangieyo?*), "Is this the new one?" is what you ask holding up a package you are not sure about, and **품절이에요?** (*pumjeorieyo?*), "Is it sold out?" is the question that saves you from checking four more stores for nothing.

> - Check the new item: **이거 신상이에요?** (*igeo sinsangieyo?*), "Is this the new one?"
> - Ask where to find it: **그거 어디서 팔아요?** (*geugeo eodiseo parayo?*), "Where do they sell it?"
> - Confirm the shelf is empty: **품절이에요?** (*pumjeorieyo?*), "Is it sold out?"
> - Ask a friend about it: **맛 어때요?** (*mat eottaeyo?*), "How does it taste?"
> - Call the last one: **하나 남았어요** (*hana namasseoyo*), "There's one left."

## How do you actually find the snack everyone is posting about?

Staff restock at odd hours and the viral item rarely sits where the video showed it. Asking beats searching the aisle twice.

1. Hold up the package you found and ask **이거 신상이에요?** to confirm it is the one from the video.
2. If the shelf is bare, ask a staff member **품절이에요?** instead of assuming.
3. When they say yes, follow with **그거 어디서 팔아요?** — they often know which nearby branch still has stock.
4. Text a friend **맛 어때요?** before you buy four of them.
5. If you spot the last package behind another item, say **하나 남았어요** and take it.

| Moment | Say this |
|---|---|
| Confirm the new item | 이거 신상이에요? (*igeo sinsangieyo?*) |
| Empty shelf | 품절이에요? (*pumjeorieyo?*) |
| Find another store | 그거 어디서 팔아요? (*geugeo eodiseo parayo?*) |
| Ask about taste | 맛 어때요? (*mat eottaeyo?*) |
| Last one on the shelf | 하나 남았어요 (*hana namasseoyo*) |

## What do Korean speakers say back?

Convenience store staff answer in a few words while scanning someone else's items, so the reply comes fast.

You: **품절이에요?** — *pumjeorieyo?* — "Is it sold out?"  
Them: **네, 내일 들어와요** — *ne, naeil deureowayo* — "Yes, it comes in tomorrow."  
You: **그거 어디서 팔아요?** — *geugeo eodiseo parayo?*

If they point to another branch, **하나 남았어요** is the message you send your friend the moment you get there first. For everything else at the counter — heating, bags, bottle deposits — see [Korean phrases for a Korean convenience store](/korean-phrases-convenience-store).

## What usually goes wrong on a snack hunt?

Most learners silently circle the aisle rather than ask, then leave without knowing the item was behind the counter or arriving the next morning. One question answers what ten minutes of searching cannot.

The other habit is describing the snack in long English while the staff waits — showing the video and asking **이거 신상이에요?** gets you there in a second. For the same hunt outdoors during festival season, see [Korean phrases for summer street food in Korea](/korean-phrases-summer-street-food-korea).
