Forbes and Soompi covered the 2026 K-World Dream Awards on August 27 at KINTEX in Goyang, where **라이즈** (*Raijeu*, RIIZE) walked away with three trophies: Best Performance Award, Journalist Pick Artist Award, and an Artist Award. The moment the first trophy landed, every fan chat in Korea hit the same reaction phrase — **라이즈 세 개 받았어요!** (*Raijeu se gae badasseoyo!*, "RIIZE won three awards!") These are the Korean phrases that carried the entire night.

> - Three trophies: **라이즈 세 개 받았어요!** (*Raijeu se gae badasseoyo!*), "RIIZE won three awards!"
> - Best Performance: **퍼포먼스상 당연하죠** (*peopeomenseu-sang dangyeonhajyo*), "Of course they won Best Performance."
> - Great speech: **수상 소감 너무 좋았어요** (*susang sogam neomu joasseoyo*), "Their acceptance speech was so good."
> - Red carpet: **레드카펫 사진 봤어요?** (*redeu kaepet sajin bwasseoyo?*), "Did you see the red carpet photos?"
> - Next awards: **다음 시상식도 기대돼요** (*daeum sisangsikdo gidaedwaeyo*), "I'm already looking forward to the next one."

## What do fans say the moment RIIZE's name is called on stage?

The 10th K-World Dream Awards was a big night — ATEEZ led with four trophies, but RIIZE's three-peat was the moment that landed hardest in the fan community. Every fancam comment and group chat started with the same burst of Korean.

You: **시상식 갔다 왔어요?** — *sisangsik gatda wasseoyo?* — "Did you go to the awards ceremony?"  
Them: **아니요, 라이브로 봤어요. 라이즈 세 개 받았어요!** — *aniyo, raibeuro bwasseoyo. Raijeu se gae badasseoyo!* — "No, I watched the live stream. RIIZE won three awards!"  
You: **퍼포먼스상 당연하죠! 수상 소감 너무 좋았어요.** — *peopeomenseu-sang dangyeonhajyo! susang sogam neomu joasseoyo.* — "Of course they won Best Performance! Their speech was so good."

## What do you say about watching the ceremony from home?

Most fans outside Korea watch live streams from their room at odd hours. The phrase that starts that conversation is **라이브로 봤어요** (*raibeuro bwasseoyo*), "I watched it live (online)." When someone asks how long you stayed up, say **밤새 봤어요** (*bamsae bwasseoyo*), "I watched all night."

If a K-friend in Seoul was actually at KINTEX Goyang, ask **현장 분위기 어땠어요?** (*hyeonjang bunwigi eottaesseoyo?*), "What was the atmosphere like at the venue?" That single question opens an entire conversation about the stage, the crowd energy, and the outfits.

## What do fans say when RIIZE's acceptance speech drops?

After a big win like Best Performance, fan communities parse every word of the speech. Two useful phrases:

**오늘 너무 빛났어요** (*oneul neomu binnasseoyo*), "They shone so bright tonight," is the post-ceremony caption that dominates every fan edit. **다음 시상식도 기대돼요** (*daeum sisangsikdo gidaedwaeyo*), "I'm already looking forward to the next one," is the phrase that keeps the hype alive after the stream ends and the edit compilations start rolling in.

When someone shares a fancam of the acceptance moment, respond with **말이 필요 없어요** (*mari piryo eopseoyo*), "No words needed," and everyone in the chat will know exactly what you mean.
