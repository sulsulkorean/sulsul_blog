Korean phrases for a day at Hangang River Park in Seoul open with Pattern 11 from the SULSUL workbook — **빌릴 수 있어요?** (*billil su isseoyo?*), "Can I rent this?" At any **한강공원** (*Hangang gongwon*, Hangang River Park) rental booth you can request a **돗자리** (*dotjari*, picnic mat) or **자전거** (*jajeongeo*, bicycle) with that one pattern. Pair it with Pattern 03 — **있어요?** (*isseoyo?*), "Is there...?" — to track down food carts, bathrooms, and charging stations along the riverside path.

> - Rent a mat: **돗자리 빌릴 수 있어요?** (*dotjari billil su isseoyo?*), "Can I rent a picnic mat?"
> - Rent a bicycle: **자전거 빌릴 수 있어요?** (*jajeongeo billil su isseoyo?*), "Can I rent a bicycle?"
> - OK to sit here?: **여기 앉아도 돼요?** (*yeogi anjado dwaeyo?*), "Is it okay to sit here?"
> - Find the bathroom: **화장실 어디예요?** (*hwajangsil eodiyeyo?*), "Where is the bathroom?"
> - Order delivery: **치킨 시킬 수 있어요?** (*chikin sikil su isseoyo?*), "Can I order fried chicken delivered here?"

## How do you rent a mat or bicycle at Hangang?

Rental booths dot all major park zones — Yeouido, Banpo, and Ttukseom are the most tourist-friendly. Look for an orange stand near the river path.

You: **돗자리 빌릴 수 있어요?** — *dotjari billil su isseoyo?* — "Can I rent a picnic mat?"  
Them: **네, 이쪽으로 오세요.** — *ne, ijjoguro oseyo.* — "Yes, come this way."  
You: **자전거도 있어요?** — *jajeongeodo isseoyo?* — "Do you also have bicycles?"

## How do you order fried chicken delivery to the park?

Having **치킨** (*chikin*, fried chicken) delivered directly to a Hangang lawn is a Seoul rite of passage. Delivery apps list designated pickup spots at each zone. When the rider calls, one phrase keeps pickup smooth.

You: **저 지금 한강공원에 있어요.** — *jeo jigeum Hangang gongwone isseoyo.* — "I'm at Hangang Park right now."  
Them: **몇 번 출구 앞이에요?** — *myeot beon chulgu apieyo?* — "Which exit are you in front of?"  
You: **3번 출구 앞에 있어요.** — *sam beon chulgu apee isseoyo.* — "I'm in front of exit 3."

For ordering food on the go, **이거 주세요** (*igeo juseyo*), "I'll have this one," from [ordering at a Korean restaurant](/korean-phrases-ordering-restaurant-korea) works the same way at any riverfront food stand.

## What do you say when you need help finding a spot?

Weekend afternoons at Banpo or Yeouido fill up fast. Ask before you spread your mat.

You: **여기 자리 있어요?** — *yeogi jari isseoyo?* — "Is there space here?"  
Them: **네, 앉으세요.** — *ne, anjeuseyo.* — "Yes, please sit."  
You: **감사합니다.** — *gamsahamnida.* — "Thank you."

For bus directions from the city center to the riverside, **어디서 내려야 해요?** (*eodiseo naeryeoya haeyo?*), "Where should I get off?" from [riding a bus in Korean](/korean-phrases-riding-bus) gets you to the right stop.
