Korean phrases for renting a **자전거** (*jajeongeo*, bicycle) lean on Pattern 12 from the SULSUL workbook — verb plus **ㄹ/을 수 있어요?** (*l/eul su isseoyo?*), "Can I…?" Ask **자전거 빌릴 수 있어요?** (*jajeongeo billil su isseoyo?*), "Can I rent a bicycle?" at a **따릉이** (*Ttareungi*, Seoul public bike) kiosk or a riverside rental shop. Pattern 13 — verb plus **ㄹ/을 줄 알아요** (*l/eul jul arayo*), "I know how to" — covers skill checks: **자전거 탈 줄 알아요** (*jajeongeo tal jul arayo*), "I know how to ride a bike."

> - Ask to rent: **자전거 빌릴 수 있어요?** (*jajeongeo billil su isseoyo?*), "Can I rent a bicycle?"
> - Confirm skill: **자전거 탈 줄 알아요** (*jajeongeo tal jul arayo*), "I know how to ride a bike."
> - Ask where to return: **어디에 반납해요?** (*eodie bannaphaeyo?*), "Where do I return it?"
> - Helmet request: **헬멧 있어요?** (*helmet isseoyo?*), "Do you have a helmet?"
> - Parking check: **여기 주차해도 돼요?** (*yeogi juchahaedo dwaeyo?*), "Can I park here?"

## How do you rent a bike along the Han River?

**따릉이** stations and private rental counters sit near **한강** (*Hangang*, Han River) parks. Tap your transit card at the kiosk, or walk up to the counter and lead with Pattern 12 — can I rent — before you ask about helmet size or hourly rates.

You: **자전거 빌릴 수 있어요?** — *jajeongeo billil su isseoyo?* — "Can I rent a bicycle?"  
Them: **신분증 있어요?** — *sinbunjeung isseoyo?* — "Do you have ID?"  
You: **네, 여권 있어요.** — *ne, yeogwon isseoyo.* — "Yes, I have my passport."

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Can I rent a bike? | 자전거 빌릴 수 있어요? (*jajeongeo billil su isseoyo?*) |
| Know how to ride | 자전거 탈 줄 알아요 (*jajeongeo tal jul arayo*) |
| Cannot ride | 자전거 탈 줄 몰라요 (*jajeongeo tal jul mollayo*) |
| Where to return | 어디에 반납해요? (*eodie bannaphaeyo?*) |
| Can I park here? | 여기 주차해도 돼요? (*yeogi juchahaedo dwaeyo?*) |

## What if you are still learning to ride?

Pattern 13 also has a negative form — **자전거 탈 줄 몰라요** (*jajeongeo tal jul mollayo*), "I do not know how to ride." Staff may suggest a smaller frame. Pattern 07 — **못** (*mot*) — works too: **자전거 못 타요** (*jajeongeo mot tayo*), "I cannot ride a bike."

You: **자전거 탈 줄 몰라요.** — *jajeongeo tal jul mollayo.* — "I do not know how to ride."  
Them: **연습용 있어요.** — *yeonseub-yong isseoyo.* — "We have a practice bike."  
You: **얼마예요?** — *eolmayeyo?* — "How much is it?"

Pattern 44 — **해도 돼요?** (*haedo dwaeyo?*), "May I?" — fits return spots the same way as [Korean phrases for parking in Korea](/korean-phrases-parking-korea). Ask **여기 반납해도 돼요?** (*yeogi bannaphaedo dwaeyo?*), "Can I return it here?" before you lock the bike.

## What usually goes wrong when renting a bike in Seoul?

Travelers return a **따릉이** bike outside the designated zone and get an extra charge — ask **어디에 반납해요?** at the start, not after an hour of riding. A second mistake is skipping the helmet; **헬멧 있어요?** uses Pattern 03 and takes five seconds at the counter.

You: **몇 시간 빌릴 수 있어요?** — *myeot sigan billil su isseoyo?* — "How many hours can I rent it?"  
Them: **두 시간이에요.** — *du sigan-ieyo.* — "Two hours."  
You: **알겠어요, 감사합니다.** — *algesseoyo, gamsahamnida.* — "Got it, thank you."
