August tour season means **K-pop encore concert** announcements land in group chats while the official poster is still uploading — and the Korean fans type still works even if you read the news in English. **앵콘 공연 나왔어요?** (*aengkon gongyeon nawasseoyo?*), "Did the encore concert news come out?" opens the thread, and **티켓팅 언제예요?** (*tiketing eonjeyeyo?*), "When is the ticket sale?" is what everyone asks once the date is confirmed.

> - Ask if it dropped: **앵콘 공연 나왔어요?** (*aengkon gongyeon nawasseoyo?*), "Did the encore concert news come out?"
> - Check the sale time: **티켓팅 언제예요?** (*tiketing eonjeyeyo?*), "When is the ticket sale?"
> - Share the hype: **꼭 가고 싶어요** (*kkok gago sipeoyo*), "I really want to go."
> - React to the venue: **대박이에요** (*daebakieyo*), "That is amazing."
> - Plan with friends: **같이 가요** (*gati gayo*), "Let us go together."

## How do you react to an encore announcement without writing a whole essay?

Fan communities mix English artist names with Korean reactions — a few Korean lines signal you are in the room, not just lurking.

1. Drop the link and ask **앵콘 공연 나왔어요?** before the feed refreshes.
2. When the poster lands, send **대박이에요** — safe praise, no rumor theories.
3. Add **티켓팅 언제예요?** so friends know you are planning the sale window.
4. Type **꼭 가고 싶어요** when the venue is one you have been hoping for.
5. Close with **같이 가요** when you want to coordinate seats with a friend.

| Moment | Say this |
|---|---|
| Open the chat | 앵콘 공연 나왔어요? (*aengkon gongyeon nawasseoyo?*) |

| Quick reaction | 대박이에요 (*daebakieyo*) |
| Go together | 같이 가요 (*gati gayo*) |

## What do Korean speakers say back?

Replies in encore announcement threads stay loud and short.

You: **앵콘 공연 나왔어요?** — *aengkon gongyeon nawasseoyo?* — "Did the encore concert news come out?"  
Them: **응, 방금 봤어!** — *eung, banggeum bwasseo!* — "Yeah, I just saw it!"  
You: **티켓팅 언제예요?** — *tiketing eonjeyeyo?* — "When is the ticket sale?"

If someone shares the sale schedule, **고마워요** (*gomawoyo*) works with peers. For presale chat lines, see [Korean phrases for K-pop world tour ticket presale](/korean-phrases-kpop-world-tour-ticket-presale).

## What usually goes wrong during encore announcement week?

Learners memorize dramatic lines from subtitles but never practice the short question fans send when a second show date first lands.

A second mistake is typing long English paragraphs while friends switch to Korean for two seconds — one clear reaction keeps you in the conversation. For concert-day vocabulary, see [Korean phrases at a K-pop concert](/korean-phrases-kpop-concert).
