When aespa released "Switchblade" with Ty Dolla $ign on August 4, 2026, Korean fan group chats erupted within minutes. The two phrases that opened every thread were **대박이다** (*daebakida*), "That's incredible," and **완전 미쳤다** (*wanjeon michyeotda*), "Completely insane — in the best way." These are not slang invented for the moment; they are the core reaction phrases Korean K-pop fans reach for every time a big release lands.

> - Pure amazement: **대박이다** (*daebakida*) — "That's incredible / jackpot!"
> - Blown away: **완전 미쳤다** (*wanjeon michyeotda*) — "Totally insane (in the best way)."
> - Loving the song: **이 노래 너무 좋아** (*i norae neomu joa*) — "I love this song so much."
> - Ask if they watched: **뮤비 봤어?** (*myubi bwasseo?*) — "Did you watch the music video?"

## What do Korean fans actually say when a K-pop drop hits?

The moment a music video goes live, group chats move faster than you can type. These are the phrases that show up in the first five minutes:

**대박이다** (*daebakida*) — "Incredible." Short, emphatic, and works for any drop — album, teaser, live stage, or collab.

**미쳤다** (*michyeotda*) — "Crazy." Upgrade it with **완전** (*wanjeon*, "totally") when the reaction is stronger: 완전 미쳤다.

**역대급이야** (*yeokdaegeubiyo*) — "All-time best." Save this one for the releases that genuinely feel historic.

When your friend has not seen the video yet:

**빨리 봐** (*ppalli bwa*) — "Watch it now / hurry up and watch."

## How do you keep the conversation going after the first reaction?

After the initial burst, the chat settles into specifics. These phrases move it forward:

| Line | Say it when |
|---|---|
| 몇 번 봤어? (*myeot beon bwasseo?*) "How many times did you watch?" | Checking if your friend is as obsessed as you are |
| 어느 부분이 제일 좋아? (*eoneu bubuni jeeil joa?*) "Which part did you like best?" | Starting a real breakdown of the song |
| 협업 진짜 잘 됐다 (*hyeopeop jinjja jal dwaetda*) "The collab really worked out." | Giving your verdict on the aespa x Ty Dolla $ign pairing |
| 다시 들어봐 (*dasi deureobwa*) "Listen again." | Sending someone back to the chorus |

Them: **아직 못 봤어. 지금 보고 있어.**

— "Haven't seen it yet. Watching right now."

— *daebakida, matji? yeokdaegeubiyo.*  
— "Incredible, right? All-time best."
