Korean phrases for a jjimdak restaurant start with Pattern 12 from the SULSUL workbook — **주세요** (*juseyo*), "Please give me…" The essential opener at any **찜닭** (*jjimdak*, braised spicy chicken) restaurant is **찜닭 한 마리 주세요** (*jjimdak han mari juseyo*), "One jjimdak, please." Pattern 38 — **돼요?** (*dwaeyo?*), "Is it possible?" — handles spice: **맵기 조절 돼요?** (*maepgi jojyeol dwaeyo?*), "Can you adjust the spice level?" These two patterns handle ninety percent of what happens from the moment you sit down.

> - Order the pot: **찜닭 한 마리 주세요** (*jjimdak han mari juseyo*), "One jjimdak, please."
> - Spice level: **맵기 조절 돼요?** (*maepgi jojyeol dwaeyo?*), "Can you adjust the spice level?"
> - Ask what is popular: **뭐가 인기 있어요?** (*mwoga ingi isseoyo?*), "What is popular here?"
> - Boneless option: **뼈 없는 걸로 주세요** (*ppyeo eomneun geollo juseyo*), "Boneless version, please."
> - Extra rice: **밥 추가 주세요** (*bap chuga juseyo*), "Extra rice, please."

## How do you place your first order at a jjimdak spot?

찜닭 restaurants — from Andong-origin spots to Seoul chain counters — display spice levels on a wall board or plastic menu. Use Pattern 12 the moment the server arrives.

You: **찜닭 한 마리 주세요. 맵기 조절 돼요?** — *jjimdak han mari juseyo. maepgi jojyeol dwaeyo?* — "One jjimdak, please. Can you adjust the spice?"  
Them: **안 맵게, 중간, 많이 맵게 있어요.** — *an maepge, junggan, manhi maepge isseoyo.* — "We have mild, medium, or very spicy."  
You: **중간 맵기로 해 주세요.** — *junggan maepgiro hae juseyo.* — "Medium spicy, please."

## What do you say when the glass noodles disappear mid-pot?

The broad glass noodles — **당면** (*dangmyeon*) — soak up sauce fast and usually run out before the chicken does. Ask for more with **당면 추가 주세요** (*dangmyeon chuga juseyo*), "More glass noodles, please." If the sauce thickens too much, say **육수 조금 더 주세요** (*yuksu jogeum deo juseyo*), "A little more broth, please."

Many diners stir **고추장** (*gochujang*, red pepper paste) into the last spoonful of broth and eat it with plain rice. Ask for the paste at the table with **고추장 있어요?** (*gochujang isseoyo?*), "Do you have gochujang?"

## What do you say when the dish is too spicy mid-meal?

Step one is **너무 매워요** (*neomu maewoyo*), "It's too spicy." Most jjimdak spots keep small dairy cartons nearby — ask **우유 있어요?** (*uyu isseoyo?*), "Do you have milk?" A side of steamed egg, **계란찜** (*gyeran jjim*), cuts the heat fast; order it with **계란찜 주세요** (*gyeran jjim juseyo*), "Steamed egg, please."

On your way out, say **너무 맛있었어요** (*neomu massisseoyo*), "It was so delicious." Every cook at every 찜닭 counter remembers those words.
