Korean phrases at a **방탈출 카페** (*bangtaltchul kape*, escape room cafe) matter most at two moments: when staff explain the rules before the clock starts, and when you need a hint mid-game. Pattern 26 from the SULSUL workbook — noun plus **주세요** (*juseyo*), "Please give me" — handles both. Say **힌트 주세요** (*hinteu juseyo*), "Please give me a hint," into the microphone and the game master responds.

> - Reservation check-in: **예약했어요** (*yeyakhaesseoyo*), "I have a reservation."
> - Ask for a hint: **힌트 주세요** (*hinteu juseyo*), "Please give me a hint."
> - Don't understand: **다시 설명해 주세요** (*dasi seolmyeonghae juseyo*), "Please explain again."
> - Mid-game pause: **잠깐요, 모르겠어요** (*jamkkanyo, moreugesseoyo*), "Hold on, I don't understand."
> - Celebrate escape: **탈출했어요!** (*talchulhaesseoyo!*), "We escaped!"

## How do you book and check in at a Korean escape room?

Most **방탈출 카페** require a reservation. Book online in advance — the site is usually in Korean, so Pattern 12, **ㄹ/을 수 있어요?** (*l/eul su isseoyo?*), "Can I?", covers any follow-up phone call. At check-in, give your name and the number of players.

You: **예약했어요. 박유나예요.** — *yeyakhaesseoyo. Bak Yuna-yeyo.* — "I have a reservation — it's Park Yuna."  
Them: **몇 명이에요?** — *myeot myeong-ieyo?* — "How many people?"  
You: **세 명이에요.** — *se myeong-ieyo.* — "Three people."

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 예약했어요 (*yeyakhaesseoyo*) | I have a reservation |
| 힌트 주세요 (*hinteu juseyo*) | Please give me a hint |
| 다시 설명해 주세요 (*dasi seolmyeonghae juseyo*) | Please explain again |
| 탈출했어요! (*talchulhaesseoyo!*) | We escaped! |
| 다음에 또 올게요 (*da-eume tto olgeyo*) | I'll come again next time |

## What do you say during the game?

The game master watches via camera and speaks through a speaker. If you miss the clue, call out **힌트 주세요** toward the microphone — most rooms have one on the wall or ceiling. For a rule you already forgot, **다시 설명해 주세요** (*dasi seolmyeonghae juseyo*) uses Pattern 15, "please do [it] again."

You: **이 자물쇠 어떻게 열어요?** — *i jamuljoe eotteoke yeoreoyo?* — "How do I open this lock?"  
Them: **힌트를 드릴게요. 숫자 세 개예요.** — *hinteureul deurilgeyo. sutja se gaeyo.* — "I'll give you a hint — it's three digits."  
You: **감사합니다! 해볼게요.** — *gamsahamnida! haebollgeyo.* — "Thank you! We'll try."

If time runs out before you escape, staff will open the door and debrief in Korean — **시간 초과예요** (*sigan chogwayeyo*), "Time's up," is what you will hear.

## What do you say when you escape — or when you don't?

Victory: **탈출했어요!** is enough — the whole room hears it. Staff will come in to take a group photo. Ask **사진 찍어도 돼요?** (*sajin jjigeodo dwaeyo?*), "May I take a photo (of the set)?", before touching any props.

You: **탈출했어요!** — *talchulhaesseoyo!* — "We escaped!"  
Them: **축하해요! 기록 세웠어요.** — *chukahaeyo! girok sewosseoyo.* — "Congratulations! You set a record."  
You: **다음에 또 올게요.** — *da-eume tto olgeyo.* — "We'll come again next time."

If you don't escape, **다음에 또 올게요** still works — it is the most common exit line and staff will smile.
