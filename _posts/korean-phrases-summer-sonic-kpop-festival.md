**Summer Sonic 2026** is filling Pacific and Massive stages with K-pop acts this week — and when **Xdinary Heroes** stepped up as a five-member band after a sudden lineup change, Korean fan chats lit up with the same short lines you hear at any summer festival set. **공연 시작했어요** (*gongyeon sijakhaesseoyo*), "The show started," is what someone yells when the lights drop, and **응원해요** (*eungwonhaeyo*), "I am cheering for you," is the safe chant when you want to match the crowd without memorizing a fanchant.

> - Flag the start: **공연 시작했어요** (*gongyeon sijakhaesseoyo*), "The show started."
> - Cheer the group: **응원해요** (*eungwonhaeyo*), "I am cheering for you."
> - React to the encore: **한 곡 더요** (*han gok deoyo*), "One more song, please."
> - Ask where friends are: **어디 있어요?** (*eodi isseoyo?*), "Where are you?"
> - Share the hype: **대박이에요** (*daebakieyo*), "That is amazing."

## How do you talk about a festival set without blocking the view?

Outdoor stages move fast — fans mix English artist names with Korean reactions, and one clear line keeps you in the group chat while the bass hits.

1. When the intro video ends, send **공연 시작했어요** so late friends sprint from the food stalls.
2. During the chorus, type **응원해요** — short, loud, no lyric spoilers.
3. If the crowd screams for an encore, join with **한 곡 더요**.
4. Lost your group? Text **어디 있어요?** with a pin drop.
5. After the final bow, close with **대박이에요** before anyone switches to the next stage.

| Moment | Say this |
|---|---|
| Lights drop | 공연 시작했어요 (*gongyeon sijakhaesseoyo*) |
| Mid-set cheer | 응원해요 (*eungwonhaeyo*) |
| Encore chant | 한 곡 더요 (*han gok deoyo*) |
| Find friends | 어디 있어요? (*eodi isseoyo?*) |
| Post-set reaction | 대박이에요 (*daebakieyo*) |

## What do Korean speakers say back in a festival group chat?

Replies stay quick because the next act starts in twenty minutes.

You: **공연 시작했어요?** — *gongyeon sijakhaesseoyo?* — "Did the show start?"  
Them: **응, 방금 시작했어!** — *eung, banggeum sijakhaesseo!* — "Yeah, it just started!"  
You: **응원해요.** — *eungwonhaeyo.* — "Cheering for them."

If a friend sends a fancam clip from the barrier, **고마워요** (*gomawoyo*) works with peers; save **감사합니다** (*gamsahamnida*) for staff helping you find an exit after the set.

## What usually goes wrong at a multi-stage festival?

Learners memorize dramatic concert lines from subtitles but never practice the two-second status update friends actually send between stages.
