To exchange contact info with a Korean friend, open with **카카오톡 있어요?** (*KakaoTok isseoyo?*), "Are you on KakaoTalk?" If yes, ask **카카오톡 ID 알려 주세요** (*KakaoTok ID allyeo juseyo*), "Please tell me your KakaoTalk ID." Pattern 15 in the SULSUL workbook — **전화번호가 뭐예요?** (*jeonhwabeonhoga mwoyeyo?*) — works when someone prefers a phone number instead.

> - Check for KakaoTalk: **카카오톡 있어요?** (*KakaoTok isseoyo?*), "Are you on KakaoTalk?"
> - Ask for their ID: **카카오톡 ID 알려 주세요** (*KakaoTok ID allyeo juseyo*), "Please tell me your KakaoTalk ID."
> - Ask for a phone number: **전화번호가 뭐예요?** (*jeonhwabeonhoga mwoyeyo?*), "What is your phone number?"
> - Offer yours first: **제 번호 적어 주세요** (*je beonho jeogeo juseyo*), "Please write down my number."

## How do you ask without making it awkward?

Koreans rarely exchange bare phone numbers on a first meeting unless you already clicked. The KakaoTalk opener from the blockquote above is the standard move — it signals you want to stay in touch without sounding too formal. If they say yes, open KakaoTalk, tap Add Friend, and say **ID 알려 주세요** (*ID allyeo juseyo*), "Please tell me your ID."

When someone asks your name first, answer and bounce back with Pattern 16: **저는 미국 사람이에요. 지민 씨는요?** (*jeoneun miguk saramieyo. Jimin ssineunyo?*), "I am American. How about you, Jimin?" Swap in your country and their name. That keeps the conversation moving toward contact info naturally.

## What does the exchange sound like in real life?

You: **카카오톡 있어요?** — *KakaoTok isseoyo?* — "Are you on KakaoTalk?"  
Them: **네, 있어요!** — *ne, isseoyo!* — "Yes, I am!"  
You: **ID 알려 주시겠어요?** — *ID allyeo jusigesseoyo?* — "Could you tell me your ID?"

If they prefer a phone number, they might say **번호 알려 드릴게요** (*beonho allyeo deurilgeyo*), "I will give you my number." Have your phone ready — asking **천천히 말해 주세요** (*cheoncheonhi malhae juseyo*), "Please speak slowly," is fine when you are typing digits.

| Step | Say this |
|---|---|
| Open the ask | 카카오톡 있어요? (*KakaoTok isseoyo?*) |
| Get their ID | 카카오톡 ID 알려 주세요 (*KakaoTok ID allyeo juseyo*) |
| Ask phone instead | 전화번호가 뭐예요? (*jeonhwabeonhoga mwoyeyo?*) |
| Confirm you saved it | 저장했어요 (*jeojanghaesseoyo*) |
| Follow up later | 연락할게요 (*yeollakhalgeyo*) |

## What usually goes wrong?

The most common mistake is waiting until you are already walking away. Ask while you are still standing together — phones out, KakaoTalk open, ready to tap Add Friend.

A second slip is mixing up **알려 주세요** (*allyeo juseyo*), "please tell me," with **적어 주세요** (*jeogeo juseyo*), "please write it down." If your Korean is still slow, offer your phone and say **여기에 써 주세요** (*yeogie sseo juseyo*), "Please write it here." Survival Mission 7 in the SULSUL workbook walks through this full role-play.
