Slogans, photocard binders, birthday goods — most fan-made merch never sits in a shop. One fan opens a **공구** (*gonggu*), short for 공동구매 (*gongdonggumae*), a group order: they collect everyone's form, everyone's payment, and send the whole batch out at once. The fan running it is the **총대** (*chongdae*). Getting in is two questions long — **공구 아직 열려 있어요?** (*gonggu ajik yeollyeo isseoyo?*), "Is the group order still open?" and **해외 배송도 되나요?** (*haeoe baesongdo doenayo?*), "Does it ship overseas too?"

> - Check it is open: **공구 아직 열려 있어요?** (*gonggu ajik yeollyeo isseoyo?*), "Is the group order still open?"
> - Ask about shipping: **해외 배송도 되나요?** (*haeoe baesongdo doenayo?*), "Does it ship overseas too?"
> - Ask the deadline: **입금 언제까지예요?** (*ipgeum eonjekkajiyeyo?*), "When is the payment due?"
> - Order more than one: **두 개 신청해도 돼요?** (*du gae sincheonghaedo dwaeyo?*), "Can I sign up for two?"
> - Close the loop: **입금했어요, 확인 부탁드려요** (*ipgeumhaesseoyo, hwagin butakdeuryeoyo*), "I've sent the payment, please confirm."

## How do you get your name onto the list?

A 공구 is one person doing admin for two hundred strangers, so short questions get answered and long ones get scrolled past.

1. **공구 아직 열려 있어요?** before anything else, because the form usually closes days before the merch is made.
2. **해외 배송도 되나요?** next, since some 총대 ship only inside Korea and it is better to know in the first minute.
3. **두 개 신청해도 돼요?** if you are picking one up for a friend, asked before you fill the form rather than after.
4. **입금 언제까지예요?** so your slot does not get released to whoever is on the waiting list.
5. **입금했어요, 확인 부탁드려요** once the transfer is out, which is what actually moves you onto the 명단 (*myeongdan*), the confirmed list.

## What do Korean speakers say back?

The replies are clipped, because the 총대 is answering the same thing forty times that night.

You: **공구 아직 열려 있어요?** — *gonggu ajik yeollyeo isseoyo?* — "Is the group order still open?"  
Them: **네, 아직 열려 있어요. 오늘 밤에 마감이에요** — *ne, ajik yeollyeo isseoyo. oneul bame magamieyo* — "Yes, it's still open. It closes tonight."  
You: **해외 배송도 되나요?** — *haeoe baesongdo doenayo?* — "Does it ship overseas too?"  
Them: **해외는 배송비가 따로 있어요. 폼에 주소 영어로 적어 주세요** — *haeoeneun baesongbiga ttaro isseoyo. pome juso yeongeoro jeogeo juseyo* — "Overseas shipping is charged separately. Write your address in English on the form."  
You: **입금했어요, 확인 부탁드려요** — *ipgeumhaesseoyo, hwagin butakdeuryeoyo* — "I've sent the payment, please confirm."  
Them: **입금 확인했어요. 명단에 올려 드릴게요** — *ipgeum hwaginhaesseoyo. myeongdane ollyeo deurilgeyo* — "Payment confirmed. I'll add you to the list."

That last line is the one you are waiting for. For the other way fandom merch changes hands, see [Korean phrases for trading K-pop photocards](/korean-phrases-kpop-photocard-trade-fans).

## What usually goes wrong in a group order?

The most common one is going quiet after sending money. The 총대 matches transfers by 입금자명 (*ipgeumjamyeong*), the depositor name on the transfer, and an overseas payment often arrives under a name that looks nothing like the one on your form. **입금했어요, 확인 부탁드려요** with your form name next to it saves both of you a night of searching.

The other is asking about shipping last. Once you are on the list and the batch is packed, **해외 배송도 되나요?** is too late to be a useful question — ask it while the form is still open, along with **입금 언제까지예요?**, and the rest is just waiting for a parcel.
