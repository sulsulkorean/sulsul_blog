Korean phrases for finding the checkout counter combine a location question and a payment check. Say **계산대 어디예요?** (*gyesandae eodiyeyo?*), "Where is the checkout counter?" when you are holding items and cannot spot the register, then **카드 돼요?** (*kadeu dwaeyo?*), "Can I pay by card?" before you tap. Pattern 04 in the SULSUL workbook — place noun plus **어디예요?** (*eodiyeyo?*) — is the compass line, and Pattern 03 — **돼요?** (*dwaeyo?*) after a noun — handles the card question.

> - **계산대 어디예요?** (*gyesandae eodiyeyo?*) — "Where is the checkout counter?"
> - **카드 돼요?** (*kadeu dwaeyo?*) — "Can I pay by card?"
> - **현금 없어요** (*hyeongeum eopseoyo*) — "I do not have cash."
> - **영수증 주세요** (*yeongsujeung juseyo*) — "Receipt, please."

## How do you ask when you are ready to pay?

Large stores in Korea often hide the register behind a pillar or on another floor. Walk toward an employee with your basket visible — they will point before you finish the sentence.

You: **실례합니다, 계산대 어디예요?** — *sillyehamnida, gyesandae eodiyeyo?* — "Excuse me, where is the checkout counter?"  
Them: **저쪽이에요.** — *jeojjogiyeyo.* — "It is over there."  
You: **감사합니다.** — *gamsahamnida.* — "Thank you."

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Where is checkout? | 계산대 어디예요? (*gyesandae eodiyeyo?*) |
| Card accepted? | 카드 돼요? (*kadeu dwaeyo?*) |
| No cash on you | 현금 없어요 (*hyeongeum eopseoyo*) |
| Need a receipt | 영수증 주세요 (*yeongsujeung juseyo*) |
| How much total? | 얼마예요? (*eolmayeyo?*) |

## What does the cashier say at the register?

Listen for **카드** (*kadeu*, card), **현금** (*hyeongeum*, cash), or **영수증** (*yeongsujeung*, receipt). You only need to catch the keyword and answer **네** (*ne*) or **아니요** (*aniyo*).

You: **카드 돼요?** — *kadeu dwaeyo?* — "Can I pay by card?"  
Them: **네, 카드 되세요.** — *ne, kadeu doeseyo.* — "Yes, card is fine."  
You: **여기서 결제할게요.** — *yeogiseo gyeoljehalgeyo.* — "I will pay here."

At open-air markets, some stalls are cash-only. If you hear **현금만** (*hyeongeum-man*, cash only), say **현금 없어요** (*hyeongeum eopseoyo*) and ask **근처 ATM 어디예요?** (*geuncheo ATM eodiyeyo?*), "Where is the nearest ATM?" ## What usually goes wrong?

Travelers often queue at an information desk thinking it is checkout. Look for **계산** (*gyesan*, payment) on hanging signs — that marks the register, not customer service. Another mistake is tapping your card before the total appears on the screen; wait until the cashier says **카드 대세요** (*kadeu daseyo*), "Please insert or tap your card."

You: **얼마예요?** — *eolmayeyo?* — "How much is it?"  
Them: **12,000원이에요.** — *man du cheon won-ieyo.* — "It is 12,000 won."  
You: **영수증 주세요.** — *yeongsujeung juseyo.* — "Receipt, please."

A second slip is walking away without **영수증** (*yeongsujeung*) when you might need it for a tax refund later. One extra line at the counter costs nothing and saves a trip back.
