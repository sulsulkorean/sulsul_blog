Korean phrases at a glasses shop pair Pattern 01 — noun plus **주세요** (*juseyo*), "please give me" — with Pattern 44 — verb plus **해도 돼요?** (*haedo dwaeyo?*), "may I?" At an **안경점** (*angyeongjeom*, glasses shop), ask **안경 있어요?** (*angyeong isseoyo?*), "Do you have glasses?" when you need a new pair fast, then **시력 검사해 주세요** (*siryeok geomsa hae juseyo*), "Please check my eyesight," before you pick frames.

> - Ask about stock: **안경 있어요?** (*angyeong isseoyo?*), "Do you have glasses?"
> - Request an eye test: **시력 검사해 주세요** (*siryeok geomsa hae juseyo*), "Please check my eyesight."
> - Ask about thin lenses: **렌즈 얇은 거 있어요?** (*renjeu yabeun geo isseoyo?*), "Do you have thin lenses?"
> - Check the price: **이거 얼마예요?** (*igeo eolmayeyo?*), "How much is this?"
> - Pay by card: **카드 돼요?** (*kadeu dwaeyo?*), "Do you accept card?"

## How do you start at the counter?

Glasses shops in Seoul often sit inside malls or on busy shopping streets — staff expect you to browse frames first, then ask for a test. Use Pattern 02 from the SULSUL workbook — **있어요?** (*isseoyo?*), "Do you have…?" — with the item name.

You: **안경 있어요?** — *angyeong isseoyo?* — "Do you have glasses?"  
Them: **네, 이쪽으로 오세요.** — *ne, ijjogeuro oseyo.* — "Yes, please come this way."  
You: **시력 검사해 주세요.** — *siryeok geomsa hae juseyo.* — "Please check my eyesight."

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Do you have glasses? | 안경 있어요? (*angyeong isseoyo?*) |
| Eye test, please | 시력 검사해 주세요 (*siryeok geomsa hae juseyo*) |
| Thin lenses | 렌즈 얇은 거 있어요? (*renjeu yabeun geo isseoyo?*) |
| How much is this? | 이거 얼마예요? (*igeo eolmayeyo?*) |
| Card payment | 카드 돼요? (*kadeu dwaeyo?*) |

## What happens after the eye test?

The optician reads your prescription — **도수** (*dosu*, prescription strength) — and asks whether you want contact lenses or frames. Listen for **렌즈** (*renjeu*, lens) and **테** (*te*, frame).

You: **렌즈 얇은 거 있어요?** — *renjeu yabeun geo isseoyo?* — "Do you have thin lenses?"  
Them: **네, 추가 비용 있어요.** — *ne, chuga biyong isseoyo.* — "Yes, there is an extra charge."  
You: **이거 얼마예요?** — *igeo eolmayeyo?* — "How much is this?"

Travelers often point at a frame and say **this one** in English. **이거 주세요** (*igeo juseyo*), "This one, please," from [buying clothes in Korea](/buying-clothes-in-korea) uses the same pointing pattern — hold the frame and request it clearly.

## What usually goes wrong at a glasses shop?

A common mistake is skipping the eye test and guessing your prescription from an old pair at home — staff can adjust, but **시력 검사해 주세요** saves you from headaches later. A second mistake is forgetting to ask **카드 돼요?** before you pick premium lenses; most mall shops take card, but small street shops sometimes prefer cash.

You: **카드 돼요?** — *kadeu dwaeyo?* — "Do you accept card?"  
Them: **네, 됩니다.** — *ne, doemnida.* — "Yes, we do."  
You: **영수증 주세요.** — *yeongsujeung juseyo.* — "Receipt, please."
