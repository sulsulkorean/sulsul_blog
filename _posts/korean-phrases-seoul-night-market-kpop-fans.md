August 2026 comeback season means **Seoul night markets** fill with K-pop fans still buzzing from a music show — tteokbokki steam, skewer smoke, and a group chat that will not stop replaying the encore. **분식 뭐가 맛있어요?** (*bunsik mwoga masisseoyo?*), "What street food is good here?" is what you ask the friend who lives near the stall row, and **같이 먹을래요?** (*gati meogeullaeyo?*), "Want to eat together?" keeps the table from splitting when everyone is starving after the venue.

> - Ask what to order: **분식 뭐가 맛있어요?** (*bunsik mwoga masisseoyo?*), "What street food is good here?"
> - Invite someone to share: **같이 먹을래요?** (*gati meogeullaeyo?*), "Want to eat together?"
> - Order one portion: **하나 주세요** (*hana juseyo*), "One please."
> - Say it looks good: **맛있어 보여요** (*masisseo boyeoyo*), "It looks delicious."
> - Split the bill casually: **더치페이 할까요?** (*deochipei halkkayo?*), "Should we go Dutch?"

## How do you order at a night market without holding up the line?

Fan groups mix English artist names with short Korean at the counter — a few clear lines keep you moving when the tteokbokki pot is boiling over.

1. Ask a local friend **분식 뭐가 맛있어요?** before you reach the front.
2. When two people want the same skewer tray, offer **같이 먹을래요?**
3. Point at the menu photo and say **하나 주세요** — staff hear it every night.
4. While the food sizzles, send **맛있어 보여요** to the group chat with a quick photo.
5. Before anyone pulls out a card, float **더치페이 할까요?** so paying stays casual.

| Moment | Say this |
|---|---|
| Pick a stall | 분식 뭐가 맛있어요? (*bunsik mwoga masisseoyo?*) |
| Share a tray | 같이 먹을래요? (*gati meogeullaeyo?*) |
| Order one item | 하나 주세요 (*hana juseyo*) |
| React to the food | 맛있어 보여요 (*masisseo boyeoyo*) |
| Split the bill | 더치페이 할까요? (*deochipei halkkayo?*) |

## What do Korean speakers say back?

Replies at night market tables stay loud, warm, and fast.

You: **분식 뭐가 맛있어요?** — *bunsik mwoga masisseoyo?* — "What street food is good here?"  
Them: **떡볶이 여기 유명해!** — *tteokbokki yeogi yumyeonghae!* — "The tteokbokki here is famous!"  
You: **같이 먹을래요?** — *gati meogeullaeyo?*

If someone buys an extra drink for the table, **고마워요** (*gomawoyo*) works with peers. For chi-maek night lines after the market, see [Korean phrases for chi-maek night with friends](/korean-phrases-chimaek-night-friends).

## What usually goes wrong after a music show?

Learners memorize dramatic drama lines but never practice the short food question fans actually use when everyone is hungry.

A second mistake is staying silent while the group switches to Korean for ten seconds at the stall — one clear order keeps you at the table instead of waiting on the sidewalk.
