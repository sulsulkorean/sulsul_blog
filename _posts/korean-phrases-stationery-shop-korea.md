Korean stationery shops — Artbox, Kyobo Hottracks, the stationery floor at most 대형마트 (*daehyeong mateu*) — are laid out for browsing, but staff are easy to approach when you need to find something specific or check a detail before buying. **이거 있어요?** (*igeo isseoyo?*), "Do you have this?" and **다른 색 있어요?** (*dareun saek isseoyo?*), "Do you have it in another color?" cover the two questions you will ask most.

> - Find a specific item: **이거 있어요?** (*igeo isseoyo?*), "Do you have this?"
> - Ask about colors: **다른 색 있어요?** (*dareun saek isseoyo?*), "Is there another color?"
> - Buy just one: **낱개로 살 수 있어요?** (*natgaero sal su isseoyo?*), "Can I buy just one?"
> - Get it gift-wrapped: **포장해 주세요** (*pojanghae juseyo*), "Please wrap this."
> - Ask the price: **이거 얼마예요?** (*igeo eolmayeyo?*), "How much is this?"

## How do you find what you are looking for?

Korean stationery shops group items by type — notebooks together, pens together, washi tape in its own wall. If you cannot spot something, hold up a photo on your phone or describe it simply:

**이런 거 있어요?** — *ireon geo isseoyo?* — "Do you have something like this?"

Staff will point you to the right aisle or say **없어요** (*eopseoyo*) — "We don't have it" — so you can move on.

If you find the item but want a different option, these two phrases open the conversation:

| What you want to check | What to say |
|---|---|
| Another color (*dareun saek*) | **다른 색 있어요?** (*dareun saek isseoyo?*) |
| A different design (*dareun dijain*) | **다른 디자인 있어요?** (*dareun dijain isseoyo?*) |
| A larger size (*deo keun geo*) | **더 큰 거 있어요?** (*deo keun geo isseoyo?*) |

## Can you buy items individually, not in a pack?

Many Korean stationery shops sell pens, markers, and tape individually — **낱개** (*natgae*) — even when the display shows a full set. It is worth asking before you commit to a whole pack:

**낱개로 살 수 있어요?** — *natgaero sal su isseoyo?* — "Can I buy just one?"

If the answer is yes, they will ring it up separately or point you to the single-unit section. If only the set is available, you will hear **세트로만 있어요** (*seteuroman isseoyo*) — "We only have the set."

## What do you say at the checkout?

Korean stationery stores often offer basic gift-wrapping for free or a small fee. If you are buying a present, ask before the cashier starts scanning:

**포장해 주세요** — *pojanghae juseyo* — "Please wrap this."

At the register, two more phrases are useful every time:

**영수증 주세요** — *yeongsujeung juseyo* — "Please give me a receipt."

**카드 되요?** — *kadeu dwaeyo?* — "Do you accept card?"

You: **이거 다른 색 있어요?** — *igeo dareun saek isseoyo?* — "Does this come in another color?"  
Them: **파란색이랑 초록색 있어요.** — *parangsaegiran choroksaek isseoyo.* — "We have blue and green."  
You: **파란색으로 주세요.** — *parangsaeguro juseyo.* — "The blue one, please."

## What if you want to browse without being interrupted?

Korean shop staff greet you with **어서 오세요** (*eoseo oseyo*) — "Welcome" — when you walk in. A short **그냥 볼게요** (*geunyang bolgeyo*), "I am just looking," signals politely that you do not need help. Staff will leave you to browse and step in when you call them.
