When Koreans make a K-drama list, they don't sort it by "romance" or "thriller" in English. They sort it by short Korean genre words: **로코** (*roko*) for romantic comedy, **사극** (*sageuk*) for historical dramas, **막장** (*makjang*) for over-the-top family chaos, **복수극** (*boksugeuk*) for revenge stories, and **힐링 드라마** (*hilling deurama*) for the gentle, comforting ones. Learn these five and you can read a Korean streaming menu, ask a Korean friend for a recommendation, and explain what you like in one sentence: **저는 로코를 좋아해요** (*jeoneun roko-reul joahaeyo*) — "I like rom-coms." Below is each genre word, what it really promises, and the lines that go with it.

> - **로코** (*roko*) — romantic comedy, short for 로맨틱 코미디.
> - **사극** (*sageuk*) — historical drama, usually Joseon-era.
> - **막장 드라마** (*makjang deurama*) — "dead-end" drama: birth secrets, slaps, amnesia.
> - **복수극** (*boksugeuk*) — revenge drama.
> - **스릴러** / **범죄물** (*seurilleo* / *beomjoemul*) — thriller / crime show.
> - **판타지** (*pantaji*) — fantasy: goblins, time slips, body swaps.
> - **힐링 드라마** (*hilling deurama*) — a "healing" drama, slow and warm.
> - **청춘물** / **학원물** (*cheongchunmul* / *hagwonmul*) — youth drama / school drama.

## How the genre words are built

Most Korean genre words end in one of two pieces, and once you see them, new ones become easy to read.

**극** (*geuk*) means "play" or "drama." Put a theme in front and you have a genre: **복수극** (revenge drama), **사극** (historical drama — 사 from 역사, history), **법정극** (*beopjeonggeuk*, courtroom drama), **가족극** (*gajokgeuk*, family drama).

**물** (*mul*) means "thing" or "stuff" in the sense of "this kind of story." **범죄물** is crime stuff, **청춘물** is youth stuff, **의학물** (*uihangmul*) is medical stuff, **좀비물** (*jombimul*) is zombie stuff. Fans add 물 to almost anything: a show built around a contract marriage is a **계약결혼물** (*gyeyakgyeolhonmul*).

The third pattern is the borrowed English word, cut short. Korean loves clipping long loanwords, so 로맨틱 코미디 becomes **로코**, and a romance with fantasy elements becomes **로판** (*ropan*, 로맨스 판타지). If you already know Konglish, you can often guess these.

## The genres that need a little explaining

Some genre words can't be translated one-to-one, because the genre itself is very Korean.

**막장** started as a mining word: the dead-end of a tunnel, the place you cannot go further. A 막장 drama is one where the plot has gone as far as it can go — secret children, a mother-in-law throwing water, a character returning from the dead with a new face. Koreans say it half as criticism, half with love. **완전 막장인데 계속 보게 돼요** (*wanjeon makjang-inde gyesok boge dwaeyo*) — "It's total makjang, but I keep watching" — is a very honest sentence.

**사극** is more than "period drama." It brings its own speech: kings say **과인** (*gwain*) for "I," servants answer **예, 전하** (*ye, jeonha*, "Yes, Your Majesty"), and everyone ends sentences with **~하오** or **~느냐**. A **퓨전 사극** (*pyujeon sageuk*) mixes that setting with modern dialogue and humor, so it's easier for learners to follow.

**힐링** is the English word "healing," but in Korean it means something closer to "soothing." A 힐링 드라마 has no villain to beat; people in a small town eat well and slowly get better. When someone says **힐링됐어요** (*hilling dwaesseoyo*) after an episode, they mean "That made me feel better."

## Asking for a recommendation

The core line is **드라마 추천해 주세요** (*deurama chucheonhae juseyo*) — "Please recommend me a drama." Add the genre in front to narrow it down:

- **로코 추천해 주세요.** — Recommend me a rom-com.
- **재밌는 사극 있어요?** (*jaemineun sageuk isseoyo?*) — Is there a good historical drama?
- **너무 슬프지 않은 거요.** (*neomu seulpeuji aneun geoyo*) — Something not too sad.
- **짧은 거 있어요? 열두 부작 정도.** (*jjalbeun geo isseoyo? yeoldu bujak jeongdo*) — Anything short? About 12 episodes.

**부작** (*bujak*) is the counter for a drama's total episodes. A 16부작 drama has sixteen episodes in all, which is the classic K-drama length. With friends, drop the 요: **로코 추천해 줘** (*roko chucheonhae jwo*).

## Saying what you like (and what you don't)

Liking a genre is **좋아해요**; the more fan-sounding word is **취향** (*chwihyang*), "taste."

- **사극이 제 취향이에요.** (*sageugi je chwihyang-ieyo*) — Historical dramas are my taste.
- **복수극은 제 취향 아니에요.** (*boksugeugeun je chwihyang anieyo*) — Revenge dramas aren't my thing.
- **저는 로코만 봐요.** (*jeoneun roko-man bwayo*) — I only watch rom-coms.
- **스릴러는 무서워서 못 봐요.** (*seurilleo-neun museowoseo mot bwayo*) — I can't watch thrillers, they're too scary.

"Not my taste" is the polite way to turn down a recommendation. It says nothing bad about the show, only about you, which keeps the conversation friendly.

## A conversation about building a K-drama list

**Mina:** 요즘 뭐 봐요? (*yojeum mwo bwayo?*) — What are you watching these days?
**Alex:** 아직 없어요. 드라마 추천해 주세요. — Nothing yet. Recommend me a drama.
**Mina:** 어떤 장르 좋아해요? 로코? 스릴러? (*eotteon jangneu joahaeyo?*) — What genre do you like? Rom-com? Thriller?
**Alex:** 로코요. 근데 너무 막장은 싫어요. — Rom-com. But nothing too makjang.
**Mina:** 그럼 힐링 로코 하나 있어요. 16부작이에요. — Then there's a healing rom-com. Sixteen episodes.
**Alex:** 좋아요! 이번 주말에 정주행할게요. — Great! I'll binge it this weekend.

**장르** (*jangneu*) is simply "genre," and **정주행** (*jeongjuhaeng*) means watching a series straight through from episode one.

## Four mistakes learners make

**Saying 로맨스 when you mean 로코.** 로맨스 is a romance in general and can be heavy and tearful. If you want the light, funny kind, ask for 로코 specifically.

**Using 막장 as a compliment to the actors.** 막장 describes the script, not the acting. Saying a drama is 막장 in front of someone who loves it can sound like a dig, so add the softener **재밌는 막장** (*jaemineun makjang*, "fun makjang").

**Copying 사극 speech in real life.** 그대, ~하오, and 전하 are lovely to hear and strange to say at a café. Use them for jokes with friends who watch the same show.

**Forgetting 부작.** Saying 16개 for sixteen episodes sounds like you're counting apples. Total length is 16부작; a single episode is **1화** (*il-hwa*) or **1회**.

## From reading the list to saying it

Pick your three favorite shows and describe each in one sentence: genre + 취향. **이건 사극인데 퓨전이라 재밌어요.** **이건 힐링 드라마예요.** Say them out loud until they come without a pause.
