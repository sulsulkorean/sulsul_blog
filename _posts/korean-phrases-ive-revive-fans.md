Starship Entertainment's six-member group IVE (아이브) dropped their second full album **REVIVE+** and title track "BLACKHOLE" on August 23, 2026. Within minutes, Korean fan communities — Weverse threads, group chats, fan cafes — filled with a specific set of phrases that signal you belong in the conversation. These are the expressions you need to read and reply in real time.

> - It is out: **나왔다!** (*nawatda!*), "It's out!"
> - Title track reaction: **타이틀곡 대박이다** (*taiteulgok daebagida*), "The title track is insane."
> - Which song do you like: **어떤 곡 제일 좋아?** (*eotteon gok jeil joa?*), "Which song do you like best?"
> - Cannot stop listening: **계속 듣고 있어** (*gyesok deutgo isseo*), "I keep listening to it."
> - Let's stream: **스트리밍 하자!** (*seuteuriiming haja!*), "Let's stream it!"

## What do Korean fans say the moment a new album drops?

The first message after a release is always short and loud. Three lines cover it:

**나왔다!** — *nawatda!* — "It's out!"

**타이틀곡 대박이다.** — *taiteulgok daebagida.* — "The title track is insane."

**지금 듣고 있어?** — *jigeum deutgo isseo?* — "Are you listening right now?"

You: **나왔다! 타이틀곡 들었어?** — *nawatda! taiteulgok deureosseo?* — "It's out! Have you heard the title track?"  
Them: **응, 방금 들었어. 대박이야!** — *eung, banggeum deureosseo. daebagiya!* — "Yeah, just heard it. It's incredible!"  
You: **어떤 파트 제일 좋아?** — *eotteon pateu jeil joa?* — "Which part do you like best?"

## How do you talk about your favorite song or member part?

When a new album lands, Korean fans share which track they are stuck on and which member's part hit hardest. The phrase structure is simple:

**[song name] 진짜 좋다** (*jinjja jota*) — "[song name] is really good."

For a specific member's moment: **[name] 파트 최고야** (*pateu choegoya*) — "[Name]'s part is the best." If a song is on constant repeat, **이 노래 못 끊어** (*i norae mot kkeunheo*) — "I can't cut off this song" — is how Korean fans say they are stuck on a track.

IVE fans clip phrases like **원영 파트 구간** (*wonyeong pateu gugan*), "Wonyoung's part section," when sharing MV timestamps in fan threads.

## What do Korean fans say when streaming an album?

Streaming coordination uses a handful of fixed phrases:

**스트리밍 하자** (*seuteuriiming haja*) — "Let's stream it." A call to action, usually followed by a platform name.

**같이 듣자** (*gachi deutja*) — "Let's listen together." Used for synced listening parties.

**차트 올리자** (*chateu ollija*) — "Let's push it up the chart."

When fans celebrate a chart result, they post **1위 했다!** (*il-wi haetda!*), "[Group] took number one!" or ask **몇 위야?** (*myeot wiya?*), "What place is it at?"
