Korean phrases at a **사진관** (*sajingwan*, photo studio) lean on Pattern 23 from the SULSUL workbook — verb stem plus **아/어 주세요** (*a/eo juseyo*), "please do this for me." Walk in with your passport or visa form and say **증명사진 찍어 주세요** (*jeungmyeongsajin jjigeo juseyo*), "Please take an ID photo." Pattern 22 — **천천히 말해 주세요** (*cheoncheonhi malhae juseyo*), "Please speak slowly" — saves you when the clerk lists size options in one breath.

> - ID photo request: **증명사진 찍어 주세요** (*jeungmyeongsajin jjigeo juseyo*), "Please take an ID photo."
> - Passport size: **여권 사진이에요** (*yeogwon sajin-ieyo*), "It is for a passport."
> - Ask the price: **얼마예요?** (*eolmayeyo?*), "How much is it?"
> - Slow down: **천천히 말해 주세요** (*cheoncheonhi malhae juseyo*), "Please speak slowly."
> - Retake: **다시 찍어 주세요** (*dasi jjigeo juseyo*), "Please take it again."

## How do you order photos at a Korean studio?

**증명사진** (*jeungmyeongsajin*, ID photo) booths and full-service **사진관** cluster near subway exits, university gates, and immigration offices. Many studios know visa specs by heart — lead with Pattern 23 and name the document.

You: **안녕하세요. 증명사진 찍어 주세요.** — *annyeonghaseyo. jeungmyeongsajin jjigeo juseyo.* — "Hello. Please take an ID photo."  
Them: **여권용이에요, 비자용이에요?** — *yeogwon-yong-ieyo, bijayong-ieyo?* — "For a passport or a visa?"  
You: **비자용이에요. 얼마예요?** — *bijayong-ieyo. eolmayeyo?* — "For a visa. How much is it?"

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Take an ID photo | 증명사진 찍어 주세요 (*jeungmyeongsajin jjigeo juseyo*) |
| For a passport | 여권 사진이에요 (*yeogwon sajin-ieyo*) |
| Ask the price | 얼마예요? (*eolmayeyo?*) |
| Speak slowly | 천천히 말해 주세요 (*cheoncheonhi malhae juseyo*) |
| Retake the shot | 다시 찍어 주세요 (*dasi jjigeo juseyo*) |

## What do you say when they adjust lighting or background?

Studios often ask you to sit straight, remove glasses, or pick a background color. If the instructions come too fast, Pattern 22 is your reset. Pattern 01 — noun plus **주세요** (*juseyo*), "please give me" — works when you need extra prints.

You: **배경 흰색으로 해 주세요.** — *baegyeong huinsaek-euro hae juseyo.* — "Please use a white background."  
Them: **고개 조금만 들어 주세요.** — *gogae jogeumman deureo juseyo.* — "Please lift your chin a little."  
You: **네. 사진 두 장 주세요.** — *ne. sajin du jang juseyo.* — "Yes. Two photos, please."

The **얼마예요?** line from [exchanging currency at a bank in Korea](/exchange-currency-bank-korea) works the same at the photo counter when you want the total before you pay.

## What usually goes wrong at a Korean photo studio?

Travelers often say **사진** (*sajin*, photo) without **증명사진** (*jeungmyeongsajin*, ID photo) and get a portrait session priced for weddings, not visas — name the document every time. A second mistake is accepting the first shot when your hair covers an eyebrow; **다시 찍어 주세요** (*dasi jjigeo juseyo*), "Please take it again," is normal and free at most studios.

You: **이 사진 비자에 써도 돼요?** — *i sajin bija-e sseodo dwaeyo?* — "Can I use this photo for the visa?"  
Them: **네, 규격 맞아요.** — *ne, gyugyeok majayo.* — "Yes, it meets the size rules."  
You: **감사합니다. 파일로도 보내 주세요.** — *gamsahamnida. pail-rodo bonae juseyo.* — "Thank you. Please send a file copy too."
