**K-drama location tours** in Seoul surged in summer 2026 as fans booked weekend buses to the Han River bridge and cafe corners from hit series — and the Korean you need at the stop is simpler than the plot you binge-watched. **촬영지 어디예요?** (*chwaryeongji eodiyeyo?*), "Where is the filming location?" is what you ask the guide at the first landmark, and **사진 찍어도 돼요?** (*sajin jjigeodo dwaeyo?*), "May I take a photo?" keeps you polite when a crowd forms around the same bench.

> - Ask where to go: **촬영지 어디예요?** (*chwaryeongji eodiyeyo?*), "Where is the filming location?"
> - Request a photo: **사진 찍어도 돼요?** (*sajin jjigeodo dwaeyo?*), "May I take a photo?"
> - Point out a scene spot: **여기예요** (*yeogiyeyo*), "It is here."
> - React to the view: **너무 예뻐요** (*neomu yeppeoyo*), "It is so pretty."
> - Thank the guide: **감사합니다** (*gamsahamnida*), "Thank you."

## How do you talk on a location tour without spoiling the drama?

Tour groups mix English drama titles with Korean directions — a few Korean lines signal you are following along, not just taking photos silently.

1. At the bus stop, ask **촬영지 어디예요?** so the guide knows you want the exact spot.
2. Before raising your phone, say **사진 찍어도 돼요?** — staff appreciate the ask.
3. When you recognize the set, point and say **여기예요** to confirm with the group.
4. React to the skyline with **너무 예뻐요** instead of a long English caption.
5. At the end of the stop, tell the guide **감사합니다** before boarding again.

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Find the spot | 촬영지 어디예요? (*chwaryeongji eodiyeyo?*) |
| Ask about photos | 사진 찍어도 돼요? (*sajin jjigeodo dwaeyo?*) |
| Confirm the location | 여기예요 (*yeogiyeyo*) |
| Admire the view | 너무 예뻐요 (*neomu yeppeoyo*) |
| Thank the guide | 감사합니다 (*gamsahamnida*) |

## What does a Korean guide or fellow fan say back?

Replies on location tours stay practical because everyone is juggling a map and a camera.

You: **촬영지 어디예요?** — *chwaryeongji eodiyeyo?* — "Where is the filming location?"  
Them: **저기요, 다리 앞이에요.** — *jeogiyo, dari apieyo.* — "Over there, in front of the bridge."  
You: **사진 찍어도 돼요?** — *sajin jjigeodo dwaeyo?* — "May I take a photo?"

If someone moves out of your shot, **실례합니다** (*sillyehamnida*), "Excuse me," works better than a long apology in English.

## What usually goes wrong on a K-drama location tour?

Learners memorize dramatic confession lines from subtitles but never practice the simple location question the tour guide hears fifty times a day.

A second mistake is assuming every fellow fan speaks English — **여기예요** and **너무 예뻐요** are enough to join a quick chat at the railing. For binge-watch lines after you get home, see [Korean phrases when a new K-drama episode drops](/korean-phrases-new-kdrama-episode-fans).
