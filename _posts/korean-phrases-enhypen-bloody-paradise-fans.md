Soompi reported on August 28, 2026 that ENHYPEN claimed their third consecutive music show trophy for "Bloody Paradise" on KBS2 *Music Bank*, beating KiiiKiii with 12,817 points during promotions for mini album THE SIN : BLISS. The moment the winner announcement dropped, fan group chats lit up with **3연승이에요!** (*sam yeon seung-ieyo!*, "Three wins in a row!") — and the conversation did not stop there. These are the Korean phrases every **엔진** (*Enjin*, ENGENE) fan actually used.

> - Three wins: **3연승이에요!** (*sam yeon seung-ieyo!*), "Three wins in a row!"
> - Won on Music Bank: **뮤직뱅크에서 1위 했어요** (*myujik baengkeu-eseo il-wi haesseoyo*), "They won #1 on Music Bank."
> - Watch the encore: **앙코르 봤어요?** (*angkoreu bwasseoyo?*), "Did you watch the encore?"
> - Still streaming: **아직도 스밍 중이에요** (*ajikdo seuming jung-ieyo*), "I'm still streaming."
> - Want to see live: **직관 가고 싶어요** (*jikgwan gago sipeoyo*), "I want to go in person."

## What do fans say the moment the winner is called?

The winner announcement on *Music Bank* comes with a score reveal — 12,817 against KiiiKiii — and every fan's reaction follows the same pattern. You see the trophy, you react, you text.

You: **어제 뮤직뱅크에서 1위 했어요! 3연승이에요!** — *eoje myujik baengkeu-eseo il-wi haesseoyo! sam yeon seung-ieyo!* — "They won #1 on Music Bank yesterday! Three wins in a row!"  
Them: **진짜요? 앙코르 봤어요?** — *jinjayo? angkoreu bwasseoyo?* — "Really? Did you watch the encore?"  
You: **네, 완전 대박이었어요!** — *ne, wanjeon daebak-ieosseoyo!* — "Yes, it was totally amazing!"

## What do you text your fan friends right after a win like this?

The chat between wins is where the real Korean vocabulary lives. You need to go beyond "대박" (*daebak*, amazing) to keep up with native-speaking fans.

**아직도 스밍 중이에요** (*ajikdo seuming jung-ieyo*), "I'm still streaming," is how you show you are pulling chart weight. **직관 가고 싶어요** (*jikgwan gago sipeoyo*), "I want to go in person," tells everyone your next Korea trip priority. And when a fan mate in Seoul texts back about concert tickets, reach for **같이 가요!** (*gachi gayo!*), "Let's go together!"

## What do K-pop fans say about voting and keeping the win streak alive?

A three-peat like this takes coordinated fan work — voting, streaming, and buying physical albums. Three phrases that fit:

**스밍 열심히 하고 있어요** (*seuming yeolsimhi hago isseoyo*), "I'm streaming hard," is the standard check-in. When someone asks about the comeback schedule, **컴백 기대돼요!** (*keombaek gidaedwaeyo!*), "I'm so excited for the comeback!" keeps the hype going. And when the trophy comes: **팬 덕분에 받은 거예요** (*paen deokbune badeun geoyeyo*), "It's because of the fans that they won it."
