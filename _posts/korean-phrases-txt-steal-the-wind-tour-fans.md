BigHit Music confirmed on August 28, 2026 that Tomorrow X Together will open their **"Steal the Wind"** world tour with three nights in Seoul from November 13 to 15, then run through Asia, North America, and Europe into June next year. The announcement hit MOA group chats before most fans finished reading the press release — and the Korean lines that came next are worth practising now.

> - React to the announcement: **투어 발표났어요** (*tueo balpyo nasseoyo*), "The tour was announced."
> - Ask about tickets: **티켓 예매했어요?** (*tiket yemaehaesseoyo?*), "Did you buy tickets?"
> - Rally your crew: **같이 가요** (*gati gayo*), "Let's go together."
> - Ask when sales open: **언제 예매해요?** (*eonje yemaehaeyo?*), "When do tickets go on sale?"
> - Celebrate good news: **대박이에요** (*daebakieyo*), "That is amazing."

## How do you talk about a world tour in Korean fan chats?

**투어 발표났어요** (*tueo balpyo nasseoyo*) is the standard announcement line: "The tour was announced." It follows the Pattern-08 structure — **났어요** signals that something just happened. Add a city before it for regional chats: **서울 콘서트 발표났어요** (*Seoul konseuteu balpyo nasseoyo*), "The Seoul concert was announced."

You: **TXT 투어 발표났어요!** — *TXT tueo balpyo nasseoyo!* — "The TXT tour was announced!"  
Them: **진짜요? 언제 예매해요?** — *jinjjayo? eonje yemaehaeyo?* — "Really? When do tickets go on sale?"  
You: **아직 몰라요. 같이 가요!** — *ajik mollayo. gati gayo!* — "Not yet. Let's go together!"

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Tour announced | 투어 발표났어요 (*tueo balpyo nasseoyo*) |
| Ask ticket status | 티켓 예매했어요? (*tiket yemaehaesseoyo?*) |
| Rally the group | 같이 가요 (*gati gayo*) |
| Presale timing | 언제 예매해요? (*eonje yemaehaeyo?*) |

## What do you say when you get your ticket?

Once you lock in a seat, the group chat reply is **티켓 잡았어요** (*tiket jabaseoyo*), "I got the ticket" — **잡다** (*japda*), "to grab," is the verb fans use when they land a hard-to-find spot. If you are still in the queue, say **대기 중이에요** (*daegi jungieyo*), "I am waiting," and keep refreshing.

If seats are gone before you get through, look for the waiting list option on the ticketing page — in Korean fan culture, **취소표** (*chwisopyo*), "cancellation ticket," is a real strategy that MOA talk about regularly in the weeks after presale.

## How do you plan the Seoul meetup in Korean?

Fan groups heading to the November Seoul shows are already coordinating in chats right now. **어디서 만나요?** (*eodiseo mannayo?*), "Where are we meeting?" is the simplest way to organise. Most groups agree on a venue exit: **몇 번 출구에서 만나요?** (*myeot beon chulgueseo mannayo?*), "Which exit do we meet at?" covers the logistics.

November in Seoul turns cold fast — add **따뜻하게 입어요** (*ttatteutage ibeoyo*), "Dress warmly," to your meetup message and you sound like a local.
