When Forbes (August 24, 2026) reported that BTS had declined to submit 'ARIRANG' for the Grammy's new "Best Asian Pop Music Performance" category — despite the album sitting at the top of global charts — fans on Weverse and fan communities reached for two phrases first: **대박났어요!** (*daebaknaesseoyo!*), "It blew up!" and **진짜 자랑스러워요** (*jinjja jarangseurowoyo*), "I'm really proud of them." Those two cover every K-pop milestone moment, not just this one.

> - First reaction: **대박났어요!** (*daebaknaesseoyo!*), "It blew up!"
> - Fan pride: **진짜 자랑스러워요** (*jinjja jarangseurowoyo*), "I'm really proud of them."
> - Chart talk: **1위 했어요?** (*il-wi haesseoyo?*), "Did they hit number one?"
> - Award chat: **수상할 것 같아요?** (*susanghal geot gatayo?*), "Do you think they'll win an award?"
> - Concert dream: **공연 보고 싶어요** (*gongyeon bogo sipeoyo*), "I want to see them live."

## How do you react when a big K-pop moment drops?

Fan reactions happen fast — Weverse threads fill within minutes of a news article. These three stack naturally and work for any chart achievement or artist milestone, not just Grammy news.

You: **'아리랑' 뉴스 봤어요?** — *'Arirang' nyuseu bwasseoyo?* — "Did you see the ARIRANG news?"  
Them: **네! 대박났어요! 진짜 자랑스러워요.** — *ne! daebaknaesseoyo! jinjja jarangseurowoyo.* — "Yes! It blew up! I'm really proud of them."  
You: **맞아요. 우리 가수가 최고예요.** — *majayo. uri gasuga choegoeyeo.* — "Right. Our singer is the best."

## How do you discuss the Grammy story with Korean fans?

BTS's decision sparked a bigger conversation about how global artists are classified. In Korean fan spaces, that turns into opinions fast — and both sides of the debate use the same short phrases.

You: **그래미 상이 좋은 것 같아요?** — *geuremi sangi joeun geot gatayo?* — "Do you think the Grammy Award is a good thing?"  
Them: **글쎄요. 우리 가수는 이미 최고예요.** — *geulsseoyo. uri gaseuneun imi choegoeyeo.* — "I'm not sure. Our singer is already the best."  
You: **맞아요! 1위 했잖아요.** — *majayo! il-wi haetjanayo.* — "Right! They already hit number one."

For counting chart positions out loud — **1위** (*il-wi*, first place), **2위** (*i-wi*, second place) — the same number system from [Korean numbers when paying and counting](/korean-numbers-paying-counting-korea) works for music charts, too.

## What do you say when a friend has not heard the album yet?

Not every Korean fan follows BTS closely. One easy opener turns a blank look into a shared listen, no long explanation needed.

You: **'아리랑' 들어봤어요?** — *'Arirang' deureobwasseoyo?* — "Have you listened to 'ARIRANG'?"  
Them: **아직요. 어때요?** — *ajikyo. eottaeyo?* — "Not yet. How is it?"  
You: **진짜 좋아요. 한번 들어보세요.** — *jinjja joayo. hanbeon deureoboseyo.* — "It's really good. Give it a listen."
