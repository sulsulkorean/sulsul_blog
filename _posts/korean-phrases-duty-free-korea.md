Korean phrases at a duty-free shop in Korea lean on Pattern 03 from the SULSUL workbook — **있어요?** (*isseoyo?*), "Do you have it?" — and Pattern 23 — **아/어 드릴게요** (*a/eo deurilgeyo*), "I will do this for you." Walk up to a 면세점 (*myeonsejeom*, duty-free shop) counter and say **이거 있어요?** (*igeo isseoyo?*) while pointing at the product, then offer your passport when the clerk asks. Pattern 22 — **어디예요?** (*eodiyeyo?*), "Where is it?" — covers one final step: finding the gate pickup counter before you board.

> - Ask if it's in stock: **이거 있어요?** (*igeo isseoyo?*) — "Do you have this?"
> - Confirm duty-free status: **면세 되어요?** (*myeonse doeeoyo?*) — "Is this duty-free?"
> - Offer your passport: **여권 보여 드릴까요?** (*yeogwon boyeo deurilkkayo?*) — "Shall I show my passport?"
> - Show boarding pass: **탑승권 보여 드릴게요** (*tapseunggwon boyeo deurilgeyo*) — "I will show my boarding pass."
> - Find pickup counter: **픽업 카운터가 어디예요?** (*pikeop kaunteoga eodiyeyo?*) — "Where is the pickup counter?"

## How do you shop at a Korean duty-free shop?

**면세점** (*myeonsejeom*, duty-free shop) counters appear at Incheon and Gimpo airports, and in big city mall basements like Lotte and Shinsegae. Staff will always ask to see your passport and boarding pass — have both ready before you point at anything.

You: **안녕하세요. 이 향수 있어요?** — *annyeonghaseyo. i hyangsu isseoyo?* — "Hello. Do you have this perfume?"  
Them: **네, 있어요. 여권 보여 주세요.** — *ne, isseoyo. yeogwon boyeo juseyo.* — "Yes, we have it. Please show your passport."  
You: **네, 여기 있어요. 탑승권도 드릴게요.** — *ne, yeogi isseoyo. tapseungwon-do deurilgeyo.* — "Yes, here it is. I will show my boarding pass too."

| Confirm duty-free | 면세 되어요? (*myeonse doeeoyo?*) |

| Find pickup counter | 픽업 카운터가 어디예요? (*pikeop kaunteoga eodiyeyo?*) |

## What do you say when you collect your order at the gate?

City duty-free shops — Lotte and Shinsegae have large ones — do not hand goods to you at the counter. Items are held at the airport and collected after passport control at the **인도장** (*indojang*, delivery counter).

You: **픽업 카운터가 어디예요?** — *pikeop kaunteoga eodiyeyo?* — "Where is the pickup counter?"  
Them: **탑승 게이트 지나서 면세 인도장이에요.** — *tapseung geiteu jinaseo myeonse indojang-ieyo.* — "It is the duty-free delivery counter past the boarding gates."  
You: **감사합니다. 몇 번 게이트예요?** — *gamsahamnida. myeot beon geiteu-yeyo?* — "Thank you. Which gate number?"

The **어디예요?** pattern from [finding the checkout counter in Korea](/korean-phrases-finding-checkout-counter) works just as well when tracking down the right terminal or desk.

## What usually goes wrong at a Korean duty-free shop?

Travelers often forget that airport duty-free goods are collected at pickup, not at the counter. Miss the **인도장** (*indojang*, delivery counter) window and you board without your purchase.

A second mistake is skipping **면세 되어요?** (*myeonse doeeoyo?*) at in-town mall counters. Not every product on the shelf qualifies — some cosmetics or electronics fall outside the duty-free scheme. Ask before the clerk rings it up.
