Korean phrases at a copy center in Korea lean on Pattern 23 from the SULSUL workbook — verb stem plus **아/어 주세요** (*a/eo juseyo*), "please do this for me." Hand over your papers and say **흑백으로 복사해 주세요** (*heukbaek-euro boksahae juseyo*), "Please copy in black and white," or **컬러로 복사해 주세요** (*keolleo-ro boksahae juseyo*), "Please copy in color." Pattern 22 — **천천히 말해 주세요** (*cheoncheonhi malhae juseyo*), "Please speak slowly" — saves you when the clerk lists options fast.

> - Black-and-white copy: **흑백으로 복사해 주세요** (*heukbaek-euro boksahae juseyo*), "Please copy in black and white."
> - Color copy: **컬러로 복사해 주세요** (*keolleo-ro boksahae juseyo*), "Please copy in color."
> - Ask the price: **얼마예요?** (*eolmayeyo?*), "How much is it?"
> - Slow down: **천천히 말해 주세요** (*cheoncheonhi malhae juseyo*), "Please speak slowly."
> - Scan to email: **스캔해서 보내 주세요** (*sekaenhaeseo bonae juseyo*), "Please scan and send it."

## How do you order copies at a Korean print shop?

**복사점** (*boksajeom*, copy shop) counters sit inside university areas, subway stations, and office districts. Many also **인쇄** (*inswae*, print) PDFs and **스캔** (*sekaen*, scan) passports for visa forms. Start with Pattern 23 and name the format you need.

You: **안녕하세요. 이거 흑백으로 복사해 주세요.** — *annyeonghaseyo. igeo heukbaek-euro boksahae juseyo.* — "Hello. Please copy this in black and white."  
Them: **몇 장이요?** — *myeot jang-iyo?* — "How many sheets?"  
You: **세 장이요. 얼마예요?** — *se jang-iyo. eolmayeyo?* — "Three sheets. How much is it?"

| Situation | Say this |
|---|---|
| Black-and-white copy | 흑백으로 복사해 주세요 (*heukbaek-euro boksahae juseyo*) |
| Color copy | 컬러로 복사해 주세요 (*keolleo-ro boksahae juseyo*) |
| Ask the total | 얼마예요? (*eolmayeyo?*) |
| Speak slowly | 천천히 말해 주세요 (*cheoncheonhi malhae juseyo*) |
| Scan and send | 스캔해서 보내 주세요 (*sekaenhaeseo bonae juseyo*) |

## What do you say when you need printing or scanning?

For visa paperwork or a rental contract, clerks often ask about paper size and binding. Listen for **A4** and **양면** (*yangmyeon*, double-sided). If the question comes too fast, Pattern 22 is your reset button.

You: **컬러로 인쇄해 주세요.** — *keolleo-ro inswaehae juseyo.* — "Please print in color."  
Them: **양면으로 할까요?** — *yangmyeon-euro halkkayo?* — "Double-sided?"  
You: **네, 양면으로 해 주세요.** — *ne, yangmyeon-euro hae juseyo.* — "Yes, double-sided, please."

The **얼마예요?** line from [exchanging currency at a bank in Korea](/exchange-currency-bank-korea) works the same way at the copy counter when you want the total before you pay.

## What usually goes wrong at a Korean copy shop?

Travelers often say **복사** (*boksah*, copy) but forget to name **흑백** (*heukbaek*, black and white) or **컬러** (*keolleo*, color) — the clerk defaults to color and the bill jumps. Add the format every time: **흑백으로 복사해 주세요.**

A second mistake is handing over a passport page without saying how many copies you need. Count first and answer **두 장이요** (*du jang-iyo*), "Two sheets," when they ask **몇 장이요?** so you are not charged for extras.
